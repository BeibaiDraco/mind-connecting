bytes. They retain the original start, charge all prior obligations, reject gaps
or overlaps in the accounting intervals, require exact pinned evidence and a
distinct review for stopped-time credit, and bound the deadline by dollars,

accruing disk charges, and absence of retained local restart intents support a
corroborated operational inference. They are not a provider-certified complete

seconds for uncertainty and uses a disk-rate upper bound above the observed rate.

reserves. Contradictory later evidence requires reconciliation before continuation.

restores now require a fresh balance covering the remaining phase obligations.
Restoration remains single-attempt, exact-instance bound, and protected by a local
budget guard plus an independent short commissioning guard before the paid call.
Remote guard handoff requires fresh matching process evidence.

changed limits, unsupported stopped credit, duplicate intervals, omitted charges,
