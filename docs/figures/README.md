### Draft English caption

**Figure 1 | From brain interfacing to a testable question about self–other attribution.**
**(a)** Conceptual illustration of a brain connected to an external machine through a neural interface.
**(b)** A hypothetical bridge between two brains motivates open questions about consciousness and the boundaries of self.
**(c)** Two instances of the same language model are coupled through an internal-state bridge. The experiment asks whether each instance distinguishes its own private information from its partner's information, and how source attribution depends on the coupling. The bidirectional arrows illustrate the TWO condition; other experimental conditions are omitted for clarity. The model experiment concerns functional source attribution and does not establish phenomenal consciousness or identity fusion.
