# Story reset: from shared content to maintained self/other organization

2026-10-20. Prospective research narrative requested by the PI. This is not a replacement for the collected-results manuscript or a claim that the proposed new study has succeeded.

Design companion: [research-reset draft](../protocol/SELF_BOUNDARY_RESEARCH_RESET_DRAFT.md). Completed evidence remains governed by its original protocols and [post-collection assessment](post_collection_story_20261012.md).

## Central question

**When another responsive system enters an agent's internal decision process, how does the agent preserve or revise the relationship between what it knows, what guides its actions, and what it attributes to itself or the other?**

The original brain-bridging vision motivates a question about the boundaries of a cognitive individual. A computational experiment can separately manipulate access, goals, actual causal influence and attribution. The interesting outcome could be selective integration with preserved distinctions, a measurable dissociation, or a bounded failure explained by general record interference.

## Why the old organizing story is insufficient

The original Qwen manuscript presented false self-attribution as the organizing phenomenon. Subsequent third-party conflict results show substantial wrong-source answers for Robin as well. High accuracy on the old nonconflicting Robin question did not establish self specificity. The two-model source-attribution account remains an empirical contribution, but cannot by itself carry a claim about real-time reciprocal self-organization.

The later live-interaction program spent substantial effort on task and interface admission. Those prerequisites matter, but faster communication or correct button choice is not the self-related endpoint. The recent accurate-goal/wrong-rule pattern is a lead: it has not measured what the agent knows about the causes of its own behavior.

The research reset therefore changes the organizing question and evidence sequence. It preserves the contrary data instead of promoting a new interpretation of the same errors.

## Proposed narrative, with evidence labels

1. **Motivation:** sharing internal content raises a question about how separate histories, commitments and actions remain organized as self and other.
2. **Existing observation:** the tested memory interface can support access while corrupting original-source answers in two checkpoints.
3. **Existing explanatory constraint:** conflicting third-party records also fail. A self-related explanation must therefore compete with ordinary record/role interference.
4. **New research question:** can an acting agent selectively use partner information while preserving its own commitment, and can it accurately predict which interventions change its behavior?
5. **Proposed test:** maintain two private histories, measure actual decisions, independently manipulate facts and goals, and separate reports from behavior. Begin with a small interpretable task rather than a large protocol.
6. **Proposed relational test:** ask whether a current partner response changes this organization beyond fresh open-loop content and matched replay.
7. **Result-dependent conclusion:** report whichever pattern survives the controls. Do not write the conclusion before collecting it.

Items 1-3 can be supported or framed now. Items 4-6 describe a research program. Item 7 is intentionally unwritten.

A provisional question-title for the new program is **Knowing Yours, Choosing Mine? Self-Other Organization in Interacting Language Models**. It is a working title, not a demonstrated result or novelty claim.

## Changes in manuscript priorities

- Keep the preserved preprint and historical `paper/STORY.md` intact. Its broad interpretation is not the current statement of the completed evidence.
- Any next version reporting completed data must include the third-party conflict, the separate Llama continuation status and capability exceptions, the unmatched Llama STATIC comparison, and endpoint-specific reversibility.
- Use task illustrations to show separate private histories, actual actions, source ground truth and the timing of interventions. Maintain the restrained paper style requested by the PI.
- Open generated reports remain output observations; they cannot establish awareness of influence. Actual action and prospective, tested prediction are distinct endpoints.
- Do not retitle generic instruction-following errors as self effects. A neutral binding comparison and matched baseline comprehension are part of the explanatory burden.
- Do not write a results-style abstract for the new live study until the relevant measurements exist.

## Scientific motivation and limits

Gallagher's distinction among ownership, agency and narrative continuity ([2000](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10637618/)) supports keeping different questions separate. The control-versus-attribution distinction also has a human experimental precedent in [Wegner and Wheatley (1999)](https://dtg.sites.fas.harvard.edu/DANWEGNER/pub/Wegner%26Wheatley1999.pdf). Neither source validates model self-reports as subjective experience.

The research ambition is to explain a functional boundary through interventions. No proposed metric is a count of subjects, and successful coordination alone is not a shared experiential perspective.

No model execution, new analysis of uncollected outcomes, numerical adoption or manuscript TeX edit occurred in preparing this draft.
