## Field account: distinctions and current precedents

Historical source, present access, actual causal contribution, reported authorship/control and identification with a task commitment are different scientific objects. Human ownership and agency experiments demonstrate why these distinctions can matter, but they are not evidence of corresponding subjective mechanisms in an LLM. Internal coupling may make dimensions independently manipulable; it does not itself establish an interesting self phenomenon.

[Trimper, Wolpe & Rommelfanger 2014](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroengineering/articles/10.3389/fneng.2014.00004/full), agency/identity discussion and Table 1: this opinion paper already asks who owns ideas produced through brain-to-brain interfaces and raises control over self/other. It motivates an empirical distinction, not an unprecedented philosophical question or evidence of identity fusion.

[Bolt & Loehr 2017](https://www.ens.psl.eu/sites/default/files/bolt2017.pdf), methods §2 and discussion: 48 participants alternated taps with apparent partners, whose sensors were inactive. Computer-generated tones varied in predictability; more predictable input increased shared-control ratings beyond participants' timing performance. However, §2.4.1 says computer tones followed participant taps and/or the final pacing tone, so a contingent computational process remained. This separates the supposed human source from the process actually producing signals; it does not establish agency under fully open-loop input or show responsiveness is unnecessary.

[Fribourg et al., Virtual Co-Embodiment](https://arxiv.org/html/1911.03166), v2, §IV–VI: 24 people controlled shared-avatar movements through five weighting levels, crossed with free-choice, target-specified and trajectory-specified conditions. Actual control and reported agency were related but predictable shared movement could inflate reported control. Body ownership was measured separately. Generic joint control versus attribution is already studied in humans; its bodily interpretation does not transfer automatically.

[Ackerman & Panickssery](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.02064), §3.1–3.2: source-judgment directions can alter Llama authorship reports. Recognition was much weaker against other models after length normalization than against human text. This is not clean evidence of recognizing the history of one same-weight instance rather than another; neither steering attribution nor a source direction is new by itself.

[Chae et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.18091v3), §3–4: judges rated prewritten constraint selections under blind and true/false self/other labels. Labels affect evaluation of the same target across tested sources; a nonsignificant source interaction does not establish universal independence from provenance. Here self identifies the producing model, not persistent-instance continuity. The blind pooled self-preference result is not robust to all specifications/weak-judge exclusions. Self labels have behavioral effects already; accepting content, authorship and governing an action remain different outcomes.

[Carauleanu et al., Self-Other Overlap](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.16325v1), §3.1 and limitations: LoRA minimizes activation differences between self- and Bob-directed recommendation contexts. Reported deception changes coexist with a largely preserved perspective test. This trains one model, rather than connecting two live computations. Representational similarity and differentiation are not logical opposites; neither similarity nor coordination establishes identity collapse.

[Li et al., Where Is the Self of a Large Language Model?](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.16717v3), §3.1–3.2.7 and introduction/results framing: same bodies in native Assistant/User/System slots produce role geometry; patching and steering affect attribution, and source role affects use of a wrong intermediate calculation. Crucially §3.1.3 says the Assistant text was not actually generated by the model: their operational index is attached to the conversational slot. Role-related causal attribution mechanisms are close precedent, not proof of actual instance authorship or a general self mechanism. Native headers also differ in length/format. Do not adopt the paper's broad self-awareness language as an established fact.

Additional already-inspected constraints: [Mixing Mechanisms](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.06182v2) tests positional/lexical/reflexive binding; [dynamic rebinding](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.08644) uses causal routing/payload interventions. [HijackKV](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19957v2) demonstrates hidden-cache behavioral attacks; [latent-channel audits](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.04893v2) test private-fact transfer and donor influence. [CoBRA](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.00967) uses counterfactual tool-access utility for trained routing, including same-reference-policy rollouts. Generic communication, donor-following, binding, confidence and with/without-assistance contrasts cannot alone carry novelty.

The [Persona Selection Model](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/psm/) is a theoretical interpretation of assistants as learned personas, with acknowledged questions about exhaustiveness. It provides a competing explanatory perspective, not a settled ontology. The primary HTML for the lead “Who Put the I in AI?” (2609.29494) was unavailable; it is not a deciding source here.

## Reviewed transferable reasoning patterns

1. **Unbundle coincident factors.** The experimental value of coupling is the possibility of separating content/source/role/site/control/availability/contingency. Bolt's controlled timing and Fribourg's weighted control illustrate different ways of doing this. A new question must specify which independent changes give rival explanations different predictions.
2. **Predict invariance as well as change.** The project's neutral Robin field and independent assignments can expose generic record effects. Actor/role/content permutations test whether a proposed mechanism is specific or simply follows labels. Chae's true/false labels show why historical origin must be independently known.
3. **Separate a computation from the report about it.** Work/readout crossings, original-source records and restored action branches provide leverage; the question itself can alter the answer. Distinct branches are distinct outputs, not simultaneous beliefs.
4. **Distinguish factual trajectory from counterfactual organization.** Frozen swapping can be two one-way effects. Identical deterministic live/replay inputs give identical recipient results; new private interventions and directional cuts are needed to test a return path. Predictability is not responsiveness.
5. **Preserve rival complete response maps.** Accuracy collapses distinct policies and cannot uniquely identify hidden goal transfer. A stronger mechanistic interpretation needs an appropriate mediator intervention and independent controls.

These patterns are a targeted selection from reviewed project evidence and the new source comparisons, not a claim to have rebuilt a complete portable pattern library.
