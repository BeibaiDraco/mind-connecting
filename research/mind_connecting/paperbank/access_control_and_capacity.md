# Two design transfers, with their limits

2026-10-20; read original-source sections rather than importing a human mechanism.

**Matched information, different mode of access.** Fisher, Goddu & Keil, [Searching for Explanations](https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/releases/xge-0000070.pdf), Experiment 1c, pages 4–5, and discussion, page 9: controls received content/time while comparing search and no-search experiences, followed by self-assessed knowledge. The transferable reasoning move is to hold informational benefit in view while measuring perceived internal capability separately. For our target, compare actual donor-generated text with a usable internal channel; oracle facts are a separate ceiling control. Then obtain conditional forecasts and real paired connected/cut outcomes. Necessary missing condition: both channels must work and confidence must be measurable. A text/KV difference would still be a treatment comparison, not proof that interface type alone caused it. This opens candidate B rather than just rephrasing source recall.

**Behavior changes, then self-prediction is checked against it.** [Looking Inward](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.13787), §3.4, changes model behavior through finetuning and checks whether trained self-prediction tracks that change without corresponding new self-prediction examples. Our proposed extension changes available computation through a connection, not weights, and compares predictions against actual restored-state challenge outcomes. We cannot import their trained capacity or privileged-access conclusion into untrained models. This transfer changes B from asking “do you feel connected?” to testing an observable forecast of dependency.

The exposed target data have snapshots and interventions but no competence-forecast measurement. These mappings generate pilot questions only. If forecasting is uninterpretable, candidate A remains separately testable; if a model retains all needed information after a cut, that is not evidence of false confidence or ongoing dependence.
