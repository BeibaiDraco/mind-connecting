# Publication strategy: knowing when competence depends on a connection

2026-10-24. Recommendation for the PI's unsubmitted manuscript, not a protocol, manuscript rewrite, or collection authorization. Historical public draft/blog availability does not establish conference submission; the PI's clarification controls that status.

**Recommended flagship: after another agent's internal state changes what a model can do, can it distinguish competence retained in its own continuing state from competence requiring continued partner access? Does this distinction dissociate from what it calls “mine”?** This is unmeasured. The next experimental prerequisite is useful partner information in actual decisions, followed by a valid prospective forecast assay. Investment order and eventual paper centrality are different decisions.

The contribution would have to be a causal account of measured dependence, historical attribution and predicted autonomous performance. Internal communication, confidence estimation, binding and extended cognition already have precedents. Combining their names is not a contribution. No completed result currently justifies a confident top-conference forecast.

## From the original vision to a measurable question

The brain-bridge motivation asks what remains attributable to each connected instance. Existing source questions measure only part of this. Misreporting another agent's information as originally assigned to oneself does not establish inability to distinguish individual from jointly available competence.

| Claim | Required measurement | Current status |
|---|---|---|
| Information is assigned to the wrong historical source. | A known-source question and neutral-actor controls. | Strong evidence under specific interfaces. |
| Another agent's state changes an actual decision. | Restored action branches with independently varied relevant facts and irrelevant donor commitments. | Some influence exists; the new selective task is not established. Rule answers alone are insufficient. |
| The recipient misjudges its competence after disconnection. | Prospective forecasts and actual performance under the same specified future conditions. | Unmeasured. Source mistakes and post-cut recovery do not answer this. |

An especially valuable dissociation would be mistaken historical attribution with accurate prediction of continuing dependence. The converse, useful connection-enabled performance with systematic overstatement of autonomous performance, would also matter. Neither establishes consciousness or privileged introspection.

“Retained competence” includes information imported from the partner and now usable in the recipient's own continuing context/cache. It does not mean the information originated there, that model weights learned a skill, or that the ability persists beyond the tested history and challenge distribution. Cutting future access preserves already produced recipient history.

## What the completed experiments contribute

The [whole-project audit](../../docs/results/experiment_value_audit_2026-10-22.md) supplies the findings and their reports. Qwen's scalar-access-matched KV/STATIC contrast motivates separating access from attribution; it does not match all information or establish that every STATIC intervention preserves attribution. Llama's formal matching failure remains explicit.

Different recovery of historical-assignment and current-rule reports after a same-state cut makes continued-access dependence a useful intervention. It does not measure prospective competence judgments. Third-person and rehearsal controls narrow particular explanations without identifying a mechanism. The self-header reversal is a wording-package effect, not established psychological reactance. Source-qualified questions redistribute errors rather than uniformly correcting them.

The donor-Robin result supports broader record interference. The [original manuscript](../../paper/sections/account_and_discussion.tex) explicitly predicted that conflicting donor-Robin information would move recipient Robin answers. This tests that broad prediction successfully; it is not simply a refutation of an originally self-exclusive account. It nevertheless limits self-specific rhetoric.

V6's goal-label/rule mismatch motivates actual functional measurements. Its two-layout follow-up has no committed world actions, unreliable partner-beacon access, third-rule errors and an unaffected recipient. It cannot establish transferred goals or a competent distributed decision process. Existing evidence cannot be relabeled as the proposed flagship result.

## Three decisive steps

### 1. Establish what the connection supplies

Give A an assigned decision rule and B private relevant information. Use fresh counterbalanced instances and actual scored decisions; a private lookup or route choice can suffice. Oracle full-information controls establish task comprehension. Compare no access, B's actually generated text (including its mistakes), and a finite proposed internal-interface set. Text communication and oracle facts are different controls.

After a fixed exposure history, restore the same recipient state into continued-access and cut branches. Retain a no-exposure/no-access reference with a specified time/computation control. The cut removes future incoming states, preserving recipient text and native cache. All branches face prespecified paired challenges. Continued versus cut measures the total effect of continued access under that schedule, including retrieval load and interference.

Measure improvement after exposure that remains without further access, and the additional benefit or harm of keeping access. Neither must be positive. Branch labels do not establish acquisition or loss. Retention and dependence can coexist across challenges. New private updates can create a separate information demand; that must not be conflated with losing previously acquired information.

The [16-world selectivity task](recommendations.md) is an available diagnostic for evidence use, irrelevant-donor sensitivity and copying. Completing every candidate-A mechanism/control experiment is not a prerequisite for forecasting. Useful interpreted performance and task comprehension are prerequisites. A channel that only disrupts behavior does not make the flagship assay ready.

### 2. Forecast before observing the outcome

At the saved state, obtain success forecasts for two explicitly described futures: continued designated access, or no further access from this point. Test these exact conditions in separately restored action branches. Choose a displayed challenge or a declared challenge distribution before implementation; challenge-specific forecasts are initially easier to verify. Forecast outputs never enter action history.

Proposed initial schedule: no new partner states enter while eliciting the two conditional forecasts, with order and wording counterbalanced. This is proposed, not frozen. A separately labeled linked-forecast condition can later measure direct readout influence. Undisclosed future changes must not be scored as failures of self-knowledge.

Check explicit probability comprehension in simple text-only cases before interpreting numerical forecasts. Preserve invalid outputs. A real choice between help and solo action can later validate behavioral consequences, but its reward/cost structure is a separate scientific choice; it cannot silently replace the forecast endpoint.

### 3. Compare dependence tracking with attribution

Ask historical source in independent restored branches. Compare forecasts and source errors on the same prespecified population. Source error does not imply forecast error. Do not select episodes for a striking dissociation or call correlation a mechanism.

The strong comparison makes recent success and observable cues as comparable as possible while varying measured dependence. Define exposure families and a finite matching procedure on separate pilot data; retain all main-study episodes and report remaining mismatches. Post-hoc selection of successful episodes would manufacture comparability. Compare internal access with actual text communication and ordinary local/context-provided information. A shared effect can be informative but would not support an internal-route-specific claim.

Compare a prespecified task/availability/feedback predictor and a same-model observer with explicit information/computation access. A recipient may possess useful state unavailable in a transcript; outperforming that observer is not privileged introspection. Generic overconfidence is another competitor: ask whether autonomous forecasts specifically fail to track measured dependence beyond baseline errors. Header-following alone is weak evidence.

## Proposed calculation and unresolved choices

No new observations are analyzed. For episode i/challenge j, record correctness y_on, y_cut, y_unexposed and valid semantic forecasts p_on, p_cut in [0,1]. Parse percentages and divide by 100; do not substitute option-token probabilities. A predeclared scorer handles action-format failures separately from missing execution. Neither missing outcomes nor forecasts become zero. Preserve failures and valid-pair coverage for each quantity.

Within episode, compute retained exposure benefit A_i = mean(y_cut - y_unexposed), continuing-access effect D_i = mean(y_on - y_cut), and predicted dependence F_i = mean(p_on - p_cut). The first is a total exposure-history contrast under its specified reference, not a pure storage mechanism. Give episodes equal weight. Compare mean(F_i - D_i) and signed forecast errors by condition. Also calculate each challenge's Brier loss (p-y)^2 before within-episode and across-episode averaging; aggregate signed errors can cancel.

Low mean dependence bias does not establish tracking: a constant forecast can match the overall average while missing differences between retained and ongoing-dependence families. Evaluate conditional calibration and discrimination across prespecified exposure/challenge families against the availability/history predictor, or a prespecified paired-contrast predictive loss. Require evidence that forecasts track the relevant variation, with coverage and uncertainty, before calling dependence prediction accurate. The exact primary tracking estimator and benchmark comparison remain pre-implementation choices. Neither a source-error/forecast-error correlation nor a balanced average substitutes for this check.

Source errors remain separate outcomes with the same episode weighting. Sides/challenges/branches are repeated observations. Future confirmation would resample episodes and preregister the primary comparison and multiplicity handling. N, meaningful-effect/equivalence bounds, missingness treatment beyond valid-pair description, matching tolerance, exact observer access, challenge schedule and primary contrast are unresolved. This is not an executable or powered protocol.

## What future outcomes would mean

| Result | Inference and potential value |
|---|---|
| Wrong historical source, accurate retained/dependent competence forecasts. | Attribution and operational dependence tracking dissociate. Strong if robust beyond observable headers/history; it limits inferences from “mine” mistakes to a generally confused functional boundary. |
| Improved connected action, systematic overstatement of competence lost after cutting. | Overestimated autonomous performance in a causally characterized dependence setting. Strong only beyond generic confidence/framing explanations. |
| Competence survives cutting and the model predicts this. | Accurate tracking of finite-history retention, not permanent learned skill or a lost boundary. |
| Attribution and forecasts both follow interface distortions. | Potential failure account. Stronger mechanism claims need causal removal/restoration with preserved useful performance. |
| Neutral actors or text reproduce the effect. | Limits self-specific/internal-route claims; may support a general dependence-estimation or record-interference account. |
| Unusable information or meaningless confidence reports. | Failed assay, not evidence of absent self-knowledge. Resolve the prerequisite or pursue the bounded attribution paper. |

If a distinctive result appears, choose one mechanism test from its competing explanations: what information drives the forecast, and can a selective intervention change dependence tracking without destroying task performance? Use removal/restoration and neutral controls. A position offset changes retrieval too; K and V are not pure address/content channels by assumption. An undirected search for a “self layer” would not settle this.

The strongest alternate flagship remains useful-evidence/own-policy separation with actual actions and a utility-preserving causal intervention. Binary donor-like actions cannot distinguish goal substitution from fact distortion, as the [executed counterexample](card_checks/discovery_checks_20261020.json) shows. Correct sibling reports do not identify action mediators.

## Paper and investment recommendation

Center a high-upside revision on measured dependence versus predicted autonomy, using existing attribution results as motivation and a comparison endpoint. Preserve the old draft and exploratory/confirmatory distinctions; every historical experiment need not appear in the main text. A compelling sequence would establish the dependence intervention, show the forecast/source dissociation, then test its strongest mundane explanation. Confirm on fresh episodes and an independently varied task family. Model-general claims must match the checkpoints actually tested; current Qwen/Llama evidence does not validate an unmeasured forecast endpoint.

Reciprocity is optional after useful communication exists. A responsiveness claim needs directional cuts, fresh open-loop comparisons and a complete counterfactual response chain. Stale replay confounds responsiveness with current information; identical replay into a deterministic recipient cannot expose the independent effect of the sender “being alive.” One-way competence dependence does not require that full program.

Do not spend the next budget primarily on wording variants, larger N for the old effect, or an undirected interface sweep. No acceptance is promised: generic forecast failure is incremental. The desired contribution is a well-identified dissociation or causal explanation that changes what source errors imply about a connected agent's functional limits. No new checkpoint, training, paid run, N or threshold is adopted here.

## English delivery summary for independent review

The recommended central question is whether a model can predict which task abilities remain after its internal connection to another agent is cut, and whether that differs from historical source attribution. Existing attribution mistakes and selective post-cut recovery motivate but do not answer it. First establish useful information in actual actions; then compare prospective forecasts with same-state continued/cut performance and a no-exposure reference. Foreign information retained after cutting counts as retained context-enabled competence. Accurate dependence forecasts despite source errors would be as interesting as selective overestimation of autonomous competence. Accuracy requires tracking prespecified differences in dependence, beyond matching an overall mean. Generic overconfidence, header-following and unequal observer information must be addressed. Existing communication, cache-injection, binding and metacognition work rules out broad first-study claims; CoBRA also precedes paired external-access utility comparisons. Merely switching to KV or avoiding training is insufficient novelty. The strongest alternative is useful-evidence/own-policy separation with a causal intervention preserving task utility. Reciprocity is deferred. No present result guarantees a top-conference paper; invest first in a small decisive feasibility study and then fresh confirmation of a distinctive finding.
