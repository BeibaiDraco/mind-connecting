# Candidate: functional self/other organization under internal coupling

2026-10-20. Publication assessment and prospective evidence proposal only. No model execution, interface selection, sample size or threshold is approved here. Literature scope and access limits: [prior-use map](../prior_use.md).

## What STATIC and KV actually mean here

STATIC is a rule-conditional mean direction from disconnected calibration traces: subtract the overall normalized-message mean from the mean for a particular rule, normalize by RMS, and add that fixed direction at a selected residual-stream layer. The direction is chosen for the donor's assigned rule, not computed anew from that episode's full donor state. Within a trial it does not respond to the receiver. It does not encode an arbitrary donor code word by design. See [original protocol §6](../../../docs/protocol/EXPERIMENT_DESIGN.md).

The main memory link lets the receiver attend to the donor's tokenwise keys and values alongside its own. Its queries determine which donor records contribute. The central manuscript uses the fixed donor card/note/reflection-prompt cache, with retained donor rotary positions; it is not inherently an online conversation. Live V6 windows are another implementation. KV versus STATIC describes different carriers/operators. Frozen versus fresh versus responsive describes signal generation. One-way versus two-way describes topology. These axes must not be conflated.

## What the completed paper establishes

Qwen V3: mean rule-access gains KV 4.081 versus STATIC 4.179 nats, with assigned-rule donor claiming 52.3% versus 0%. This distinguishes these two treatments at a matched mean access statistic, not all conceivable steering. Llama's later formal gains differ by about 40.1%, failing the frozen 25% match; its 49.917% versus 0.583% claiming contrast is not a matched replication. Conflicting third-party records produce errors in both models, so self specificity is unestablished. Capability exceptions and WORD limitations remain. Full evidence: [post-collection assessment](../../../docs/paper/post_collection_story_20261012.md).

V1 had already tested online hidden-state ONE/TWO; it cannot be erased from the narrative. It conveyed some rule information without the main memory study's claiming phenomenon, but imperfect access/control matching limits its interpretation. The later pivot was not discovery that live injection exists. It aimed to test whether a partner's new response to the receiver matters in persistent, actual-action interaction.

The two-case V6 pilot has a feedback-sensitive correct forward response in one case, unsuccessful return actions, and a second case without that forward success. There is no reliable useful complete return chain yet. Later diagnostics show stage- and partner-sensitive interference while useful explicit partner-fact access is poor. Those are development observations, not an independent robust interaction result.

## Scientific difference left after the closest precedents

| Proposed claim | Closest precedent | Substantive additional evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Models communicate internally and use partner information | Activation communication, KVComm, LatentMAS | This claim is already answered in important settings. A new interface alone is insufficient. |
| An internal intervention changes self-related reports | Authorship steering | A known causal ground truth about private information, goals and actual decisions; report changes alone do not distinguish a functional boundary effect. |
| Donor messages affect decisions or outperform unrelated messages | The two 2026 causal latent-channel audits | Additional original-source or causal-control attribution measurements, with neutral role-binding alternatives. |
| Functional coupling changes the relation between information use, local control and attribution | No exact equivalent identified in the scoped search | Working private-state exchange, controlled actual decisions, interface/role specificity and responsive-partner interventions; fresh examples after development. Novelty remains unresolved rather than certified. |

One-way effects can be scientifically substantial if they identify a causal source/control mechanism. Conversely, bidirectionality and cooperation alone would not settle the self/other question. For the stronger two-responsive-systems ambition, actual reciprocal dependence matters in addition to functional placement.

An unrestricted history-dependent steering algorithm can reproduce the partner's computation and inputs. Therefore, no experiment should promise to rule out every steering description. The identifiable comparison is against specified fixed, open-loop and replayed alternatives. Identical replay into an identical deterministic state should agree; the informative test is how the system reacts to a new private perturbation when the partner can or cannot respond. Freshness must be controlled separately from responsiveness.

## Minimum evidence ladder for the stronger paper

1. **Demonstrate usable selective integration.** Use a short task with separate private observations and goals. Correct actions require the partner observation plus the receiver's own goal; direct imitation of the partner's goal/action should sometimes be wrong. Verify the task with explicit facts, then a bounded set of functionally motivated channel candidates. Evaluate access and goal retention before interpreting self-related reports. Selecting only the largest attribution error would not identify the most meaningful interface.
2. **Measure control against a causal ground truth.** Change partner facts and goals separately while keeping the receiver's state fixed. Score actual actions, their compatibility with each goal, original-source reports, and prospective predictions about the effect of a future cut or perturbation. Neutral third-party/role controls test generic binding difficulty. Incorrect reports are not themselves internal-cause measurements; causal dependence is not automatically ownership.
3. **Test the responsive partner where the claim requires it.** Branch from a shared state, apply a private perturbation, and follow A-to-B-to-A consequences. Compare the same carrier and recipient operation with one-way, fresh open-loop and matched replay alternatives, controlling available facts and computation as appropriate. FRESH that inherits actual prior histories is not globally interaction-free. Carry out placement comparisons separately from topology before attributing effects to either.
4. **Confirm a result, including its limits.** Retain exploratory successes and failures, then test the selected question on new episodes, different superficial labels/layouts and preferably another task or checkpoint. Report whether the result is selective cooperation, correctly recognized adoption, or a dissociation between demonstrated influence and its attribution. No particular error/fusion result is required. A structured harmful effect can be publishable with specificity and replication even without a superior cooperation score; broad impairment alone is a weak self claim.

This is an evidence ladder, not a requirement to launch a large factorial study. The next useful milestone would be a small, interpretable example showing partner information used under the receiver's own goal, followed by a controlled influence/attribution or return-path contrast. If that fails, diagnose the channel/task locally rather than automatically collecting hundreds of episodes. Actual sample sizes and admissibility rules require a subsequent PI decision.

## Publication recommendation

Preserve and revise the existing source-attribution paper as an independently valuable result. Its strong contribution is the controlled relationship between access and attribution under the tested interface, including contrary third-party evidence. It cannot retain broad self-specific or first-brain-connection claims. The current interactive observations are not sufficient for a second full-strength paper or to supply the missing grander conclusion by being appended to the old manuscript.

For the intended stronger publication, aim at a precise question: **When another responsive model enters a model's decision computation through an internal channel, what determines whether partner information is used while local goals and source/control attribution remain coherent?** Connection function and responsiveness are separable causal factors. A useful connection plus an interpretable boundary result would advance that question; better task accuracy alone would overlap heavily with latent-communication work. This assessment does not guarantee any venue outcome or claim priority.

The prior pivot was a reasonable expansion toward the PI's original ambition, but treating bidirectionality as an automatic answer to the steering objection was insufficient. The paper already written and the stronger program should have separate evidence requirements, so neither forces an indefinite series of large runs for the other.
