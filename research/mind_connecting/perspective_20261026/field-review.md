## Checks to apply to the completed supplement

1. Keep phenomenal experience, functional access, indexical location, action control and organization separable. Accurate ownership words are not sufficient evidence of appropriate action control; appropriate action need not require explicit first-person words. A successful functional intervention can be interesting without becoming a subject-count test.
2. Specify connection location, donor state, receiver operation, direction, timing and retained local state. With receiver state, complete incoming trace, schedule and randomness fixed, source liveness cannot change that realized computation. A responsive partner can matter across counterfactual inputs; a replay that matches one realized trace does not match that entire mapping.
3. Treat a common policy, shared answer, performance synergy and dependence on both components as different operational claims. One composite program can implement a pair's transition rules; redescribing it as one system does not empirically settle phenomenal unity. Nor does a generic centralization baseline identify what the pair represents.
4. Do not assert literature-wide absence or novelty from this bounded search. Slot indexing, self-description and ego-control protection already have direct precedents. A proposed narrower comparison remains locally unresolved only relative to methods actually inspected. Architecture changes can alter answerability without making an old functional question novel.
