# What connection can reveal about perspective and boundaries

October 26, 2026. Bounded field supplement for the [brief](brief.md). This is conceptual and primary-source preparation, not candidate selection, a research decision, or evidence of a new effect. The coordinator owns governance and validation. No model, raw-outcome analysis, scientific code, or old document was changed.

## The scientific object needs several distinctions

The motivation is stronger than asking whether foreign information changes an answer. A connection makes it possible to manipulate the relation between a process's information, its history, and the actions it can produce. The question is whether these relations remain organized around separately situated processes, become jointly usable, or are selectively disrupted. These are functional possibilities to investigate; none is a criterion that identifies phenomenal fusion.

The word “mine” can name different relations:

| Relation | What could establish it in this platform | What the observation does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Historical origin | A controlled record of which instance generated or received a particular event | Present endorsement, current use, or experienced ownership |
| Informational access | Successful use of private content on specified new demands | Accurate origin tracking or a shared point of view |
| Situated perspective | Behavior changes appropriately with the acting instance's location, available actions, private evidence, and time | A phenomenal first-person perspective; generic role binding remains an explanation |
| Causal control | Independently manipulating a contribution changes actual committed action | A felt sense of agency or a unique goal representation |
| Joint availability | Different contents can be combined for specified reasoning and control demands | Phenomenal unity or unrestricted access across all tasks |
| Phenomenal mineness/unity | The experienced character of being a self or experiencing contents together | Not measured by the present LLM reports, cache sharing, or performance |

These distinctions are an analytic working map, not a claim that six independent psychological modules exist. A behavioral dissociation can arise from different readout sensitivities or task demands without distinct underlying mechanisms. The conceptual roots and empirical limits follow below.

## Deciding original-source readings

### 1. Phenomenal selfhood is not synonymous with agency

Blanke and Metzinger, [*Full-body illusions and minimal phenomenal selfhood* (2009)](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/lnco/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/2009_Blanke_TICS_full-body-illusions-and-minimal-phenomenal-selfhood.pdf). Newly inspected introduction/glossary, Box 1 and concluding argument, pp. 7–8, 12; intermediate empirical discussion was sampled. This is an **opinion/theory article**, with clinical and experimental evidence discussed, not a new experimental demonstration.

Their target is the conscious experience of being a situated self. They distinguish self-identification, self-location and forms of first-person perspective, and argue that motor agency is enabling rather than constitutively necessary for minimal phenomenal selfhood. Passive bodily experience is central to their argument. Their claim about constitutive necessity is philosophical interpretation, not a finding that a cache has or lacks phenomenal properties. Consequently, preserving an LLM's assigned goal would neither establish nor exhaust the human phenomenon they discuss. Conversely, an ownership-report error need not show loss of agency. The useful translation is to specify which relation a measurement addresses; adopting their phenomenological vocabulary without its target would obscure the inference.

Gallagher's [2000 author preprint](https://www.ummoss.org/tics2000.html) was located through primary author-hosted indexed text, but direct retrieval failed. Only its abstract-level ownership/agency and minimal/narrative distinction is retained as a lead, not an independently audited decisive argument.

### 2. Human self-location can be experimentally perturbed, with specific measures

Lenggenhager et al., [*Video ergo sum* (2007), original supplementary methods/results](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/lnco/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/2007_Lenggenhager_Ssom_video-ergo-sum-manipulating-bodily-self-consciousness.pdf), pp. 1–5. Newly read methods and supplementary results; original main-paper abstract located, full main-paper retrieval failed.

Two independent groups of 14 participants underwent visual–tactile manipulations. In Study I, participants saw their back being stroked through a display; synchronous versus asynchronous stimulation was compared. After displacement, returning to the initial position supplied a behavioral localization measure; questionnaires supplied attribution reports. Synchronous stimulation increased forward drift relative to asynchronous stimulation. Study II compared own body, mannequin and nonbodily object. Critically, the direct synchrony effect on drift was significant only in the mannequin condition, while attribution items distinguished felt touch from identification with a body.

The result is a controlled alteration of bodily localization and reports, not a merger of subjects. It illustrates why a single “self score” is inadequate, and why human bodily selfhood should not be reduced to grammatical first-person statements. It does not validate analogous LLM measures or show that sensory input is irrelevant to selfhood.

### 3. De se knowledge connects content to situated action, but its special status is disputed

Perry, [*The Problem of the Essential Indexical* (1979)](https://www.uvm.edu/~lderosse/courses/lang/Perry%281979%29.pdf). Newly read opening grocery/locating-belief examples and final belief-state account, journal pp. 3–5 and 18–20; intervening argumentative details were not fully audited.

The shopper can know that someone spills sugar without recognizing that he himself does. The recognition changes his action. Perry distinguishes what is believed from the belief state relevant to explanation: similar indexically characterized states can guide analogous actions in different people while concerning different people. This is a philosophical argument about belief and action, not an experimental result or a proposed phenomenal test. It motivates distinguishing transmission of a proposition from appropriate adjustment to the recipient's action situation. It does not show that the pronoun “I” implements a special representation.

Cappelen and Dever, [*The Inessential Indexical* (2013), original preview](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9780191510243_A24388035/preview-9780191510243_A24388035.pdf), introduction pp. 1–2, plus [chapter 3 abstract](https://academic.oup.com/book/5503/chapter-abstract/148419932). These authors dispute a philosophically distinctive perspective/agency connection and propose treating perspectival facts as ordinary relations. Only the introductory position and chapter abstract were inspected, not the full rebuttal. This live disagreement prevents treating an action-centered LLM effect as an empirical settlement of essential indexicality.

### 4. Joint access and joint experience are different explanatory targets

Bayne and Chalmers, [*What is the Unity of Consciousness?* (2003)](https://consc.net/papers/unity.html), newly inspected §3 and the access-bottleneck discussion. This is philosophical analysis.

They distinguish jointly available content for report, reasoning and control from conjoint phenomenology. Their analysis of limited access, including a debated interpretation of Sperling, explicitly argues that failure of joint access need not imply failure of phenomenal unity. They distinguish availability from actual simultaneous use. The relevant extension is to test which combinations of imported content a connected computation can actually use across specified demands. We should call that joint availability or functional composition, not silently equate it with their consciousness predicate. Passing a conjunction task, sharing one answer, or increasing communication bandwidth does not establish a shared experience; failing those tasks does not establish experiential separation either. Their paper is not an empirical criterion for counting subjects.

### 5. Extended cognition concerns the boundary of a capacity, with controversial constitutive claims

Clark and Chalmers, [*The Extended Mind* (1998)](https://consc.net/papers/extended.html). Reopened selected notebook, access, social-extension and concluding self passages, supplementing the [existing note](../three_lines_20261025/knowledge/field/papers/extended_mind_1998.md).

The philosophical proposal treats reliable, routinely available external resources as potentially constituting cognition, illustrated by Otto's notebook and social reliance. The authors do discuss an extended self; it would be inaccurate to say they address only task performance. But their argument concerns cognitive identity and standing dispositions, not demonstrated fusion of phenomenal subjects. Whether causal reliance establishes constitution is a further philosophical issue. A disconnection or partner-replacement result can locate functional dependence; by itself it cannot settle that issue. The computational extension remains valuable if it specifies what work is performed locally, by the partner, and by an external controller, and what persists after separation. Existing formation and capacity/portability proposals already cover parts of that space and retain their previous objections and repair counts.

### 6. Actual human brain interfaces support mediated collaboration

Jiang et al., [*BrainNet* (2019)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-019-41895-7). Reopened abstract, interface/task description and reliability result; detailed prior reading is recorded in the [existing note](../three_lines_20261025/knowledge/field/papers/brainnet_2019.md).

Five triads performed a block-rotation task. EEG-derived sender decisions were encoded into TMS signals for a receiver, and a second round allowed senders to respond to the receiver's first decision. One sender's recommendations were deliberately corrupted. Reported average task accuracy was 81.25%, with greater reliance on the more reliable sender over time. This is useful brain-mediated communication with source-sensitive integration. Decoding, stimulation, task feedback and distinct sender positions organize the exchange; rich unmediated thought transplantation was not performed. No measure establishes experiential fusion. Thus the human analogy motivates carefully decomposed functions of a connection, not an inference from “neural” to “shared self.”

### 7. LLM internal monitoring already has causal precedents and unsettled interpretations

Lindsey, [*Emergent Introspective Awareness in Large Language Models* (2025)](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2025/introspection/index.html). Newly inspected injection criteria/layer comparisons and unintended-versus-intended-output experiment; no full appendix or code audit.

The injection study scores whether a model detects and correctly names an injected concept before saying that concept, while remaining coherent. Effects depend on layer, strength and model. A separate experiment prefills an unexpected output and varies whether a matching concept was injected before that output; later reports are more likely to accept the output as intentional after the matching prior injection. A timing control injects after the prefill. These are direct precedents for internal-state-dependent reports and reported authorship. They concern imposed prefills and steering, not a donor's independently generated commitments or shared experience. The strongest reported results are not evidence that pinned Qwen3-4B has the same capacity. The paper explicitly brackets human-like self-awareness and subjective experience.

Lederman and Mahowald, [*Dissociating Direct Access from Inference in AI Introspection* (2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.05414v1). Newly inspected §§3–7, especially first-/third-person framing, priming and prompt-only versus continuous injection; no full appendix audit or reproduction. Preprint.

The third-person condition asks the steered observer about a fictional model shown saying “Ok”; it is not an actual communicating partner. First-person advantages and priming controls reject specified prompt-anomaly accounts. In the Qwen3-235B-A22B comparison at strength 6, sweeping Qwen layers, stopping injection before generation largely preserves detection while reducing correct identification. The authors infer a content-agnostic internal detection signal plus later inference about content. Rates are conditional on coherent/denies-introspection responses, and strength/coherence filters matter. This is evidence against particular alternatives, not a uniquely localized neural monitor. It establishes close precedent for detection-versus-content-report distinctions, while leaving transfer to our model and two-instance setting untested.

[*Can LLMs Introspect? A Reality Check* (2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.26242v1). Newly inspected §5.3 methods/results and conclusion; earlier biofeedback experiments were only sampled. Preprint.

This study adds textual manipulation to activation intervention and control. Its reported three-way tests fail to reliably distinguish input-level and hidden-state intervention despite successful binary detection in some models. It also records prompt dependence, searches layer/strength settings, and uses different steering schedules across models. This qualifies the interpretation of binary detection as specifically introspective. It does not reproduce or refute every previous model and paradigm, and does not establish that all internal monitoring is impossible. Together these originals make internal access, anomaly sensitivity, source discrimination and metacognitive interpretation separate empirical questions.

## Connection architecture: what can be inferred before a new study

The [corrected latent-communication audit](../gap_audit_20261024/sources.md) already establishes that internal exchange and retained local control are studied together. LACO varies KV sharing depth in collaborative driving; Goals Leak leaves goal binding, retrieval and policy competition unresolved. These are reused at the prior audit's recorded reading depth; the root is separately reopening channel details. The [previous refresh](../flagship_search_20261026b/field-refresh.md) supplies original readings on uncertainty recoverability, flexible content use, Brainets and evidence ancestry. None is promoted here to new verification.

Changing payload, insertion site, read/write operation, timing or directional topology can change which computations receive what information. Therefore an effect in one architecture cannot simply be assumed to persist in another. But architecture sensitivity alone is weak evidence of a self-related mechanism: disruption, signal scaling, representation compatibility, ordinary retrieval and changed available evidence can all alter behavior. More reciprocal connections do not logically imply more useful composition, more accurate attribution, or a wider phenomenal boundary. The [early integration note](../three_lines_20261025/knowledge/field/papers/integration_2003.md) is especially relevant: its joining simulation reallocates fixed total connection strength, so it does not predict simply adding a bridge while preserving local dynamics.

The proposed alternatives-transfer comparison addresses usable content beyond a committed answer. It could inform the organization of shared reasoning, but it does not by itself test who takes an action, how evidence is situated, or whether the pair has one experience. “Alternative possibilities” and “first-person perspective” are different constructs. Generic novelty is also constrained by the prior Interlat, uncertainty-carrier, source-report and workspace readings.

## What remains an answerable scientific uncertainty

The private character of experience does not make third-person science pointless. Human experiments can compare reports, behavior and interventions under explicit linking assumptions. But the present platform lacks a validated link from its computational measures to phenomenal experience. It can answer causal questions about functional organization without claiming that this settles experience.

A broad uncertainty worth keeping open is whether different forms of connection alter the relationship between **shared usable content, separately situated action, and accurately tracked causal contribution**. This is a research domain, not a favored candidate or a novelty claim. Several outcomes are informative: more useful sharing with stable local perspective; homogenized outputs with poorer joint use; accurate content use but impaired attribution; accurate attribution without useful complementary action. Existing project results already constrain some narrow report/access contrasts. They do not establish the complete relationship or its underlying mechanism.

To make any such extension consequential, the questioner must state the behavioral relation at issue and a competing ordinary explanation. A neutral actor-binding task, instruction hierarchy, ordinary premise recomputation, or external controller can reproduce many apparently self-related signatures. A study that only renames one of these as “fusion” has added no answerable scientific content. Conversely, discovering a reproducible limitation or invariance of useful coupling could be valuable without proving an exceptional self mechanism.

No claim that this is the uniquely deepest question follows. The field supplement deliberately leaves candidate choice to independent generation and screening. Decisive missing evidence includes reliable useful native interaction, actual committed actions under the relevant counterfactuals, and controls distinguishing the proposed relation from generic content/role handling. This is conceptual progress, not experimental readiness.
