# Sharing a thought and sharing its point of view

October 26, 2026. This answers the PI's question about connection architecture, self boundaries and the original brain-bridge motivation. It corrects the previous framing: using another model's unchosen possibility tests content reuse, not selfhood. The conceptual priority proposed here is **whether shared internal content can support two appropriately situated uses, and what makes those uses remain separate or become systematically misplaced**. This is an agenda and a candidate comparison, not a demonstrated effect, certified literature gap, adopted experiment or claim of phenomenal fusion. The [screening](screening.md) governs candidate readiness.

## Why the connection is part of the question

An internal connection can provide extra evidence, alter a computation, or supply a state already shaped by another context. These are different operations. Specify what is sent, where/how the receiver uses it, its source/position context, when it arrives and persists, and whether the sender responds to later events. A fixed contextual KV cache is not automatically an interactive partner. Changing all these factors together cannot identify which relation mattered.

Our STATIC condition was a particular fixed averaged rule direction. Our memory KV condition was structured contextual donor state. Similar scalar rule-access scores did not produce similar attribution, but neither scalar matching nor one route's low error equates complete information or proves that STATIC never confuses. [LACO](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.22504v1), §§3–5, independently demonstrates architecture-sensitive ego-control interference in collaborating driving models. Its depth result is not a location of self in Qwen. A useful study should therefore make a specific architectural distinction explain a specific behavioral contrast, rather than rank connections by an undefined amount of fusion.

## What the original philosophical intuition opens

The PI begins with the privacy of first-person experience. Possessing a description of another's experience, accessing a computational state, and experiencing what the other experiences are different propositions. A successful latent transfer establishes the second only to the extent demonstrated by its use; it does not settle the third. This is an inferential limit of the present assay, not a claim that machine experience is impossible.

The productive opening is that connection lets us separate three relations normally bundled in an individual: where a state was produced, who can use it, and whose situation organizes its consequences. A record can originate in B, be accessible to A, and appropriately guide A's action. None of those relations needs to be inferred from the pronoun in a report.

[Perry's indexical argument](https://www.uvm.edu/~lderosse/courses/lang/Perry(1979).pdf) illustrates why knowing a fact and locating oneself in that fact can guide different action. This is philosophical motivation, not a new theory we invented or a proven special self mechanism. [Paul et al.2026](https://lapaul.org/papers/Reverse%20engineering%20a%20self.pdf) already turns centering and changing embodiment into computational models and human experiments. The present candidate must add a relation involving internally shared content, not merely implement avatar recognition with an LLM.

## A concrete illustration of the retained question

Imagine two copies with the same team goal: both must reach the safe exit. A controls one avatar; B controls another with a different action mapping. B privately observes a new barrier. That observation reaches A through internal state. The relevant contrast is whether A can derive its own appropriate action while also deriving B's different appropriate action from the shared situation. Agreement is not the success criterion: the correct actions may need to differ.

Now separately change the name attached to each actor, which actuator its commands actually control, and the sender's own orientation or action mapping. Establish control through action/outcome history, rather than a name alone. A functional recentring result would follow the current control relation; sender-centered mistakes would reverse with the sender's mapping; label-binding mistakes would follow the label. These are candidate behavioral predictions, not yet uniquely identified neural mechanisms. A neutral two-controller task can reproduce the entire relational problem and is essential for interpreting any supposedly self-specific result.

The potential conceptual payoff is **shared information with differentiated situated use**, or a reproducible failure to detach useful information from the sender's action frame. The latter would be more informative than generic accuracy loss if it follows the prespecified frame reversals while ordinary factual use remains demonstrable. The former would show that functional individuation survives the tested sharing operation. Neither is evidence of one or two phenomenal subjects, and neither is automatically new relative to LACO, role-indexing or ordinary relational computation.

The next check is modest: build the exact crossed prediction table and see whether it distinguishes the proposed explanations beyond a named-controller lookup, then compare that contrast directly with the closest originals. This is not a run-ready plan. Material generation, carrier matching, competence admission, statistical precision and implementation remain unspecified. Do not select a connection using the desired perspective effect. Establish usable content and task competence independently; a failed channel cannot answer the question.

## What would and would not count as fusion

Two systems may share facts, perform useful joint computation, influence one another's decisions, or emit identical answers. Those are different observable relations. They do not supply a validated criterion for a single point of experience. [Bayne and Chalmers](https://consc.net/papers/unity.html) distinguish joint access from phenomenal unity; the distinction does not settle their neural realization. Human [BrainNet](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-019-41895-7) demonstrates mediated collaboration, not transfer of rich experience. Split-brain evidence also has contested subject-unity interpretations; reversing the wiring does not invert a settled subject-count law. The [field review](field-review.md) records those original-source limits.

For this project, asking whether the pair retains differentiated situated use is an answerable part of the motivation. Calling any loss of differentiation fusion would confuse damage, policy copying, common evidence and joint control. A high-impact result would need a reproducible functional relationship across carefully specified connections or a selective intervention that changes situated use while preserving useful content. Neither relationship has been established by current data, and a generic binding account may explain it.

## Existing clues and candidate disposition

Our old results justify separating access, source report and action. KV/STATIC differences at roughly matched scalar access, selective START/NOW recovery after a readout cut, and correct goal labels alongside wrong rule answers all motivate the separation. They do not establish the new action-frame phenomenon. The Robin controls show that generic conflicting-record errors remain a serious explanation. Cutting readout sharing retained local history; it did not restore an earlier self. No dependable useful complete reciprocal native loop is established. The [corrected evidence audit](../gap_audit_20261024/evidence.md) supplies the actual scope.

The independent [candidates](candidates.md) also ask whether predictive access to another process can be redistributed, and whether circulated evidence is mistaken for independent evidence. The first overlaps the earlier retrieval/rescue queue. [Binder et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.13787v1) already tests trained self/cross prediction; [Patchscopes](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.06102v4) already decodes internal representations in another prompt and supports output prediction and correction. A native partner's advantage must survive fair history, compute, self-clone and inspection-context comparisons before adding a new scientific claim. The evidence-circulation candidate remains literature-incomplete and needs the missing useful return path.

The recommendation is to retain the shared-content/multiple-perspective question as the most direct conceptual connection to the PI's current interest, while withholding flagship promotion. Its attractiveness and its novelty are separate judgments. Current evidence supports careful preparation and a discriminability audit, not a promise that it will produce a top-conference paper. The previous alternatives proposal remains a separate, more developed content-use option; it has not been repurposed into a self experiment.
