# Root source and architecture checks

October 26, 2026. Primary reading supplements the field author's work; no model reproduction. This is conceptual preparation, not a novelty verdict.

[LACO](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.22504v1), sections 3–5 and Tables 2–3: root reopened actual methods/results. Full-depth KV fusion can impair vehicle-specific control; shallow selected sharing supports useful closed-loop driving. Their interpretation invokes perspective/intent entanglement, but measured behavior, attention and depth ablations do not independently identify a self variable or subject fusion. The precise ORION 10% figure differs between tables, so do not quote that value. Generic useful sharing with retained local control and depth-dependent behavior already have direct precedents.

[Li et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.16717v3), sections 3.1.3, 3.2.6–3.2.7 and 6.3: root reopened original. Assistant-slot text was not generated by that instance. Role conditioning affects attribution and use of wrong arithmetic, with causal interventions at distinct positions. Role-indexed use and report/use dissociation are established comparisons; an externally supplied role tag is not a demonstrated history of actual agent authorship or phenomenal selfhood.

[Perry 1979](https://www.uvm.edu/~lderosse/courses/lang/Perry(1979).pdf), original journal pp3–5: knowing a descriptive fact and locating oneself in that fact can explain different action. This is a philosophical argument about belief individuation, not an empirical result identifying an irreducible neural self variable. Opposing essential-indexical positions exist; no consensus claim follows.

[Sensorimotor Self-Recognition](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.19237), current title differs from older search title. Root inspected abstract and Materials/Methods robot/sensor sections; sensors feed aggregated JSON plus images/memory. No root full result audit is claimed. This is a relevant precedent lead against calling sensorimotor self-mapping a new general question; the field author/reviewer may inspect further.

Existing native runtime primitives were previously inspected in ../mechanism_priority_20261026/source-and-runtime-check.md. They support separate states, clone/export/replay and actual commitment. They do not establish successful useful reciprocal communication, a self-specific recipient site, or an implemented new control/perspective task. STATIC is a fixed averaged rule direction; KV is contextual structured state. A deterministic receiver given exactly identical effective input/state cannot distinguish a live source from exact replay. New perturbations can test contingency across possible trajectories, not a mystical live-source property.

Architecture should be decomposed into what is sent, receiving operation/site, source indexing, timing/history and feedback. Varying layers changes representational content and available compute as a bundle. No assumption that a particular layer corresponds to perception, intention or self transfers from a VLA paper to our Qwen instance without independent validation. Equal scalar ACC is not equal accessible information. Finite experiments can establish specified functional dissociations or causal dependencies, not universal subjective integration or exhaustive architectural separation.

[Paul et al., Reverse Engineering the Centered Self (2026)](https://lapaul.org/papers/Reverse%20engineering%20a%20self.pdf), root read PDF pp10–18 and pp25–26, with introduction sampled. A hierarchical Bayesian agent actively identifies the controlled avatar and recenters when embodiments or key mappings change. Models are compared with human gameplay, including a new 220-participant, eleven-condition study. The authors explicitly limit their implemented framework and do not establish a necessary or sufficient account of selfhood. Thus active actuator discovery, changing body mappings and action-centered self-location already have direct computational and behavioral precedents. The inspected experiments do not join two LLMs through internal communication. That difference is not by itself a scientific contribution. The PhilArchive direct fetch failed; the author-hosted final original opened successfully.

[Binder et al., Looking Inward](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.13787v1), root read v1 sections 2.1–2.2 and cross-prediction rationale; the ICLR2025 abstract was checked but its PDF was browser-blocked. Self- and cross-predictors are fine-tuned, and ground truth is behavior in separate hypothetical/object-level contexts. Predicting a property rather than verbatim output avoids a trivial repeat-the-prompt solution. The competing observer lacks all target training data, an acknowledged assumption. This is a direct precedent for operational privileged self-prediction, not a study of frozen communicating instances sharing their current caches. A proposal for transferring such access needs an explicit comparison against ordinary state decoding, equal computation, and a self-clone. No new-candidate endorsement follows.

Late screening supplement: [Zhang and Emu, Do Latent Channels Actually Communicate? (2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.26773v1), root read audit definitions and experimental methods/results. It compares original, absent, other-example and compute-matched self-generated latent messages on Qwen3-4B/8B benchmarks. Separate-source value and example-specific content effects can differ. A cross-fitted sender-correctness decoder is distinct from actual receiver use. Reported uncertainty does not establish practical equivalence for the displayed contrasts. This is a close methodological precedent against calling message substitution or self-copy controls novel. Models/revisions, channel and task differ from the pinned platform, and these experiments do not establish phenomenal ownership or a transferable first-person advantage. The source arrived after questioner handoff and must be given to screening explicitly.

[Patchscopes, ICML2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.06102v4), root read framework introduction, sections4.1/4.4, section5 setup and appendixB.3. Same-model patching into a new inspection prompt predicts eventual output from intermediate states; cross-size variants learn affine alignment. Inspection-context ablations and multihop correction demonstrate that relocation and decoding already have direct precedents. This is a critical comparator for Q2 and the earlier retrieval/rescue family. Generic cross-instance readout success, a fresh context, or output prediction cannot be promoted as the first transfer of self-access. The full appendix and code were not audited. Given to screening before its verdict.
