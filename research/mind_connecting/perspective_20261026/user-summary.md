# Intended user-facing explanation

The connection architecture matters: its payload, receiving operation, timing and responsiveness change the computational question. Our particular STATIC and KV routes cannot stand for all fixed and interactive connections. LACO already demonstrates control interference that depends on sharing depth.

The previous unchosen-alternative proposal measures usable content, not the boundary of a self. The PI's experience-privacy intuition motivates separating where a state was formed, who can use it, and whose situation determines what action it warrants. Access to a state does not alone establish access to another's experience.

The conceptual question to retain is: after sharing internal content, can the pair still use it from two correctly distinguished action perspectives? Two copies pursuing the same team goal can need different actions because they control different bodies or tools. Transfer a privately observed obstacle, then independently vary names, action mappings and sender orientation. Correct own action and correct partner-action prediction should follow their respective mappings. Systematic sender-frame mistakes differ from label mistakes and nonspecific degradation. This is an illustration and candidate audit, not an adopted experiment or uniquely identified self mechanism. A neutral controller-binding problem may explain all results.

The potentially striking result is sharing more useful knowledge while preserving distinct situated uses, or isolating a predictable imported action frame despite successful factual access. Neither result counts subjects or proves fused experience. We do not know that either occurs. Same answers, hidden-state similarity and dependence on both components are insufficient for that inference.

Perry supplies the philosophical distinction; Paul2026 already models centering and changes of embodiment; LACO already studies useful internal sharing with retained local control. These precedents prevent claiming the broad idea is new. Old project evidence motivates separating access, attribution and action but is not evidence of the new phenomenon; Robin conflict and weak native reciprocity remain constraints.

Predictive access is an interesting secondary queue, but Binder and Patchscopes already precede generic self-prediction and reading another context's hidden representations. Independent screening and the source checks support withholding flagship promotion. The positive recommendation is a conceptual focus and the next exact discriminability/precedent check. A meaningful contribution must add a consequential reproducible functional relationship beyond what the existing comparisons establish, and remain feasible with the pinned model. Ordinary role binding could explain an informative result; a lookup solution alone does not refute the question. If the comparison merely repeats exhausted goal-control or acquisition/use map interference, those arguments and their consumed repairs carry forward. No new protocol or model study has been adopted.
