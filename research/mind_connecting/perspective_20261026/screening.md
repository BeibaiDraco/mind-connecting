## Q1: a coherent functional question, without a demonstrated special-self increment

I reopened [Paul et al., *Reverse Engineering the Centered Self*](https://lapaul.org/papers/Reverse%20engineering%20a%20self.pdf), Study 2 methods, PDF pp. 17–19, and its model comparison discussion. Eleven game variants include changing embodiments, changing mappings, noisy action contingencies and goal uncertainty. The 220 participants were told the game rules; the computational agents also know those dynamics. The comparison concerns locating the controllable avatar and acting from the appropriate problem representation. Thus causal actuator discovery and recentering are substantial precedents, not superficial terminological neighbors. These inspected comparisons do not test two internally communicating LLMs using one shared item for two actions.

I also reopened [LACO](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.22504v1), §§4–5, closed-loop setup and comparator/results table. It compares ego-only, textual, visual and latent collaboration in two-vehicle scenes and restricts deeper external injection. It already connects shared internal information to vehicle-specific useful control. The reported behavior does not uniquely establish a self variable. Neither its interpretation nor its preferred injection depth transfers automatically to pinned Qwen.

Q1's exact proposed increment is simultaneous correct transformation of a shared item under two independently grounded mappings, including label/mapping disagreement. That is scientifically intelligible. It cannot be dismissed simply because a lookup rule can solve it: ordinary relational computation could be the informative answer. However, the present candidate does not identify a consequence that makes the contrast substantially more informative than established recentering plus role binding. A map-reversal error alone could be generic foreign mapping contamination. The prior acquisition/use plan already crosses recipient maps and sender-generated evidence; calling the crossover “perspective” does not detach it from that lineage.

Before major investment, the first uncertainty is conceptual contribution: provide the smallest case in which the own-action/partner-prediction pair distinguishes a consequential account from both ordinary neutral two-controller binding and the existing acquisition/use crossover. A symbolic table can prove separability of specified policies; it cannot prove a special first-person representation or its novelty. If the decisive contrast reduces to donor-goal or map interference, carry goal-control/acquisition-use's two consumed refinements. A genuinely different simultaneous dual-use argument must explain its difference before receiving a new development cycle.

## Q2: the strongest remaining hook needs a stricter readout comparison

The late lead [Patchscopes](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.06102v4), independently reopened at §§3.1, 4.1–4.4 and 5, is decisive. It patches a representation into another inference pass, including the same model with a different prompt, to decode output predictions or other information. Same-model feature extraction needs no trained mapping. Cross-model experiments can learn affine mappings. Its multihop correction uses structural knowledge of the query and counts success when some source/target layer pair succeeds; 46 examples survive its competence filtering. The authors explicitly qualify comparisons with ordinary chain-of-thought. This establishes a close precedent for recontextualized internal readout and useful latent rerouting, not an optimally resource-matched general recovery result. It also cautions that later computation can add information to a patched representation. The generic claim that another inference pass can express something the original pass did not is therefore unavailable as the new contribution.

The [previous retrieval/rescue screening](../flagship_search_20261026b/screening.md) already requires a fair donor-readout comparison and an identical self-copy control. Q2 changes the target from a hidden fact to a property of the target's independently continuing behavior. That is a meaningful possible refinement, but not yet a new scientific argument with a clean repair budget. Binder's trained self/cross-prediction work, at the [root's inspected methods scope](root-source-checks.md), further supplies the privileged-access comparison. The new latent-channel audit also already distinguishes example-specific foreign content from extra self-computation; its trained sender-correctness decoder is not the same as receiver use. I rely on the root's explicit methods/results audit for those two papers rather than claiming a fresh full independent read.

The main unresolved issue is what “privilege changes hands” means on this platform. Let the untouched target action be generated from state S under a specified action continuation. A prediction readout applies a different continuation to S, or to a transmitted function of S. A recipient can outperform one chosen self-report prompt because its access operation, context or computation differs. A literal self-clone with the identical predictor state, input and randomness cannot differ because it is called “self.” This limits interpretation to a property of the readout arrangement; it does not make that property uninteresting. The strong question is which arrangement makes target-specific predictive information usable and at what resource cost.

The first worthwhile check is therefore a paper-only ledger: unchanged target action branch and prediction timing; exact target state/history available to each predictor; shared versus private future randomness; target property chosen without outcome search; action-generation budget versus prediction budget; and identical-clone/fresh-context/complete-history baselines. State access must vary while the prediction objective stays fixed. An incoming-state intervention must change correct target prediction without merely copying the target action as the recipient's own policy. If the fair result would only show another prompt or extra inference improving a prediction, frame it as readout/access engineering and justify why that narrower result matters. Do not promote it as transferred first-person privilege.

This check has not been executed here, and no new formal plan is selected merely to fill the remaining time. A later bounded continuation could examine it before returning to this queue. No evidence in the inspected originals proves that the exact comparison is exhausted; equally, no demonstrated residual effect or compelling independent contribution currently warrants flagship endorsement.
