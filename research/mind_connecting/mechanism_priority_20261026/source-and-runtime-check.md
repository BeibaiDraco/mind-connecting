# Deciding source and implementation checks

October 26, 2026. Bounded continuation of the reviewed flagship assessment. New searches combined latent communication, exploration, evidence, active information gathering and independent exploration. The original methods below were opened; no experiments or external code were reproduced. Prior source dossiers remain the evidence for older comparisons.

## New close precedents

[ArcticSwarm](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.01870), §§3–5.1.1 and Table 3. It directly separates evidence gathering from integration: isolation tasks can publish but cannot read the shared board. In the controlled Qwen3.5-27B study on all 830 BrowseComp-Plus questions, disabling isolation changes accuracy from 82.6% to 78.8%, with relative tokens 1.00 versus .98. At ten search paths, reported distinct-passage coverage is 804 with isolation and 618 without. Thus even real external-evidence acquisition, a communication restriction and downstream utility are direct precedents. The result does not isolate button-following versus semantic-target-following, nor a native-state intervention. Some motivating review/alternative-task comparisons are observational, and other architecture comparisons differ in realized compute. The proposed project cannot claim first gathering/integration separation or first timing remedy.

[Interlat: Enabling Agents to Communicate Entirely in Latent Space](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.09149), §5 implementation/results and Appendix D/Table 6. Actor models and communication components are trained; evaluation includes ALFWorld and MATH, with three-run averages. It compares task-matched messages, text/no communication, cross-task messages and perturbations. Appendix D relates success to action count and interprets some longer successful trajectories as productive exploration. Its own unseen-task ablations show that longer trajectories can instead accompany worse success. This is a direct latent-communication/exploration precedent, but step counts and success do not measure independently acquired environmental facts or isolate a copied action level. Training-versus-freezing alone does not create our scientific contribution.

[The Weight of Teamwork](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.24131), §§2–3 and Appendix J. Fixed 50-call symbolic-regression comparisons include 100 Qwen problems and a 41-problem Terra subset. The study varies peer visibility/update timing and evaluates frozen candidate archives under common versus alternative selectors. Qwen Ring minus Independent is −0.33 held-out Q, with 95% interval [−0.49, −0.18]; Star and Complete means have zero-spanning intervals. Candidate diversity does not consistently explain delivered quality. The paper explicitly preserves exploratory contrast status and does not identify a mechanism. Therefore separating generation from final selection is also established; merely adopting that decomposition is insufficient.

These originals further narrow the opportunity. A prospective action-coordinate intervention with complete environmental information accounting could provide a specific explanatory result. No inspected paper settles that exact comparison, but bounded non-discovery is not a priority guarantee. It must predict behavior beyond generic performance loss and matter for useful task outcomes to justify a flagship.

## What the repository actually supports

Read-only inspection of `src/mb/v6_native.py`: `clone` copies caches, tokens, logits and recovery/seal metadata; `dump_state`/`load_state` and export archives provide persistence; `probe` works on a clone; `commit_answer` verifies a sealed state/window and returns a continuing state. `step` creates per-token native K/V exports. These are useful primitives, not implementation of the proposed task.

`src/mb/v6_interaction_pilot.py` reuses real action commitment, whole-pair checkpoints and replay. Its diagnostic probes assert that continuing-history digests stay unchanged. The existing runner's world and prompts are the old task; its fixed work schedule cannot silently become an acquisition experiment. `v6_execution.py` binds materials, source identity and hardware admission, so a new task requires a new explicitly reviewed runner/admission contract.

Missing work includes the instrument world, pre-observation snapshot and cue schedule, no-leak prompt rendering, matched yoke provenance, hidden late-gate assignment, actual measured/imported-fact accounting, action legality/scoring, and a fixed second task family. No current native channel is certified useful for this task. Old exact replay checks establish integrity on their tested paths, not scientific validity of new interventions. No low-level head/layer surgery is needed for the primary behavioral comparison, and none is proposed as already available.
