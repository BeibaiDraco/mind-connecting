# Candidate comparison beyond the old manuscript

2026-10-20. Ranking concerns next research investment, not probability of a desired result or conference acceptance. Existing observations can be supporting evidence rather than the center of the eventual paper.

| Candidate | Knowledge it could change | Existing support and nearest alternative | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Can evidence be shared without importing task-irrelevant control influences? | Whether an internal link preserves selective decision-making; whether access, action control and attribution dissociate | Old access/attribution findings plus goal-label/rule dissociation. | B. A and B need not both become the paper. Strong selective integration plus source/control dissociation can support A. Strong dependency tracking or systematic failure of it, beyond matched text/history predictors, could make B the main story even if the old ownership phenomenon becomes background. C could become central only after a successful, appropriately controlled interaction pilot. No branch requires fusion, impairment or LIVE superiority.

Important rejected default: improve the previous manuscript through more model/gain cells. That does not answer the PI's current question. Another rejected default is treating “bidirectional” as intrinsically more interesting than a one-way functional intervention. The scientific target determines which topology is necessary.
