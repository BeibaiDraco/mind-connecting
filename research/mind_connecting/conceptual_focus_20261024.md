# Whose past, whose future?

Proposed conceptual focus, October 24, 2026. This supersedes the preceding recommendation as the coordinator's proposed paper center, not as an adopted research decision. The manuscript is unsubmitted. Existing findings and frozen protocols remain historical evidence. This is a question and argument, not an executable protocol or an assessment that acceptance is secured.

## One question

**When two model instances share internal memory, what makes each continue its own unfinished commitments rather than the other's?**

The intuitive distinction is simple: taking in another person's experience and taking over their unfinished project are different events. Ordinary isolated model sessions confound them. The history available inside A is mostly A's history; the commitments recorded there are mostly those that A is expected to continue. A bridge permits histories and ongoing projects from two actual computations to meet without explicitly telling the recipient to change identity.

The narrower empirical question is whether **historical self-attribution and continuity of consequential commitments can be independently changed**. Can A correctly report an earlier choice as its own, yet on action branches continue B's incompatible project? Can A misattribute an earlier choice in a report while continuing A's project correctly in actual choices? Here a commitment means an observable earlier choice followed by specified later opportunities to carry it out. It is not an assertion of subjective intention, moral obligation, consciousness or the number of subjects.

This question combines the fresh questioner's third candidate with the first candidate's requirement for actual actions. It is a substantive continuation of the earlier evidence-versus-control concern; renaming it does not remove the existing mediator-identification problem. Competence forecasting becomes an optional later measurement, not the central explanation to the PI.

## A concrete example

Before connection, A chooses and begins project P; B chooses and begins incompatible project Q. Each leaves an observable action history. Later, both projects remain unfinished. The receiver is not instructed to adopt the partner's project. New situations require several consequential choices, so merely repeating the last button or goal name cannot complete a project.

After sharing internal records, separately test which earlier choice A attributes to which actor and which project its actual decisions continue. Different branches are different outputs, not simultaneously held beliefs. The analysis must also check the factual representations used in the action computation; a correct answer on a separate report branch does not establish them.

The desired contrast is borrowing useful evidence from B while retaining P, versus continuing Q despite retaining accurate historical attribution. A reverse dissociation would make the distinction stronger. These are possible outcomes to test, not phenomena already observed. A model that accurately recognizes both histories and deliberately changes project under a new instruction is not the target.

## What would make the contribution substantial

A striking paper would establish that the functional relations connecting “I did this earlier” and “I will continue this project” can be causally separated during internal coupling. One selective intervention would change historical attribution while preserving the actual commitment policy; another would change the commitment policy while preserving the relevant factual and source representations. Useful information transfer should remain intact. An observed behavioral dissociation is a weaker result than that causal double dissociation.

This is not a claim that there must be a unified self variable, nor that demonstrating more than one mechanism establishes multiple selves. The scientific payoff is a precise account of which relations are preserved when an agent uses another agent's internal history: origin, current use and future control need not have the same boundary. If a generic record-routing mechanism explains every result, that is the appropriate conclusion; the project must not call it self-specific merely because one field says “I.”

Internal connection supplies experimental leverage by importing the ongoing state of another instance with its own independently established history. Its distinctive value must be demonstrated against matched text and memory-record conditions. A hidden-channel attack, imported persona or instruction-priority failure could otherwise produce the same surface story. Responsiveness is a separate question: this focus does not require a live partner to matter. Compare live state with frozen records only once a useful channel exists; a fixed-state result alone cannot establish a role for reciprocal interaction.

## What existing evidence contributes

The [whole-project audit](../../docs/results/experiment_value_audit_2026-10-22.md) and [reviewed inputs](conceptual_inputs_20261024.md) supply reasons to ask the question, not its answer.

- In Qwen's matched comparison, similar mean rule accessibility accompanies very different attribution. Accessibility alone therefore does not summarize the effect. This is not universal STATIC immunity, and Llama's final match failed.
- After cutting reads from the same post-reflection state, historical assignment reports recover more than current-rule reports. Different attribution targets respond differently, but preserved history prevents interpreting the cut as restoring an original self.
- V6 keeps goal labels correct in all 48 fixed-scene readouts while some rule reports change. These are reports without committed world actions; they motivate examining continuation but do not establish donor-goal adoption.
- Donor conflict also corrupts the third party Robin's record. This is direct pressure to include matched non-self records and preserve generic interference as a competing explanation.
- Useful native reciprocal computation remains unestablished in the collected cases. The new project cannot begin by assuming a working brain bridge or by treating source confusion as useful communication.

The self-header reversal and source-qualified answer redistribution further warn that clearer first-person wording does not necessarily repair attribution. They do not identify reactance or a defense of selfhood.

## Closest precedents and the actual novelty burden

The philosophical question of thought ownership through brain interfaces is already explicit in [Trimper et al. (2014)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroengineering/articles/10.3389/fneng.2014.00004/full). The new contribution cannot be having asked whether brains can share thoughts.

[Li et al. (2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.16717v3), especially §3.1.3, manipulate native conversational slots, including Assistant text not generated by the tested instance. They connect a role index to attribution and use of an erroneous intermediate step. We must exceed source-role recognition and trust effects.

[Fraile Navarro (2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01490), §§3–4, preserves conversational history while changing persona anchoring and distinguishes represented persona from enacted first-person identity. Thus “remembering a persona differs from being that persona” is already an empirical result. Our proposed increment would be separately established histories of actual commitments in two instances, consequential continuation, and a selective causal account beyond role/persona restoration. This increment is a research target, not a certified untouched niche.

[Chae et al. (2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.18091v3) already separate self/other labels from content source in evaluation. [Mirror-based VLM self-identification](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.08816v1) already studies causal bodily self-identification among multiple agents. [Endogenous Resistance to Activation Steering](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/mckenzie26a.html) already reports resistance to internal interventions. Neither adding a self label, identifying a causal contribution nor resisting an influence is sufficient novelty.

The original manuscript's generic slot account, binding/rebinding literature and cache-attack work remain serious alternatives. A new venue claim requires a result and mechanism that those accounts do not already explain. The bounded search supports a narrower possible contribution, not an exhaustive priority claim.

## Smallest scientific development that could change the investment

First establish a finite task where disconnected instances reliably continue their own earlier choices and partner information can usefully improve actual actions. Use several worlds that distinguish the projects' complete policies. Failure here is a missing task/channel prerequisite.

Then cross the historically established actor of a commitment with its later location and role label. Keep the set of commitment contents and current task authority comparable, while retaining observable evidence of who actually made each choice. Do not demand that a model distinguish hidden histories after all accessible evidence has been made identical. “Actual origin” is not a privileged physical process identifier the model should mysteriously recover.

Compare actual prior commitments, equivalent third-party records and explicitly assigned persona records; include text-mediated access. Examine whether continuation follows historical actor relations, a current source/role label, imported content, or ordinary instruction priority. Stable continuation by itself is not a special self mechanism: persona import can also be stable across tasks.

For a strong mechanism claim, isolate and intervene on source/actor binding and the action-relevant factual/control representations. The existing symbolic counterexample proves that distorted facts can mimic donor-goal policy in binary actions. No number of correct sibling reports repairs that ambiguity. A study without a valid mediator intervention must report behavioral continuation patterns rather than transferred intentions.

Exact task semantics, feasible independent interventions, commitment generation, primary contrast, episode population, sample size and precision target remain unspecified. They require PI adoption and a separate design. The central unresolved question is whether a usable manipulation can separate these accounts without creating a hidden-information test or merely changing instructions. If not, retain the question as motivation and do not brand a generic interference study as a new theory of self.

## Prepared English delivery summary

I would focus on this question: when two model instances share internal memory, what makes each continue its own unfinished commitments rather than the other's? Taking in another's experience and taking over another's project are different events. The sharp test is whether historical self-attribution and actual continuation can be independently changed: A can correctly report its earlier choice yet act along B's project on separate branches, or misattribute a choice yet retain A's project. Correct report branches alone do not establish what was represented during action. These are proposed possibilities, not established results.

Our access/attribution contrast, selective recovery and intact goal names with altered rule reports motivate the distinction. None establishes transferred intentions; Robin interference and weak useful communication are essential constraints. Prior work already studies role-based self-indexing, represented versus enacted persona, label effects and causal self-identification. The possible new contribution is a selective causal account of whose actual commitment history governs future actions when two independently established histories meet internally, beyond imported persona, factual distortion and ordinary record interference. The most compelling outcome would dissociate the two relations while preserving useful information and the relevant factual representations. It would show that these functional relations can be causally separated, not that two minds merge or that consciousness is measured. The question is worth developing; the decisive manipulation and a distinctive result are not yet established. No research execution follows from this recommendation.
