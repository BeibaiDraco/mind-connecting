## Candidate 1: develop a precise test of preserved alternatives

The useful question is not whether two models can improve an answer, nor whether a rich vector contains more information than a winner token. It is whether direct state access preserves the *decision-relevant relations among unchosen possibilities* well enough for another computation to respond appropriately to a contingency the sender never saw. This makes a concrete distinction between sharing a conclusion and sharing something that remains revisable.

The closest newly reopened sources materially narrow but do not close that distinction:

- [Beyond Final Accuracy](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01042), §§3.1–3.3, directly evaluates textual and latent revision from fixed initial trajectories, with a no-message extra-reasoning control. It explicitly measures recovery when both initial answers were wrong, as well as correction and preservation. Its inspected design does not cross residual-alternative identity with private late receiver evidence while fixing the sender's winner and uncertainty summary. Therefore neither latent revision nor a correct joint answer absent from both initial answers is new.
- [Hidden Messages](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.28905), §§3–4, uses task-trained adapters around frozen diffusion backbones. It demonstrates targeted content substitution, an actual query/code return chain, and timing-sensitive communication. These close generic causal-content, return-path and timing claims. They do not in the inspected tasks isolate competing unchosen alternatives under a common initial winner.
- [CodeTalkers](https://github.com/wpm/CodeTalkers/blob/main/README.md), main experiment, superposition test and next steps, reports tiny fact-transfer and blended-message demonstrations and explicitly proposes hypothesis-distribution relay. It is an intellectual precedent for the idea, not a completed controlled residual-alternative revision test. Native transport or avoiding trained adapters is not itself the missing scientific proposition.
- [Interlat](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.09149v5), §5.2 and Appendix F, was independently reopened after the coordinator's late lead. It compares top-k probability mass across latent steps and interprets sustained breadth as parallel alternatives; task performance and content perturbations supply additional evidence. This is a substantial conceptual and empirical precedent, not just a generic accuracy claim. Its inspected analysis does not interventionally identify which unchosen alternative a receiver uses after a private later observation. The proposed increment must be that behavioral specificity, not the first assertion that latent states support alternatives.
- [Confidence Laundering](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.20662v1), as independently verified in the reviewed field record, already motivates retained alternatives and measures uncertainty recoverability with fitted predictors. [Dense Latent Communication](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.13594v2) and [LatComm](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.26840), at the coordinator's recorded original-reading scopes, already support later questions about previously encoded private context. Neither generic uncertainty retention nor an unforeseen question should carry the novelty argument.

A minimal information argument makes the proposed contrast real. Consider stipulated donor posteriors over A/B/C of (0.6, 0.3, 0.1) and (0.6, 0.1, 0.3). They share the same winner, maximum probability and entropy. If later private evidence rules out A, the appropriate choices become B and C respectively, with conditional probabilities 0.75 versus 0.25. A message restricted to that common winner and scalar summaries cannot distinguish the two stipulated worlds; a representation preserving the alternatives can. This is a mathematical illustration of the task's information requirement, **not evidence about the model's posterior or native state**.

The planner should enumerate a full small family before investment: residual order crossed with late evidence, including branches where the original winner remains appropriate. Use at least enough late contingencies to rule out the policy “always return the runner-up.” Permute identities and balance wording/length. The sender never sees the late evidence. Export one designated state and reuse it across receiver branches; intervene on private donor evidence and check the predicted choice reversal. A receiver that changes with donor evidence but ignores the late observation has not demonstrated appropriate revision.

Two essential interpretation repairs belong in the plan. First, task-implied confidence matching is not empirical model-confidence matching; neither is a forced identical answer evidence of spontaneous agreement. State separately what is constructed, observed and conditioned upon, and retain the full prospectively defined population. Second, **matching the native result with an explicit alternatives report does not invalidate the scientific question.** Such a report is a positive control that transmits the very information being tested. Include both the donor's actual explicit alternatives/probability report and a stipulated complete-distribution oracle, alongside full raw evidence; their different information guarantees must remain visible. The stronger claim that native exchange is uniquely capable or superior to text would require justified resource matching and an advantage; it is not required to show usable native preservation. Conversely, beating answer-only text is insufficient for that stronger claim.

The primary result should be called *functional access to residual alternatives or their supporting evidence*. Recovering premises and recomputing is a valid positive behavior but does not prove transfer of an already-computed hypothesis distribution. The planner should retain an evidence-export versus post-inference-export comparison if it can be made fair; otherwise explicitly reserve the “continuing another's unfinished inference” mechanism claim. The null is informative only if ordinary native private-fact transfer and explicit complete-evidence competence work on independent materials. A failed carrier does not show that alternatives were absent.

First deciding work: finite symbolic worlds, all comparator information and compute budgets, and an explicit boundary between evidence recomputation and transferred inference. Later model feasibility should be small, prospective and native. Do not demand a positive outcome before considering the question worthwhile; do stop this implementation route if only explicit evidence text supports the task. A new prompt, task or carrier proposal remains subject to PI adoption.

## Candidate 2: a potentially striking access asymmetry, conditional on fairness

This is substantively different from the exhausted retained-capacity/forecasting family: its target is a second readout of a source's current information, followed by useful return, not what survives separation or whether a model predicts its capacity.

I reopened [Inside-Out](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.15299), §§3.1–4 and Appendix A.5. Its internal scoring function is a trained logistic classifier on question-plus-candidate-answer representations. External comparators include answer likelihood and prompted truth probabilities. Its candidate pool includes manually supplied gold answers missing from extensive sampling. Thus it strongly precedes hidden knowledge beyond generated answers, but the gold answer can be present when the scored representation is constructed. It does not show spontaneous recovery by a second untrained native reader or return-path rescue. This methodological distinction is consequential; merely substituting a second frozen model for a fitted probe would still need a scientific argument.

The candidate's remaining value is an intervention chain: randomized private episodic value changes the recipient's recovered answer, and the actual native return then changes the source's committed action appropriately, beyond matched donor retrieval policies. This would demonstrate useful readout-role asymmetry within a fixed model rather than a metaphysical privilege of another agent. Hidden Messages already establishes actual internal out-and-back computation; the new proposition must be the source-access deficit and its recovery, not round-trip communication itself.

The first check should be a table specifying, for each arm, accessible original tokens/cache, export positions, final query/options, instructions, extra tokens, sampling attempts and commitment stage. The donor must retain at least its original evidence and receive a comparably favorable query/readout instruction. Compare ordinary continuation, budget-matched self-check, report-only recipient, native recipient and a fresh self-copy with the same state-access operation. A copied instance receiving identical state and inputs is computationally the same comparator regardless of its name; no self/other advantage can remain after literal equality.

That last fact does not make the experiment pointless. It limits its interpretation to a fresh readout context unlocking accessible information. The question becomes strong only if it identifies a reproducible, useful access asymmetry under fair alternatives, rather than engineering a bad donor prompt. A distraction manipulation is permissible, but unequal context removal is a treatment to explain, not a nuisance to conceal. Compare unconditional paired success and loss; donor-failure subsets are descriptive and cannot be the only headline.

If the ledger reduces the result entirely to a known context-reset or supplied-answer verification advantage, defer flagship investment and preserve the narrower engineering opportunity. If a fair contrast survives, a bounded one-way check may precede native return. Textual answer return is a localization control, not a replacement for the requested internal return. This candidate has two channel dependencies; current runtime primitives and earlier failures do not establish either for the new task.

## Candidate 3: retain for consequential interpretation, not this shortlist

This question usefully asks whether the old source-report errors have a practical cost. It is not merely goal protection under new wording: the endpoint is evidence reliability and action quality. Its best prospective result would be appropriate reversal of reliance when source reliability reverses, while source reports remain systematically wrong in separately restored branches.

The original [Source memory for advertisements: The role of advertising message credibility](https://d-nb.info/1218459654/34), Experiments 1–2, source-attribution results and General Discussion, provides a close non-LLM precedent. Source tags affect initial credibility, later tag influence decreases, and source judgments show content-based reconstruction when memory is poor. The authors explicitly distinguish item recognition, source memory and guessing; their particular model needs identification restrictions. This is not the candidate's controlled preservation of reliability-sensitive *actions* under native misattribution, so it does not close that exact proposition. It does show why source reporting and evaluation cannot be treated as previously unconnected literatures.

The reviewed human confidence-sharing, BrainNet and cached-source-report work adds direct precedents for implicit weighting and source-report transplants. The unresolved contribution is their functional relationship under the specific connection. However, source errors in a sibling branch do not show that an action used a wrongly attributed thought. Nor does a correlation establish that source confusion caused bad reliance. With that stronger interpretation removed, the proposal presently changes the interpretation of this project's old result more than it opens a new account of two-model cognition.

Retain it as a bounded follow-up if the PI wants to evaluate practical consequence. For the current search's impact priority and limited review capacity, candidates 1 and 2 offer more distinct questions. The broader source-monitoring and metacognitive-dissociation literature was not exhaustively surveyed, so no novelty rejection is asserted.
