# Late original-source checks

October 26, 2026. Root inspected these sources after fresh question generation; they are supplied to independent screening and subsequent planning, not retrospectively counted as questioner inputs. No empirical absence claim follows from this bounded search. All descriptions concern inspected experiments, not their titles alone.

## Revision audit

[Beyond Final Accuracy](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01042), §§3.1–3.3 and Appendix B. Three independently generated answers yield six directed, single-hop revision events, reusing cached initial trajectories. The receiver retains its original question, reasoning and answer. Both-wrong recovery is explicitly measured alongside correction and preservation. No-message revision controls additional reasoning. StateBridge transforms up to 64 final generated states with a trajectory-fitted alignment; the LatentMAS adaptation rebuilds the full sender KV prefix and adds ten latent steps. These are message/interface combinations, not identical information budgets. This is a direct precedent against claiming that native communication-driven revision or recovery when neither initial answer was correct is new. It does not in the inspected design hold the winner and confidence fixed while intervening on the identity of residual alternatives and a private later observation.

## Diffusion-agent communication

[Hidden Messages](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.28905), §§3–4. Independent diffusion trajectories exchange four dense vectors through trained task-specific encoder/receiver adapters around frozen backbones. Wrong-example and targeted fact swaps test content dependence. A query/code task requires an actual out-and-back request and reply; distributed QASC compares recurrent with late-only exchange. Thus useful causal content transfer, timing comparisons and a working native reciprocal dependency chain already have direct precedents. Different architecture or our lack of training would not alone create a new scientific question. The inspected tasks do not manipulate unchosen alternative structure while holding the current winner fixed.

## Reusable question-independent packets

[LatComm](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.26840), §§1–3 and Figure 3. A context encoder processes a document without the eventual query; learned compressors create reusable packets. Frozen receiver backbones read through trained cross-attention readers and prompt-dependent gates. Reconstruction training precedes task training; compressors are fixed for the latter. This independently closes generic claims of answering unforeseen questions from an imported internal memory. The correct identifier is **2610.26840**. A secondary search result mis-associated the title with 2610.26834, whose original is a different linear-attention paper; the incorrect identifier is excluded.

## Small soft-token proof of concept

[CodeTalkers README](https://github.com/wpm/CodeTalkers/blob/main/README.md), main experiment, superposition test and proposed next steps. One frozen Qwen2.5-0.5B supplies separate sender/receiver contexts. Sender vectors are probability-weighted token embeddings. The author reports eight secret-fact trials and a separate elementwise blend of two messages, with three of eight blend queries correct versus zero with the wrong message; raw outputs are not checked into the repository. The README acknowledges that the untrained messages closely shadow text. It proposes future constraint-relay tasks involving distributions over hypotheses. This is a direct conceptual precedent for transmitting alternatives, but the reported blend test is not a controlled demonstration of using an unchosen alternative in later revision. No code was executed or downloaded for execution.

## Hidden factual knowledge

[Inside-Out](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.15299), §§2–4, Appendices A.4–A.6. The internal endpoint is a trained classifier ranking candidate answers from representations of question-plus-answer pairs. External comparators use token likelihood and prompted truth scores. The work also tests adding an unsampled gold answer to the candidate set. Train/test entity separation and input-feature baselines address probe memorization. Consequently it strongly precedes the broad claim that a model has accessible internal information missing from its generated answers. It does not establish that a second frozen LLM can retrieve source-private information from the first model's native window better than fair donor continuations, or that an actual native return improves the donor. It also does not justify treating an unsampled answer as a spontaneously computed, ready-to-transfer thought: the candidate answer is supplied when obtaining the scored representation.

## Interlat's evidence for alternatives

[Interlat](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.09149v5), current v5, §§5.1–5.2 and Appendices D–F. The paper explicitly proposes that latent communication preserves parallel hypotheses. Its evidence includes useful longer trajectories, task-conditioned payload ablations and top-k token-probability mass across latent reasoning steps, including a 32-step extension. This is a substantive conceptual and empirical precedent, not merely a generic accuracy paper. Broader token distributions and productive exploration do not, by themselves, establish the identity of an unchosen semantic alternative or its causal use by the recipient after a specific private observation. A new study should test that stronger implication directly, crediting the existing hypothesis. Full alternative reports and candidate-probability vectors are necessary comparators if claiming an advantage beyond ordinary uncertainty summaries. No claim that text inherently cannot convey alternatives is justified.
