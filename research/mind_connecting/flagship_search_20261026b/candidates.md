# Fresh questions about access between two internally connected models

October 26, 2026. Question generation completed. These are unranked candidates for independent screening, not accepted studies or claims of novelty. No model, target-effect analysis, git action or shared-file modification occurred.

The common scientific object is the **functional accessibility of a private computation to another instance**. A bridge may expose more than an answer without making that extra information usable. Conversely, an instance may use foreign information competently while describing its origin incorrectly. The three questions below distinguish the content accessible through a connection, who can use it, and whether useful evaluation requires correct ownership reports. They do not infer experience from functional access.

## 1. Can another model recover the possibility you were about to discard?

**Intuitive hook.** Two models can give the same answer with the same confidence while having considered different alternatives. Could connecting their internal states let a partner rescue the right alternative after new evidence overturns that shared answer?

**Exact question.** In two frozen Qwen instances, does the donor's native state preserve a *structured set of unchosen possibilities* that a recipient can use to choose an appropriate revised action, beyond the donor's committed answer and scalar uncertainty? The missing proposition concerns useful access to the identity and relations of alternatives, rather than classification of “uncertain,” transfer of a fact, or answering an unforeseen query alone.

**Why it matters.** A positive result would identify a consequential capability of direct access: another process can continue an unfinished inference rather than inherit only its winner or its confidence. This would change the design target for model communication from transmitting conclusions to preserving revisable reasoning. A negative result with useful ordinary fact transfer would establish an informative boundary: rich-state exchange can transmit the chosen content while failing to make the donor's unresolved hypothesis space operationally available.

**Proposed native test.** Construct small diagnostic worlds with three or four competing explanations. Different private evidence bundles make the same explanation the best current choice, with the same ideal confidence, but different alternatives the runner-up. After the donor has processed its evidence, the recipient privately receives a decisive observation excluding the common leading explanation. The donor never sees that observation. Import the same designated native window into recipient branches with different late observations. Score the recipient's *actual revised choice* against the joint evidence, including whether the choice follows the changed alternative structure rather than simply abandoning the donor's answer.

For example, evidence favors A in both donor worlds, but supports B over C in one and C over B in the other. The recipient alone learns that A is impossible. Correct B-versus-C behavior must depend on the donor's residual evidence. Use a full crossing of alternative structure and recipient exclusion, with permuted names and balanced evidence lengths. The experiment must not select donor histories post hoc because they yield a desirable recipient effect. Distinguish matched *task-implied* confidence from empirically identical model confidence; the former does not prove the latter.

Compare native windows with no transfer, irrelevant donor, the donor's actual answer, answer plus actual scalar confidence, a concise explicit alternatives report, and full evidence text as a competence ceiling. Count communication bytes and computation; dense tensors are not equal-budget messages just because they contain few token positions. A fixed visible suffix can exclude visible-word leakage, but forced identical tokens alter the inference process and must be reported as an intervention, not spontaneous agreement. If useful, contrast naturally answer-matched material separately without hiding the selection rate.

**Rival outcomes.** The recipient may use the alternative structure; merely follow the donor winner; respond to nonspecific uncertainty by deferring; or retrieve the raw evidence and recompute. That last outcome is useful but narrower: it demonstrates recoverable evidence, not transfer of a precomputed hypothesis space. To distinguish the latter, compare sender states before and after inference at matched exported content/length as far as feasible, and include direct evidence-transmission controls. This distinction is an optional explanatory extension, not a prerequisite to measuring the primary behavioral capability. Do not claim a unique representation from a successful task alone.

**Closest actual evidence.** [Confidence Laundering](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.20662v1), Appendices A.7–A.11, measures probe-based uncertainty recoverability; its Appendix B explicitly motivates distributions over alternatives. Thus the idea that a latent vector can retain discarded uncertainty is already articulated. The proposed addition would be native, action-relevant recovery of *which* alternative remains appropriate, not first discovery of latent doubt. [Dense Latent Communication](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.13594v2), §§2.2 and 4.1, already encodes private documents before a later receiver question and trains a two-memory interface. An unknown future question alone is therefore no gap. [Workspace functional swaps](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html), flexible-generalization section, already show that imported concept coordinates can serve several functions; that is not yet this structured revision comparison.

**What makes it trivial or closes it.** A simple one-fact lookup with no alternative competition; superiority only to answer-only messages while an equally concise alternatives report works as well; or a prior native study already demonstrating recipient-contingent revision from answer/confidence-matched donor alternatives. Such outcomes could leave a useful engineering result while removing the proposed conceptual advance.

**First deciding check.** Before a pilot, independently inspect direct latent reasoning and uncertainty papers for exactly this structured-revision endpoint, and symbolically enumerate the small worlds to verify that the answer/scalar summaries are insufficient while the alternative structure is sufficient. The first authorized model check would then test whether the frozen pair can use even a two-alternative donor state on held-out material. No multi-round reciprocity is required for that diagnostic. Do not proceed to a large “shared thinking” study if only explicit evidence text works.

**Three-source origin.** The field's confidence-versus-content distinction opens the question. Pattern [P1](../three_lines_20261025/knowledge/patterns/question-patterns.md) contributes the distinction between retaining information and depending on another operation. The [dataset card](../three_lines_20261025/knowledge/dataset/card.md) supplies the crucial constraint that a native window is contextualized state, not purified uncertainty, and that useful recipient computation remains unestablished. This is a new candidate about revision, not the previous acquisition-action-coordinate proposal.

## 2. Can another instance use what your own continuation cannot retrieve?

**Intuitive hook.** If a model contains information it cannot bring into its answer, can another copy read that information through a direct connection and return it in usable form?

**Exact question.** Is there a reproducible region in which source-private information is more usefully accessible to a second frozen instance through the donor's native state than to matched continuations of the donor itself, and can returning that recovered information rescue the donor's subsequent action? This is a question about the asymmetry of functional access, not a claim that another subject knows one's mind better.

**Why it matters.** The usual picture treats the donor's answer as the best accessible expression of its current knowledge. A positive result would show a concrete route by which a connected pair can unlock a locally inaccessible intermediate result, with no new external evidence or learned decoder. The potentially striking result is a *causal rescue*: donor private evidence → recipient recovery → corrected donor action. A null after strong retrieval controls would constrain the premise that a second native instance naturally acts as an interpreter of its partner's otherwise hidden computation.

**Proposed native test.** Use private episodic information with known random ground truth, so the recipient cannot answer from its shared pretrained weights. Introduce a controlled retrieval difficulty such as a distractor or competing binding after encoding. From the same donor state, compare: direct continuation; a matched-budget self-check; a fresh textual-reader baseline supplied the donor's actual report; and a fresh recipient with only the native window. Give all readout policies the same final query and response options. If the recipient recovers useful information, return that answer through a predeclared native return stage and measure an actual donor action. Include the corresponding explicit answer return to determine whether failure lies in retrieval or the return channel.

Fairness is decisive. Give the donor the same readout instruction, extra tokens and number of attempts afforded to the recipient. Compare a fresh-context self-copy with equivalent state access: computational role and context can matter, but relabeling that copy “another agent” creates no further effect. An ordinary context-reset explanation is a scientifically valid answer and must be named. Intervene on the donor's encoded private value and verify corresponding recipient changes; irrelevant-state controls exclude generic extra computation. Evaluate a prospectively fixed episode population. Report recovery on donor failures as a conditional description alongside unconditional paired outcomes, not as a selected independent success rate.

**Closest evidence and uncertainty.** [Inside-Out: Hidden Factual Knowledge in LLMs](https://research.google/pubs/inside-out-hidden-factual-knowledge-in-llms-2/) establishes a gap between internal answer ranking and external generation in its published abstract; the full original methods were not recovered in this bounded pass. [Discovering Latent Knowledge Without Supervision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.03827) uses a fitted consistency-based direction to extract information. These occupy the broad “knows more than it says” claim. The [introspection notes](../three_lines_20261025/knowledge/introspection_sources.md) also show why apparent privileged access requires appropriate outside-observer controls. Dense Latent Communication already studies receiver use of source-private state, so private information transfer alone adds nothing. The missing, still-to-be-screened conjunction is native access exceeding matched donor retrieval and causing a return-path rescue.

**Rival outcomes.** Recipient success may reflect access to information hidden from the particular donor report; a better readout context; extra compute; or a general second-pass benefit. Only the first two survive controlled resource and information comparisons, and neither establishes a metaphysical self/other privilege. If the same self-copy arrangement works, describe a reusable readout-role operation, not an irreducible two-person property. If the recipient is correct only where donor self-check is also correct, there is no demonstrated access asymmetry.

**What makes it trivial or closes it.** Comparing a single rushed donor answer against an extensively prompted receiver; importing full evidence while withholding it from the donor comparator; selecting the few cases where the recipient happens to be right; or finding that ordinary textual self-check fully accounts for the benefit. A prior native study with matched donor/receiver retrieval and actual return rescue would close the proposed contribution.

**First deciding check.** Read the full Inside-Out methods and the closest native cache-reading work before any execution. Then specify a fully symmetric resource ledger and information ledger. If a fair comparator cannot be written, abandon the “another can access what I cannot” interpretation before collecting data. The smallest later pilot is one-way native recovery on a tiny fixed family; the reciprocal rescue is warranted only if that prerequisite succeeds. This candidate has a larger feasibility gap than a single transfer comparison because the existing project has no reliable complete native return chain.

**Three-source origin.** The original vision's question about a changed epistemic relation supplies the stake; latent-knowledge literature supplies the specific tension. [P1](../three_lines_20261025/knowledge/patterns/question-patterns.md) asks which operation must remain external, and [P2](../three_lines_20261025/knowledge/patterns/question-patterns.md) requires an actual useful return path rather than bidirectional wiring. The dataset's snapshots and committed actions make the causal comparison imaginable while its failed native returns prevent assuming feasibility. This does not reuse the previous partner-swap or retained-capacity argument.

## 3. Can you trust the right thought while being wrong about whose thought it is?

**Intuitive hook.** Direct connection might scramble “mine” and “yours” without destroying the ability to judge which evidence deserves belief. Are ownership reports necessary for competent reliance, or can useful evaluation survive their failure?

**Exact question.** When two internally connected instances have conflicting, independently controlled evidence, can the recipient adjust actual reliance to the donor evidence's reliability while its source reports systematically misattribute that evidence? The target is a reproducible dissociation between *appropriate evidential use* and *explicit historical-source attribution*, across the same experimental contrasts. It is not the generic existence of report/action disagreement.

**Why it matters.** This would change the interpretation of the project's strongest old finding. Misattribution could mark a loss of useful evidential organization, or only a limitation of explicit source reporting. Positive reliability-sensitive use despite bad ownership reports would supply a counterintuitive functional boundary: losing a verbal boundary between sources need not mean losing the ability to combine their evidence rationally. Conversely, if source confusion predicts inappropriate dependence under controlled reliability changes, the practical cost of the old attribution effect becomes observable.

**Proposed native test.** Give each instance an independent noisy observation of a known hidden state, with reliability manipulated by the generating process and communicated in a simple, independently comprehensible contract. Cross which observation favors which decision and which side is more reliable. Use a native donor-state window and score recipient commitments against the Bayes-optimal policy for this finite world, with robust non-Bayesian policy comparisons if numerical competence fails. Include informative conflict trials where the appropriate decision reverses as reliability reverses while signal content stays fixed. Measure historical-source reports in separately restored sibling branches and retain complete response distributions.

An especially useful comparison would preserve source-report error while reliability-sensitive decisions reverse appropriately. This is an across-branch functional dissociation, **not proof that the actual action was computed from a mistaken attribution**. Source reports can be inaccurate readouts of intact internal binding. Establishing that stronger mediator claim would require additional causal intervention and is not necessary for the bounded question. The design must not discard source-correct cases or select the bridge strength to maximize ownership errors; use independently established access/format criteria, then estimate both endpoints on fresh episodes.

**Closest evidence.** [Bahrami et al.](https://pure.au.dk/ws/files/51720586/BahramiSci2010.pdf) and [haptic confidence sharing](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-80041-6) already establish reliability-dependent collective benefits and costs, including implicit communication. BrainNet already shows differential reliance on a more reliable sender. [Context or Knowledge](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.32109v1) directly transplants cached source reports under fixed answer text. Thus neither implicit trust nor source-report transplantation is new. The missing proposition is whether their functional relationship remains intact or breaks when rich internal access induces misattribution. Its value depends on a convincing contrast to report/action and source-monitoring precedents, not on merely crossing two existing endpoints.

**Rival outcomes.** Appropriate reliability weighting and inaccurate source reports can coexist; both can fail; reports can remain accurate while reliance fails; or donor salience can mimic reliability weighting. Crossed content/reliability, self/other reversal and fixed-gain controls distinguish several of these patterns. Private uncertainty strength is not automatically reliability, and a confident donor is not automatically correct. Ground-truth generating reliability and actual decision utility must remain separate from verbal confidence.

**What makes it trivial or closes it.** Merely reproducing confidence-weighted integration with a new carrier, reporting a correlation between two noisy measures, or assuming sibling-branch source mistakes caused actions. A previous causal study already showing preserved evidence-sensitive control under source misattribution would sharply reduce the contribution. The strongest concern is that this may remain a useful interpretation test for the old paper rather than a sufficiently new flagship; screening should evaluate that concern explicitly.

**First deciding check.** Search the original source-monitoring and collective metacognition literature for preserved reliability weighting under attribution errors, including non-LLM equivalents. Then check a finite decision table for content-versus-reliability separability and whether the same frozen model can solve the explicit-evidence version without probability-calculation failure. A later native pilot needs only one-way use; no reliable reciprocal chain is presupposed.

**Three-source origin.** The field refresh supplies positive reliability-sensitive collaboration rather than only interference. The dataset's established access/attribution dissociation gives a real unexplained practical consequence, while its sibling-branch warning limits interpretation. [P1](../three_lines_20261025/knowledge/patterns/question-patterns.md) contributes the move from a verbal boundary to the operations that boundary appears to support. Unlike the previous goal-preservation proposal, neither local-goal protection nor goal substitution is the target.
