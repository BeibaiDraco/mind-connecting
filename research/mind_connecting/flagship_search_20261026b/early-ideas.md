# Coordinator early ideas, withheld from the questioner

October 26, 2026. These are rough openings for later independent screening, not recommended questions or established gaps. No target outcomes were analyzed.

1. **Internal round-trip and epistemic ancestry.** Can a recipient recognize its own previously exported evidence after another instance processes and returns it, and avoid treating the return as independent confirmation? This connects memory source to action-relevant confidence, with causal ancestry randomized separately from the returned conclusion. Strong threat: CAMA and ECMR already study correlated evidence and provenance; native transport alone is insufficient. A new comparison would have to establish an important failure or functional separation not already covered, and behavior cannot depend on inaccessible causal history when all available states are identical.
2. **Transmission before commitment.** Can a donor's internal state preserve alternatives that were not chosen in its output, allowing a receiver with newly revealed private information to select a different valid continuation? This connects rich-state exchange to sharing unfinished thought instead of a result. Strong threat: Interlat and latent reasoning already invoke alternative paths; ordinary KV context can retain all premises. Need an actual original-method comparison, a recipient-side intervention and usefulness beyond a generic bandwidth demonstration. No new idea is established by naming unspoken thoughts.
3. **Epistemic state versus proposition.** Can a frozen receiver use the donor's uncertainty about a particular fact as a distinct input, and retain or lose its correct attachment when facts are internally shared? A newly found Confidence Laundering source may distinguish decodability from native downstream control. Human confidence-sharing and ordinary source reliability are close precedents. Do not call every confidence effect self-boundary transfer, and avoid reducing the project to another source-binding error without a stronger consequential question.

The screener should compare these with the fresh questioner's candidates and all existing exhausted arguments. They are deliberately absent from the fixed questioner packet. A new user-requested search does not erase earlier literature or revive an exhausted argument through another name.

4. **Access to another perspective versus retaining a distinction between perspectives.** Does direct access help predict what a partner will do while interfering with the receiver's own use of true world information? The intuitive hook is whether access to a mind makes perspective taking unnecessary, or whether it makes perspective separation more necessary. A native false-belief/action setup could require both correct partner prediction and correct own action, under independently manipulated world truth and donor observation history. Closest threats: standard ToM tasks; causal role/binding work; LACO ego control; the already exhausted broad goal-preservation family. This is not automatically distinct from those arguments. Search lead arXiv2606.13594 explicitly calls heterogeneous latent transfer mind reading, but its abstract describes context-aware/unaware benchmark transfer, not the proposed dual-perspective test; original methods must decide. Candidate must survive comparison before any promotion.
