## Deciding originals and implications

### Uncertainty carriers

[Shi et al., Confidence Laundering, arXiv:2606.20662v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.20662v1), Appendix A.5–A.11, explicitly constructs labels from semantic entropy over twenty tool-mediated answer trajectories. A.9 calls the downstream component an uncertainty predictor; A.11 defines AUROC and binary classification accuracy as uncertainty recoverability. Hidden-state probes and alternative handoff inputs therefore establish an information-carrier comparison. They do not, in the inspected contract, establish that a second frozen language model chooses better verification or retry actions, or that reciprocal coupling improves outcomes. The refresh correctly resists promoting broad recovery language into that result. Conversely, a new proposal cannot claim the first latent uncertainty carrier merely because its receiver differs. A consequential unresolved comparison could concern appropriate receiver use under controlled evidence reliability; its scientific value needs its own argument, beyond implementing a classifier endpoint natively. Semantic entropy must remain a behavioral instability proxy rather than ground-truth correctness.

### Cached source reports

[Afendulev et al., Context or Knowledge, arXiv:2610.32109v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.32109v1), §§2–4 and discussion, verifies the deciding comparison. Runs share question and answer strings but differ in document history; transplanting Q+A cache while retaining the recipient document makes reports follow the donor. The controlled-task table reports complete donor-following among eligible runs; selected DynamicQA paragraphs show 97.2–100%. Eligibility conditions and fixed source-question wording limit generalization. Document edits after answering do not change controlled-task reports, although replacement documents remain readable. The paper explicitly leaves evidence availability versus actual use unresolved. Consequently, a fixed-answer cache-induced report shift is already directly studied. Historical ownership between separately acting instances, useful recipient computation and source-report reliability require additional comparisons. The refresh's description is accurate and appropriately conditional.

### Flexible semantic use

[Gurnee et al., Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html), flexible-generalization experiment and Figure 68 appendix, uses four argument categories, four functions per category and 192 source-target swap trials. Target-appropriate top-one outcomes vary sharply: 42/48 country cases versus 0/48 number-relation cases. These are functional interventions, not mere decoding demonstrations. The refresh correctly treats this as a stronger criterion and a close precedent while retaining heterogeneity. It does not justify assuming broad semantic portability through arbitrary K/V windows, nor a workspace spanning two models. Candidate generation may ask about flexible recipient use, but should specify which new organizational relation is missing from the single-model intervention rather than count additional tasks as sufficient novelty. I did not audit all models, other experimental sections or training procedures.

### Human confidence and joint decisions

[Bahrami et al., Optimally Interacting Minds](https://pure.au.dk/ws/files/51720586/BahramiSci2010.pdf), original paper, distinguishes group benefit for similar-sensitivity observers from group cost when sensitivities differ. Free communication remains beneficial without outcome feedback. Its direct-signal-sharing account is a statistical model, not an actual brain-to-brain channel. The refresh's useful lesson is to compare predicted response patterns across reliability asymmetry rather than only average group accuracy; it supplies no established prediction for native LLM transfer without a justified observation model. The PDF includes material from the preceding Science article; the relevant paper starts on PDF page 2. Supplementary methods were not independently inspected here.

[Pezzulo et al., Haptic communication](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-80041-6), setup, Figure 2 and Results, confirms separately made initial choices, coupled-handle resolution of disagreement, and better/worse joint performance depending on relative sensory sensitivity. Weighted-confidence-sharing predictions fit that pattern; movement variables predict leadership. This supports an implicit communication account, without isolating a unique transmitted confidence representation. The refresh appropriately retains this inferential boundary and identifies the verbal-speed comparison as cross-study. A native study should distinguish sensitivity to evidential support from generalized donor dominance or motor-style commitment strength.

### Brainet control and circulating memory

[Ramakrishnan et al., Computing Arm Movements with a Monkey Brainet](https://www.nature.com/articles/srep10767), experimental setup and dyad/triad methods, specifies fitted Kalman decoders, averaging or assigned axes, and visual feedback. The triad has different two-dimensional views of one three-dimensional avatar, with each shared axis averaged across two monkeys. This supports distributed causal control through an engineered common object; it is not rich-state transplantation between monkeys. The refresh correctly credits the decoder and task architecture. “Common object” should not be shortened to identical visual input for every animal: triad views differ.

[Pais-Vieira et al., Building an organic computing device](https://www.nature.com/articles/srep11869), storage Results, Figure 5 and memory methods, confirms a three-rat four-trial relay returning information to the first rat. Success requires correct decoding throughout the chain and is compared with no-stimulation and single-rat four-trial controls. Methods for parallel/sequential processing place a rule-based transformation in the external controller. These are positive precedents for circulation and hybrid distributed storage, not persistent individual autobiographical memory or a computation arising entirely inside the brains. The refresh preserves that scope. The linked corrigendum and precise stimulation mappings were not independently checked; no conclusion here depends on them.
