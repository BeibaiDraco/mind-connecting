# Proposed user-facing assessment

I recommend three concrete opportunities: the first as conceptual priority, the second as a compact initial diagnostic, and the third as a higher-risk direction. These are candidates for a flagship, not established gaps or findings. The common question is what each participant can do independently after internal information is shared.

**1. Does a shared past make participants interchangeable or dependent on each other?** Gao already forms frozen-model pairs and swaps experienced partners. One high-coupling condition rises from 4.33 to 6.53 messages per progress unit, with smaller progress loss. The proposed advance randomizes previous work organization while keeping the computation fixed, then tests all histories on the same fresh tasks. Formation A–A–B–B versus A–B–A–B changes handoffs; later original-versus-swapped pairs reveal whether the past changes current replaceability. Intervening on a specific anticipated handoff and testing a portable scheduling remedy could explain the effect. Opposite outcomes matter: interleaving could create private dependencies or standardize expectations. This concerns imposed-partition handoff organization, not proof of emergent identity. The first implementable study uses text packets and bounded notebooks with transcript/cache resets. A native-cache extension is separately unvalidated; useful native reciprocity is still missing.

**2. Can sharing help use evidence while reducing the ability to obtain different evidence?** The broad tradeoff is known. Bernstein and colleagues randomize human triads to constant, intermittent or absent peer-solution exposure over 17 rounds of a 25-city task, with triads finding an optimal solution in 48.3% versus 33.3% of trials under intermittent versus constant access; LLM studies cover diversity collapse and deficient peer exploration. The sharper comparison observes new measurements and distinguishes copied action labels from copied information targets. A and B seek the same two-bit code. A gets a private availability cue and commits its probe choice; early state is captured before any measurement value arrives. B then chooses. Reversing B's public left/right instrument map makes identical buttons complementary in one condition and redundant in the other. Independently gate early A-to-B and later B-to-A sharing, hiding late assignment during acquisition. This separates producing evidence from combining it. A causal channel/timing-dependent failure of complementary inquiry would be valuable even if ordinary copying explains it. The crossover distinguishes behavioral response maps, not a literal internal copying operation; correlated answers alone would not suffice.

**3. After a joint solution, who can solve a new problem alone?** Task vectors already transfer useful task state to a new query without source demonstrations; ExpeL and CLIN reuse derived experience without actor-weight updates. The proposal is therefore not generic retention. Give both agents complete component mappings and identical actual final outputs, but randomize who integrates and who checks. After cutting, test both on unused practice inputs present in the mappings. Ask whether assigned roles affect local portability and whether continued support masks the asymmetry. This initially estimates a role bundle, including instructions and different work histories. Cache restoration is not proof of an abstract rule; stronger role-cue controls remain unresolved. This is closest to whose future the joint work supports but has the weakest current flagship case.

Old experiments motivate these distinctions without confirming the predictions. Fixed donor states can change attribution without responsiveness; mean-access matching is incomplete; post-cut readout recovery is not restored identity; correct goal reports do not establish the rule used in actions. The native platform has not shown a reliable complete useful reciprocal chain. Select the question and its decisive comparison rather than require every old result in the new paper.

If choosing one paper-level bet, prioritize formation and replaceability, conditional on useful history dependence. For the smallest informative diagnostic, prioritize the instrument-map/stage comparison. Keep role-dependent portability exploratory. A strong paper needs a causal explanation, a serious rival distinguished and fresh-task replication; the philosophical motivation alone cannot establish novelty or guarantee conference acceptance.
