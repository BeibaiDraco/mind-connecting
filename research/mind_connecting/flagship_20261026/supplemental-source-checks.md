# Supplemental primary-source checks

Read on October 26, 2026. These checks supplement the question-specific evidence tables, not a systematic review. Search leads included combinations of latent communication, memory/retention, cognitive offloading, collective causal integration, and brain-to-brain agency/ownership. Unread search hits were not used to establish novelty or mechanism. Original HTML sections were read; experiments and code were not reproduced.

## Emergent Coordination, revised version

[Riedl, v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.05174v4), abstract, Methods, Results and Limitations. Ten agents propose numbers whose sum should hit a target, receiving only group-level high/low feedback. Main GPT-4.1 study: 200 groups per prompt condition, 600 groups total; plain, persona, and persona plus consideration of others. Temporal information decompositions and shuffled nulls characterize differentiation and complementarity. Overall success does not significantly differ across these main conditions. The reported indirect performance effect has p=.053; the manuscript recognizes difficulty connecting synergy measures to performance. Other models provide robustness checks.

This is strong prior coverage of prompt-induced collective differentiation. It does not randomize a native channel, formation topology or the location of an acquired computation. Predictive synergy and prompt randomization do not alone identify a causal mechanism of interpersonal integration. The authors explicitly frame the result as conditional on a multi-agent task, not team superiority over a solo model. The present assessment uses v4; the previous dossier's v3 reference is preserved as historical provenance.

## Social Catalysts

[Hu et al., v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.02598v1), Methods, Phase 1 and Phase 2 results. A 3-model × 3-anchor-proportion × 2-visibility × 2-horizon design has three replications per cell: 108 ten-agent games of ten rounds. Fixed anchors contribute all their wealth. Round 11 changes to a one-shot game with new strangers and no anchors, retaining only the final belief summary. Previous anchor proportion has no significant main transfer effect; GPT-4.1 has a reported Public × 20%-anchor exception.

This directly precedes a broad question about persistence after social influence. It does not isolate removal of connection: partners, horizon, information and retained history change together. Nonsignificant transfer effects do not establish equivalence or absence of internalization. Subtracting a verbal expectation from an action and analyzing vocabulary do not independently identify moral preference, emotion or cognitive offloading. We use the behavioral design and its limits, not those stronger interpretations. Independent games, rather than 972 non-anchor agents, are the natural replication units.

## Other leads and reading depth

[CoThinker v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.06843v3) was initially inspected at abstract, method outline and appendix structure, then passed to the interdependence reader for the relevant methods. Its transactive-memory terminology alone is not evidence of native interpersonal dependence. MACIE, embodiment/episodic-memory papers and education-specific offloading results appeared in search; no deciding scientific claim here relies on their unread contents.

[Hollingshead (2001)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11761309/) was located through its primary indexed abstract. It describes a 2×4 experiment crossing expectations of similar/different partner expertise with four recall incentives. Differentiated versus integrated transactive-memory structures vary with that combination. Thus a broad claim that cognitive interdependence causes memory organization would not be new. Original full Methods and sample size remain unverified in this pass; an available preview is not a completed close reading. Constant-computation-graph formation history followed by common-task replacement tests is a narrower proposed contrast, whose novelty is still conditional.
