# First decision: is post-separation success more than contextual reuse?

October 26, 2026. **Disposition: conditional causal opportunity; broad new-learning and identity claims remain unsupported.** This is the fourth queued candidate, linked to the earlier capacity/forecasting argument. The final remaining substantive repair is used below, with independent review completed and stronger claims deferred; the present development is not a reset or an adopted experiment.

The question was: after two frozen instances combine complementary evidence, can each use the resulting relation on new cases after separation? An answer would tell us whether useful support has become available in each local history or still requires partner access. That is valuable for deciding what a connection leaves behind, but it does not by itself establish a new kind of learning, experience or identity.

The first affordable investment-changing check was the closest original methods, not another proposed metric. [Source evidence](source-evidence.md) shows that experience-derived rules applied to new tasks without weight changes already have direct precedents; activation-based task transfer without direct access to source demonstrations also does. Therefore “fresh case + frozen weights + partner gone” fails as a distinctive flagship claim. LatentMAS also makes clear that transferring a cache transfers computed intermediates as well as premises. Calling the remaining cache “the agent itself” would hide the causal question in a storage convention.

The second check is an exact finite counterexample in [logicalcheck.py](logicalcheck.py). A private affine rule modulo five is determined jointly by two observations, neither alone sufficient. New queries exclude both observed inputs. Both a retained rule and the retained two premises with recomputation produce exactly the same fresh answers in every case. New-case accuracy cannot distinguish them. In contrast, saving one previously generated answer cannot solve the whole challenge family. This establishes only logical distinctions, not Qwen competence or a mechanism.

The first draft stopped at state sufficiency too early. The coordinator requested a concrete causal thesis rather than treating an ordinary memory mechanism as an automatic disqualifier. The final refinement asks: **does allocation of integration work determine which participant can independently reuse the jointly available information after separation, even when both received the same complete premises and final outputs?** Randomize the integrating role, then measure each participant's fresh applications after cutting. Role-dependent asymmetry would explain why successful team work need not guarantee a usable handoff to each member. Symmetric portability would show that competence travels with shared evidence regardless of this work allocation. Either is more specific than merely observing retention.

Ordinary text/context mechanisms remain admissible explanations. Removal/restoration and full-text re-prefill locate what survives and whether it is sufficient; they need not reveal a novel storage primitive. The possible contribution is a causal account linking work allocation to later individual competence, with a concrete handoff implication. It remains conditional because this comparison is unmeasured, useful native collaboration is unestablished, and generated-versus-observed-example work was not fully audited. No priority or publication guarantee follows.

The plan therefore provides an interpretable diagnostic with clear stopping conditions. It does not promote the missing experiment cell into a research gap. Existing START/NOW recovery motivates separating readout changes from retained task usefulness; it supplies no evidence that a new relation was acquired. Old native reciprocal usefulness remains unestablished. No forecast, subjective report or separate sibling answer is treated as the mediator of action.

The [independent review](review.md) conditionally supports the total integration/checking-role opportunity and defers a distinctive flagship or acquired-representation claim. Coordinator precision corrections clarify paired units, valid held-out inputs, restoration versus sufficiency, and own-transcript versus common-transcript re-prefill. Historical role instructions remain a rival; stronger cue-control choices are unresolved. This is narrowing and specification after the final substantive repair, not a new argument or another repair allowance.
