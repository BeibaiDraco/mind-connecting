## Concrete question and minimal setting

Does the participant assigned to integrate complementary information retain more independently usable competence than its partner, even after both receive complete premises and identical final outputs? If so, which retained state makes the difference?

The thesis is that equal informational exposure and successful joint answers may leave unequal independent competence because work allocation creates different usable representations. The competitor is that shared evidence supports equal fresh use, with apparent asymmetries explained by missing facts, output copying, interruption or computation budget. This concerns functional handoff after collaboration, not experience transfer or personal continuity.

Let A initially hold a small arbitrary symbol mapping f and B a second mapping g. The task is composition g(f(x)). During a fixed exchange, **both receive every entry of both mappings**, not only the final answer. Randomize whether A or B then integrates the mappings and produces answers for prescribed practice inputs. The other performs a fixed checking task with the same allowed local steps. Both receive the identical actual generated final-answer record, including mistakes. After separation, reveal unused input symbols and score each participant's composed output. Counterbalance initial mapping ownership, integrating role and labels. No main episode is selected for successful work; report joint/practice performance by role arm. Unequal practice performance limits a claim about equally successful histories.

This preserves semantic premise access, not identical complete states: processing history is the treatment. Checking is part of the role bundle, not a neutral operation; the initial contrast estimates that allocation's total effect. Use identical neutral test instructions for both sides, while acknowledging that earlier role instructions persist. Reversing checking does not isolate historical competence from current role cues. The stronger interpretation remains deferred pending the history-by-current-cue and cue-only comparisons specified in the [independent review](review.md); these unresolved controls are not silently adopted here. Start with an ordinary full-text communication comparator and verify the all-premise delivery schedule. Separate fact-report probes are checks, not action mediators.

Here, unused inputs are withheld from practice outputs but present in both complete mapping tables. Before any collection, the generator must verify that each component mapping matters and exclude structurally degenerate cases without consulting model outcomes. A separate logical example draws f(x)=u·x+v mod 5. A receives f(0), B receives f(1), and both know the family. Either observation alone leaves five possible functions. After exposure reveal x=2, 3 or 4. This checks an inferential limit, not the proposed role effect or Qwen competence: saved-rule application and retained-premise recomputation are exactly equivalent. One saved answer alone is insufficient, but an answer plus an original premise can identify the affine rule. New-case success therefore does not automatically exclude answer retention as part of a sufficient context.

Do not condition the main sample on successful collaboration. Development first establishes that the chosen finite channel/task combination can use both private observations; confirmation retains the complete prespecified episode population. A one-way route can supply the necessary information, so this design does not by itself require or demonstrate reciprocity.

## Contrasts and their interpretations

At a declared end-of-exposure state, restore paired branches before the unseen query. Keep the query schedule and available inference budget fixed. Cross integrating role with continued/cut conditions. In a prespecified mechanism subset, cross retention of original-premise positions with retention of later work/answer positions (2×2), with position/size-matched sham removals and exact restoration. These are cache-position interventions, not pure fact/rule interventions; later positions can encode premises.

| Branch or control | What it answers; what it does not |
|---|---|
| Continue the connection versus cut future incoming states, preserving recipient history/cache | Total benefit of continued access. Cut success is retained-state sufficiency, not proof of abstraction. No new private facts arrive after the cut in this primary comparison. |
| No exposure, with matched local work opportunity | Total benefit remaining from the exposure history. Same token count cannot guarantee identical effective computation; report this limit. |
| Fresh recipient with both original premises in text | Whether ordinary recomputation is sufficient at the declared budget. Similar behavior defeats a need for a special acquired operation. |
| Fresh recipient with explicit correct rule | Application ceiling; separates task misunderstanding from failure to derive/access the rule. This is an oracle, not a communication baseline. |
| Actual generated text from the partner at the same schedule | Ordinary contextual communication comparator, retaining mistakes. Equal token count is not equal information. |
| Remove and restore a prespecified retained segment; compare matched unrelated removal and wrong-rule segment replacement | Causal contribution and reversibility in the specified context. Sufficiency requires insertion into a specified recipient background, which is not established by restoration alone. Cache positions mix premises, computations and context. |
| Re-prefill exactly the recipient's visible transcript with foreign cache absent | Sufficiency of visible text. Difference from original cache can reflect any nontextual computational history, not uniquely a stored insight. |

A mechanism account should explain content-specific swap behavior, restoration of useful performance and the retrieval/recomputation controls together. If a simple contextual account predicts the full role-dependent portability pattern, that is an informative explanation. Selective state interventions establish a causally useful component under those interventions, not an experience-transfer mechanism. Historical origin cannot independently change deterministic output when complete current state and future inputs are identical.

## Quantity, population and missing choices

The stated within-world contrast requires paired role-assignment branches from the same pre-exposure world, with counterbalanced execution order and labels; one randomized assignment per world would instead require a between-world estimator. For the paired sketch, average A's cut performance difference when A versus B integrates and B's corresponding difference when B versus A integrates. This estimates the assigned role bundle's effect. Average queries and sides within the independent mapping/world draw; several episodes sharing that draw form one cluster. The role-by-cut interaction asks whether continuation masks the role effect. Report practice, cut, continued and no-exposure performance without selecting successful histories. Missing execution is not zero. No N, interval threshold, equivalence margin or multiplicity rule is adopted here.

Rule/world draws, token labels, held-out applications, source positions and removals must be separated from interface selection. Use independent development episodes and one fixed tested comparison family before confirmation. Validated action scoring is required; sibling reports about the rule do not establish what mediated the action. Source attribution and forecasts are optional secondary questions with separate definitions and the inherited repair allowance.

## Rival results and stopping conditions

- Only the original answer repeats: retained answer, failed generalization.
- Both roles transfer equally after cutting: symmetric portability. Adequate precision and valid controls would limit the proposed role dependence, not show that interaction had no effect.
- Fresh applications work after cutting, and full-premise/text controls reproduce them: ordinary contextual reuse is sufficient. Re-prefilling each side's own exact transcript tests what that visible transcript can support, subject to a validated numerical contract; different transcripts still contain different visible roles/work. Giving both sides one identical transcript erases several treatments together. Loss of asymmetry under the latter is not selective evidence for a hidden representation.
- The integration assignment benefits either side after role reversal, despite prescribed equal premise/output exposure, and a selective content-specific removal/restoration explains the gap: supports work-allocation-dependent portability. Confirm across fresh mappings and a second composition family.
- Cut fails, continuation helps, and reconnection rescues: continued support is useful; retrieval, additional computation and disruption remain explanations until separated.
- A content-specific retained-state manipulation changes new applications while matched disruption does not: a candidate mechanism worth a renewed closest-method check; a single cache ablation is insufficient.
- Both full-information and explicit-rule controls fail: task not viable for this model, not evidence that joint knowledge cannot persist.
- Native sharing is unusable: stop at channel feasibility. Do not relabel source confusion as acquired competence.
