## Verdict

**Conditionally support delivery as a concrete causal opportunity. Defer a distinctive flagship or identified representation-mechanism claim.** The defensible question is whether assigning integration rather than checking changes later independent composition when both sides received the complete premises and actual final outputs. This is appreciably better specified than “knowledge survives disconnection.” A successful answer could inform how collaboration should hand work back to individual participants, even if ordinary context is the explanation. A native carrier is not itself the contribution.

The first role contrast identifies the total effect of a treatment bundle: different instructions, generated intermediate content, attention history, checking operations and effective computation. Equal facts, final outputs and allowed steps do not make those factors equal. The plan acknowledges much of this correctly. Its stronger wording that work allocation creates different usable representations must remain a hypothesis, not the immediate interpretation of a role effect.

## Closest-method checks

The original-method account of [CLIN](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.10134) is sound: Algorithm 1 and §§3.2–3.3 explicitly update textual memories using trial trajectories and reward, then construct meta-memory for new tasks/environments. This already establishes the broad design of retained experience-derived information applied later without weight changes. I inspected those sections independently; I did not reproduce its experiments.

[Hendel et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.624.pdf) explicitly formulate query-independent task-state application without direct dependence on demonstrations. This defeats “source examples absent + fresh query + latent state” as a generic novelty claim. [ExpeL](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.10144v3) likewise explicitly separates experience collection, insight extraction and later application. Its abstract/overview were independently inspected here; the detailed transfer numbers remain backed by the planner's section-specific reading, not a new numerical replication by this reviewer.

The newly identified neighbors were checked beyond their abstracts:

- [Self-ICL](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.968.pdf), §§2.1–2.3, Figure 2 and Table 2: generate pseudo-inputs, infer pseudo-labels with the same frozen model, then prepend those textual pairs to a test query. Evaluation covers 23 BBH tasks, including comparisons with zero-shot and real demonstrations. This shows utility of self-generated demonstration content. The inspected design does not compare a generator's retained processing state with an observer receiving exactly the same examples.
- [Self-contemplation prompting](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/19914b5bf19ab2b245d2e6ff7299e8f0-Paper-Conference.pdf), Figures 1–2, §2 and Appendix Figure 9: construct demonstrations tailored to the test input, extract them, and pass them into a subsequent ICL prompt. Its human-versus-generated comparison changes demonstration content and construction. It is close prior art for generating useful context, but the inspected methods do not identify an effect of having personally integrated identical information.
- [ECI2D](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.21149v1), §§3.2–4.1, Table 1 and Appendix F: translate generated rationales into programs, adapt parameters for related samples, execute them and retain demonstrations with adequate transfer performance. The reported endpoint is downstream benchmark performance. Its interventions concern executable procedures and demonstration selection, not a paired agent's retained native state. Program transfer does not by itself establish that a rationale mediated the original model answer; Appendix F itself preserves an identification limitation. Do not import the paper's stronger causal vocabulary into our cache experiment.

These reads narrow a particular overlap rather than certify absence of an equivalent study. Function-vector composition and broader learning-by-generation/memory literature remain incompletely inspected. No first-ever claim is supported or needed for the conditional recommendation.

## Required precisions before a stronger claim or execution

1. **Separate historical allocation from immediate role instructions.** Give both sides the same neutral instruction and response format at the post-cut test. If the intended claim is retained competence rather than the total role bundle, add the already relevant history-by-current-role check: hold each saved historical state fixed while varying the post-cut role cue, and compare both integration histories under the same current cue. A cue-only/no-practice comparator estimates how much asymmetry can be induced without integration experience. Appending a neutral cue does not erase earlier role instructions, so describe exactly what this control identifies. Reversing checking alone does not distinguish these alternatives. Without this check, report assigned-role effects only.

2. **Specify the paired unit and intervention schedule.** The proposed episode aggregation is correct: queries, sides, caches and removals are repeated measurements. But the stated within-world role contrast requires running both role assignments from a common pre-exposure snapshot; randomizing one role per world does not provide both potential outcomes. Choose either that paired branch design with randomized/counterbalanced order and labels, or a genuinely randomized between-world estimate. If several episodes share one sampled mapping/world, cluster at that shared draw. Practice performance remains a treatment outcome, not an inclusion criterion. Do not add a successful-only principal claim.

3. **Keep cache-position evidence at its actual level.** The premise-position × later-position factorial is useful, but later positions can re-encode premises, and retained positions can encode roles, demonstrations and answers together. Removing source positions does not remove their semantic information. Position/size-matched shams, wrong-content substitutions, restoration and recomputation controls can support a content-sensitive state contribution. They cannot uniquely distinguish an abstract composition operation from compressed evidence plus recomputation. Restoration establishes reversibility in that context; segment sufficiency requires insertion into a specified recipient background. Preserve these qualifications in the headline mechanism interpretation.

4. **Clarify the text comparison.** Re-prefill each recipient's exact visible transcript under a fixed deterministic numerical contract. A difference from the original native cache then concerns information/computation absent from that re-prefill, subject to implementation checks. If transcripts differ between roles, the comparison does not equalize visible role or work content. Conversely, replacing both with one identical transcript erases several treatments at once. Disappearance of asymmetry under that replacement is informative but not selective evidence for one hidden representation. Keep output errors, order, positions and work budgets auditable.

5. **Bind the actual composition generator before collection.** “Fresh input” means an input withheld from practice outputs but present in the complete component mappings; it does not mean a symbol lacking an f entry. Verify that both private mapping components matter for each held-out query, and prevent degenerate mappings or answer repetitions from turning the task into single-source lookup. These are answerability checks, not outcome selection. The affine logical example is a separate counterexample, not validation of this generator.

## Strongest case, killer and logical verification

The strongest result would be a reproducible allocation-dependent handoff gap under common test instructions, complete premise/output exposure and fixed budgets, together with an explanation that predicts when a modest handoff intervention restores the disadvantaged side. A text/context explanation would still yield useful new knowledge about allocation and handoffs. It would need substantive effect size, reproducibility across fresh worlds and a second composition setting to carry a broad contribution; a single arbitrary-symbol demonstration is unlikely to do so.

The sharpest killer for the stronger acquired-representation thesis is that post-cut role cues alone reproduce the gap, or that visible transcript differences fully predict it without any additional state account. That outcome leaves a bounded prompting/workflow result, not nothing. Symmetric performance with adequate precision would limit the proposed allocation dependence. Native-channel failure kills the native version at feasibility, while an ordinary text study remains a separate possible study whose value must be assessed on its own.

I reran `logicalcheck.py`: 25 affine worlds and 75 held-out applications pass. Its stored-rule versus recomputation equality is valid and important. One retained earlier answer alone is insufficient in the example; the current plan correctly notes that an answer plus a private premise can suffice. This check supplies neither a role effect nor model feasibility. Root should preserve this limited interpretation in the final synthesis and carry the required controls as unresolved design choices rather than silently treating them as adopted protocol.
