# Independent review: responsive evidence acquisition

October 26, 2026. Initial substantive review of the actual source audit, development note, plan, logical script and saved output. Root owns shared claims, tests and git; this review changes no protocol or experimental authorization.

**Disposition: support as a concrete, conditional explanatory opportunity; defer readiness for a model diagnostic and any established flagship claim.** The strongest opportunity is not that communication can impair exploration. It is whether useful internal sharing changes *which level of an information-seeking policy is copied*, and consequently changes what the pair can discover. The calibration crossover gives that question a falsifiable form. A demonstrated ordinary copying mechanism would be a legitimate answer if the intervention supports it; generic exploration precedents do not alone veto the project. The current prose nevertheless leaves timing and information-source ambiguities that must be resolved before its scripted predictions describe an executable experiment.

## What the logical check establishes

I reproduced the exact script with file writes intercepted; all six stored calibration/policy rows match. With A's action fixed, nominal-button copying gives one distinct observed bit in aligned calibration and two in crossed calibration. Semantic-component copying gives one in both; choosing the complementary component gives two in both. Under independent uniform bits, perfect probes, and no other world information, these yield ideal full-code accuracy bounds of one-half and one respectively. This is a valid, useful distinction among these three scripted policies.

“Information rank” here is the count of independently revealed world coordinates, equivalently entropy reduction under those assumptions. It is not a rank estimate of neural representations. Counts must include information imported through every channel, not just environment probe logs. Policy signatures separate the stipulated behavioral rules; they do not uniquely identify a latent representation or exhaust mixed and mapping-error policies.

## Bounded corrections required

1. **Match the crossover's update order to its script.** The script fixes A and chooses B conditional on A. The stronger plan instead lets both agents revise tentative choices during a two-turn consultation. If both copy the other's previous different choice, they can remain different; reciprocal copying need not increase same-button responses. Specify a committed first actor and one responding actor for the initial crossover, carrying over the fresh private sensor-availability cue, or enumerate the actual two-turn update rule and derive new predictions. This is a timing correction, not a demand to abandon reciprocal work later.

2. **Remove the opposite-cue yoke's possible information leak.** In the minimal chain, A first observes a bit and then transmits state. An opposite-cue yoke generated from the same hidden code can carry the other bit. A trajectory scored as one distinct actual probe component may then already contain both facts. The cleanest proposed repair is to capture all early consultation states after committing A's privately constrained probe but before any probe results are revealed, and generate/reveal observations only after choices commit. If post-observation yokes are retained, specify independent donor worlds and establish the required conditional independence; audit every imported observation. Source tags or matching message length cannot prove that a native state excludes the other value.

3. **Retain the private contingency, or narrow the claim.** The stronger extension currently describes public instrument maps and freely chosen probes, omitting the minimal chain's fresh private cue. With public maps and stable roles, a fixed role policy can obtain both bits without responsive communication. This does not invalidate a causal finding that early sharing harms an already competent policy, but it would not demonstrate a necessary responsive acquisition chain. Explicitly retaining a newly randomized private availability/committed-action cue prevents this ambiguity. Intervention on that cue or committed proposal is also needed to distinguish induced copying from shared button/label preferences. Report within-episode responses to the intervention, not just correlations of final choices.

4. **Make stage isolation an operational contract.** Independent assignment of early and late gates is sound, as is the forced-complementary arm. The late gate must also be hidden from the agents until acquisition ends if the intended acquisition contrast excludes anticipation of pooling. No late instruction, answer query, environment receipt, filename or bookkeeping field may disclose unseen bits or the optimal probe. Late forks must start from exact saved states, prevent further probes and change only the specified return operation/work. The forced-complementary arm must deliver B's measured value to B alone before testing its return to A. These requirements are not yet verified by the symbolic script.

5. **Keep the final claim at the measured level.** Improved A final-code accuracy with controlled complementary evidence supports useful late pooling. It does not itself establish that both agents' knowledge converges. Use that narrower wording unless mirrored endpoint tests support the broader statement; sides remain paired observations. Likewise, a native/text difference identifies a difference between the implemented routes. It does not isolate a pure carrier mechanism when payload extraction, source labels or processing budgets differ. Mapping competence and prospective label permutation are necessary and already correctly proposed; a causal copying interpretation additionally needs the exogenous proposal intervention above.

## Feasibility, interpretation and value

The independent early/late gates and forced-complementary arm are a substantial strength: they separate an acquisition effect from the availability of useful final evidence without conditioning on naturally successful acquisition. They estimate controlled interventions, not a natural mediation decomposition. A late benefit with an early loss would locate a consequential stage-specific failure only after the leakage and timing repairs. A lost bit reduces attainable final performance under the audited information assumptions, so the coverage endpoint has an objective consequence beyond diversity of wording.

The episode/layout family is the appropriate unit, with gates, cue flips, calibrations, sides and restored branches paired. Four hidden codes do not become many independent model subjects through repeated answers. Any uncertainty estimate must refer to the explicitly sampled layout/remapping distribution and the fixed checkpoint. Invalid decisions should remain visible and separate from valid duplicate probes. No numerical sample-size or feasibility claim can be justified by the symbolic check; useful native acquisition and return remain missing capabilities in the existing project.

For the brain-bridge motivation, the meaningful payoff is precise: a connection can make previously unavailable evidence usable while altering whether two systems continue to investigate complementary parts of their world. The crossover could explain whether the loss follows a nominal action or a semantic target, and whether delaying that same useful connection preserves acquisition. That is a defensible explanatory opportunity even if the best account is familiar copying. It becomes a substantial contribution through reliable causal separation and consequential performance, not through a new interface name, a first-phenomenon claim, or an implication about consciousness.

**Recommendation:** preserve this candidate and repair the five bounded items before proposing the finite fresh native diagnostic to the PI. Do not launch a campaign or add task complexity to rescue it. If useful late transfer is absent, report the feasibility limit. If calibrated semantic coordination succeeds, retain that successful chain and reject the predicted differentiation failure in the tested range. If both text and native routes show the same causally established copying, report the common mechanism and the bounded extension. This initial review does not certify novelty or publication strength; it supplies a concrete path to assess them.
