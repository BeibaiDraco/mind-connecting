## What the closest studies actually establish

**Auvray, Lenay & Stewart (2009), Perceptual interactions in a minimalist virtual environment.** [Original PDF](https://cepa.info/fulltexts/478.pdf), methods §§2.1–2.4, results §§3.1–3.3, pp. 35–43; original passages read. Twenty people formed ten dyads; each moved a one-dimensional avatar and clicked when recognizing the partner. Partner contact stimulated both people; an offset moving lure copied the partner's movement without reciprocal stimulation. Avatar contacts generated 52.2% of stimulation and 65.9% of clicks; lure contacts generated 15.2% and 23.0%. The normalized click/stimulation ratios did not significantly distinguish avatar and lure. Thus interaction organized encounters without establishing individual encounter-level discrimination. This was not a randomized mediation experiment: post-treatment encounter normalization cannot identify a direct recognition effect. It provides the distinction to investigate, not a new LLM claim or proof of absence of recognition.

**LACO (Chen, Wu & Lee, 2026).** [Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.22504v1), §§3–5 and supplement A.1–A.2; methods, ablations and evaluation passages read. Frozen driving models exchange compressed shallow KV states in CARLA. Ego-only, language, visual and latent collaboration are compared; route-level driving score, completion and infractions are averaged. ORION driving score improves by 8.80 over ego-only and 4.00 over visual sharing. Full-depth fusion can impair ego control; depth and compression ablations support their engineering remedy. This directly occupies useful internal sharing in closed-loop independent action. The checked study does not randomize acquisition separately from interpretation with fixed evidence inventories. That narrower distinction is an extension, not proof that the broader question is new. Exact route count was not located in the passages checked; no sample-size claim is made.

**CacheBack (Rossi et al., 2026), Receiver-Conditioned Latent Communication gives 94% CacheBack.** [Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.32046v1), §§2–4; source interface, diagnostics and end-to-end methods/results read. The question is which completed sender positions should enter a receiver's message. A receiver query selects evidence using attention; the transmitted continuous rows differ from full native KV. A 13-task FanOutQA diagnostic compares conditioned and independent selectors using annotated evidence recall. Fifty tasks per end-to-end benchmark test fan-in QA or document chains; text and latent senders receive the same query. The paper reports improved accuracy–latency tradeoffs. Receiver-specific extraction of already computed information is therefore established. Selecting a new environmental measurement whose outcome does not yet exist is different, but the distinction alone is not sufficient importance. Text length and latent budgets are not matched into a universal equal-resource comparison.

**ECMR (2026), Evidence-Aware Deliberation Control for Multi-Agent LLM Reasoning.** [Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17869v1), §§3–5.3; method, theory, setup and ablation passages read. A trained controller selects challenge, verification, diversification, correction or stopping around frozen LLMs. Fixed schedules and state-shuffled controllers test state-dependent action allocation. Declared main evaluation is 3,000 records per each of three domains, with paired tests limited to separate released diagnostics. State shuffling decreases reported accuracy by 11.7 points and changes mean rounds by −0.7. This supports adaptive control beyond operation availability; it does not establish equal-compute superiority, since tokens are reported rather than equalized. Any generic claim that responsive computation creates better evidence opportunities overlaps directly.

**Lauri et al. (2020), Multi-agent active information gathering in discrete and continuous-state decentralized POMDPs by policy graph improvement.** [Original](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10458-020-09467-6), §§8.1–8.2 and task definitions; methods/results read. Two simulated vehicles choose camera/radar observations; two rovers choose movement and measurements; a continuous source-localization task is also studied. Some simultaneous measurements interfere, while co-located rover measurements improve reliability. Policies optimize information gain and cost, compared with exact/heuristic, greedy open-loop and blind policies; discrete comparisons average 100 optimization runs. Short-horizon values approach the exact optimum, and continuous policies divide search regions. Thus complementary sensing, observation-contingent policies, and favorable opportunity distributions have longstanding computational precedents. This is planning-algorithm evidence, not evidence about a frozen LLM bridge's acquisition/interpretation dissociation.

**Bernstein, Shore & Lazer (2018), How intermittent breaks in interaction improve collective intelligence.** [Original PDF](https://www.pnas.org/content/pnas/115/35/8734.full.pdf), pp. 8735–8738, Table 1 and methods; primary methods/results read. Triads solve 25-city traveling-salesperson maps over 17 rounds, randomized to see peers' solutions always, every third round, or never; storage is a second intervention. Constant access increases average quality but reduces exploration and best-solution quality. Intermittent access retains stronger exploration with high mean performance; optimum frequencies are 48.3% intermittent versus 33.3% constant, with the reported adjusted comparison significant. This directly preempts a broad "sharing helps use but harms discovery" or "separate before sharing" novelty claim. The task searches candidate solutions rather than acquiring externally unknown facts, leaving a possible bounded extension, not a clean conceptual vacuum.

**Diversity Collapse in Multi-Agent LLM Systems (2026).** [Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.18005v1), §§2.1–2.3 and 5.2; primary methods and topology results read. Persona-conditioned agents discuss research proposals, then synthesize them; each setting has 50 sessions per topic across 20 topics. Semantic and lexical diversity, plus quality, assess outputs. Standard interaction, nominal-group separation and subgroup structures change convergence; nominal groups improve initial diversity and subgroups sustain later critical engagement. This already transfers communication/independence concerns to LLM teams. Semantic diversity is not an oracle measure of newly acquired information. No new factual-acquisition claim follows just by replacing the diversity metric; a causally isolated and consequential failure would be required.

**MACE (Choi et al., 2026), Multi-Agent LLMs Fail to Explore Each Other.** [Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.11250v1), §§5.1–5.3; methods/results read. Ten Qwen agents receive different HotpotQA contexts; heterogeneous models are used in other tasks. In-context, random, fixed-peer and guided contextual-bandit selection are compared. HotpotQA uses 600 hard questions split into trial-and-error and exploitation halves. In-context agents can concentrate on an early peer and underperform random selection; guided exploration improves performance. Peer/source exploration failures are therefore direct precedent, including same-model agents with different contexts. Newly generated measurement outcomes differ from choosing an existing evidence holder, but an ordinary exploration failure remains a plausible explanation here.

**CAMA (2026), Beyond Memory Majority.** [Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.19701v1), evidence-recovery method, Tables 1–3 and ablation discussion read. Query-conditioned dependence estimates and a learned expand/trace/stop policy arbitrate correlated retrieved memories and recover missing independent evidence. MemoryAgentBench, LongMemEval and LOCOMO comparisons and component removals support reported improvements in arbitration and independent evidence recovery. This occupies both correlated-support handling and active evidence recovery. Our possible difference concerns communication changing which observations ever get made before the evidence exists, rather than recovering entries from memory; its novelty remains conditional.

**Contribution verdict:** generic adaptive benefit is occupied; generic sharing/exploration tradeoffs are occupied. We have not established that this extension is sufficiently important or distinct for the requested flagship.
