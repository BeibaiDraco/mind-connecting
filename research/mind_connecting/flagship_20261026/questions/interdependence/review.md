# Independent review: acquired partner dependence

October 26, 2026. Initial substantive review of the actual [source record](source-evidence.md), [development](development.md) and [plan](plan.md), after reading the project governance, current STATUS, latest decisions and relevant protocol contracts. Root owns shared governance, tests and commits. This reviewer changes only this file. No model execution or new research adoption.

## Judgment

**Accept as a conditional causal research opportunity; do not yet present it as a ready flagship experiment.** The useful question is whether repeated interleaving during formation changes later partner specificity on the same fresh task, and which acquired handoff behavior carries that change. This is more precise than showing another swap penalty. The plan correctly allows conventions to explain a valuable result: discovering a historical cause and demonstrating its operation and repair can be substantive even when the mechanism belongs to a known family.

The present task underdetermines the efficiency opportunity, state carrying history, and scheduling intervention. These are repairable design gaps, not reasons to relabel an empty literature cell as novelty. The broad proposition that interdependence causes cognitive organization already has human precedents. The strongest supportable prospective contribution is a controlled formation-to-swap-to-repair account in frozen internally connected systems, conditional on useful native operation.

## Independent source check

I independently opened the original methods for [Testing Interchangeability](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05279v1), §§3–4.1. It explicitly uses frozen role-matched agents whose persistent learned state is a two-section notebook. It compares experienced swaps with placebo reinstatement, clearing partner notes, intact amnesia and naive replacement. Its low/high coupling settings also vary recipe and equipment complexity, which the authors explicitly describe as an observational gradient. Thus randomizing formation topology while holding the evaluation task common is an intelligible causal extension. Familiarity, notebook-supported anticipation and experienced swaps themselves are already direct precedents. I did not independently re-audit every reported result table; the planner's numerical source audit retains that responsibility.

I independently checked [Shih et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2104.02871v1), §5.1 and the adaptation procedures immediately preceding it. Their 23 human pairs transfer conventions after repeated coordination attempts; the AI method separates trainable task and partner modules and tests new-partner and new-task adaptation. This precludes a broad claim to first separate component skill from conventions. It does not establish the proposed effect of randomized graph ownership on subsequent replacement cost.

I independently checked [MATE](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.06025v1), §§4–5.1. A partner's previous attention map is transformed into the receiver's coordinates and supplied as an observation channel; training additionally aligns attention to future partner focus over relevant objects. This is substantive internal-signal coordination precedent. Its trained policies and objective are materially different from the fixed-Qwen study, but that difference alone is not novelty. Its aim also motivates the opposite prediction: shared signals can help unfamiliar partners coordinate.

For [Hollingshead 2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11761309/), I checked the indexed original abstract and the [publisher preview](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/325670993_Cognitive_interdependence_and_convergent_expectations_in_transactive_memory). The latter exposes the first page, not the full Methods. The abstract reports a 2 × 4 experiment crossing partner-knowledge expectations with incentives for overlapping versus distinct recall, and different transactive-memory structures. This is direct overlap with a broad causal-organization claim. Sample size, allocation details and full outcome analysis remain unverified. Do not call the remaining question the first causal test of interdependence or imply that this source is only observational. Full original access remains a deciding novelty check, alongside the identified dissertation lead; the bounded review cannot certify field absence.

## Accepted points

- The formation-history × experienced-swap contrast on common evaluation tasks separates a historical treatment from the currently required crossing count. Report its component contrasts; a null interaction must not hide strong swap costs in both arms.
- Equal assigned operations, role reversal, fresh facts and preserved incoming state make the proposed contrast appreciably better than changing entire task difficulty and then interpreting a coupling gradient.
- Communication amount, successful practice and conventions can be consequences of formation. The total-effect analysis should not adjust them away using post-treatment regression. Their existence nevertheless limits a specific anticipation explanation until separately tested.
- Immediate test and fixed recovery horizon, dependent swap-block inference, action rather than report outcomes, and fresh confirmation after mechanism discovery are appropriate.
- Portable-convention rescue is informative. A short contract eliminating costs can support an account of how useful organization forms and transfers; it defeats irreducible-partner claims, not all research value.
- The recorded existing project evidence supplies reusable snapshots and reasons to investigate history, but supplies neither usable reciprocal competence nor estimates of this historical effect. The plan states that distinction correctly.

## Required bounded corrections

1. **Specify what the four-step task buys.** A purely serial chain with identical public tables, equal component skill and unrestricted recomputation has no inherent computational speedup from splitting it between two processors; handoffs add overhead. If ownership constrains who may commit each operation, state this explicitly. Clarify whether nonowners may compute, whether their intermediates may be used, and what resources requests, waiting, computation and handoffs consume. The minimal defensible target is *learned handoff efficiency under an imposed partition*, compared with a less adapted pair. A same-budget serial solver is a benchmark, not evidence that cooperation is advantageous. Do not claim spontaneous emergence of work allocation when the engine assigns it. Broader efficiency or emergent-allocation claims need separate evidence, not a longer chain chosen after failure.

2. **Name the state carrying history.** Frozen weights cannot acquire persistent differences unless something survives formation. Specify, at proposal level, whether this is full transcript/KV, an explicit notebook, or another state; what is reset at the task boundary; the ex ante capacity/context budget; and precisely what moves with a swapped member. If history includes old ownership instructions, current instructions must clearly supersede them. Otherwise persistent task-set interference or conflicting cached instructions can cause the same interaction. History length and useful content can be mediators of the total effect, but cannot silently become evidence for anticipatory scheduling. Hold the retention rule fixed and report realized lengths rather than trimming after seeing the effect.

3. **Operationalize the anticipation intervention.** Cloning each actual pre-handoff state and delaying or replacing an actual scheduling act can test a component cause. There is no single naturally identical state across all formation histories. Specify the receiver input, computation allowance, clock/deadline and packet content preserved in each branch. In a native channel, an unchanged whole packet can be moved in time; semantic equality alone does not establish equality of internal representations. The useful prediction is a history-specific miss at the expected handoff, followed by selective rescue, with substantially smaller effects in an appropriate nonanticipating comparison. Any actor can fail when a necessary packet arrives after a deadline. That generic effect is not evidence that formation created partner-specific anticipation. Do not describe this controlled component experiment as a quantified mediation analysis.

4. **Add a prior-formation caveat now.** Carry the Hollingshead abstract-level causal precedent into the current claim. Full methods remain unresolved; do not delay the correction until access improves. The proposed extension concerns constant-graph formation topology, later experienced replacement and targeted scheduling repair, rather than discovery that cognitive interdependence shapes organization.

These corrections require a short task/state contract and narrowing of the prose, not an expanded menu of new experiments. They are one bounded repair of this initial review. Scientific choices remain recommendations for the PI.

## Rival outcomes and feasibility limits

If formation improves both arms similarly and experienced swaps cost the same, that supports history use without the proposed effect of repeated crossings. If interleaving reduces swap cost, common signals or repeated feedback may standardize expectations; this is a plausible counterprediction, not a failed positive story. If the interaction is explained by different retained facts or operation competence, the total treatment effect remains real but its organization interpretation weakens. If only a stale ownership instruction predicts mistakes, the result is task-set persistence. If ordinary schedule repair restores performance selectively, the mechanism becomes portable coordination rather than particular-partner necessity.

If no useful native intermediate transfer and return can be demonstrated, stop the native claim. A working text version can establish a task or replicate an organizational mechanism; it cannot stand in for the original internal-bridge question. Conversely, native implementation without the historical contrast adds no causal account of acquisition.

Retain fresh held-out records and independent formation families; no formation answer may answer an evaluation item. Predefine which information the engine displays, and prohibit it from supplying an ungenerated intermediate or choosing requests. Measure raw successful completions, committed operation counts and communication/waiting costs on all branches; do not divide only by successful trials or select pairs showing a penalty. Technical missingness and behavioral failure need distinct prespecified treatment. Equal wall time alone does not equalize two models' inference work, while equal token count need not equalize latency; report the relevant resource profile.

## Bounded recommendation

Proceed with one written task/state-contract repair and an offline symbolic audit. Then reassess against the unresolved human original before asking the PI to invest in a competence pilot. Substantial value would require a replicated causal historical effect, an actual action-level explanation, and a demonstrated portability/repair boundary. A bare topology-by-swap interaction is a controlled extension; a bare native swap penalty is close to replication. Neither guarantees novelty or venue acceptance. No new target-effect search or pilot expansion is justified by this review.

**Fixed-clock check.** The new 16-tick horizon is an unadopted common draft capacity, not evidence of an empirical history cost. Under the written rules and correct operations, a public ABAB handoff schedule can complete in seven ticks: EXEC1, SEND1, EXEC2, SEND2, EXEC3, SEND3, EXEC4. AABB needs five corresponding sequential ticks. Both histories face the same later evaluation partition; a shared public schedule therefore permits zero swap penalty irrespective of formation. Unrestricted private recomputation also supplies a noncommunication route. Thus the deadline does not logically force a history-by-swap effect. This is a paper-and-pencil feasibility observation, not model validation or a substitute for the planned symbolic audit.

The remaining operational limit is real: a common finite horizon can amplify any difference in headroom. All-episode operation/request/wait counts and the proposed pre-deadline wasted-work prediction must carry the mechanism interpretation; completion failures at tick 16 alone cannot. Returning a delayed packet to its original time is also a restoration control, not independent proof of partner specificity. These limitations are consistent with the repaired plan's narrow claim. No horizon or packet time may be selected because it yields the desired interaction.
