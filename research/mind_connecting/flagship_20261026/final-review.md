## Scientific alignment

**Formation.** Randomized AABB versus ABAB formation followed by the same fresh evaluation mixture and experienced/placebo swaps identifies a history-by-replacement contrast. The primary task concerns handoff efficiency under imposed ownership. It is not emergent division of labor and has no inherent serial-chain speedup. The repaired plan specifies owner-only commitment, optional recomputation, task/cache resets, a capped notebook and timing interventions on actual generated packets. A shared public schedule can produce zero swap cost, so the proposed result is not guaranteed by the graph. A history-specific response to a packet delay plus restoration is a useful component test, but generic deadline failure or stale ownership instructions would not establish acquired anticipation. The text/notebook diagnostic and a future native extension must remain explicit in the final recommendation.

**Acquisition/use.** The repaired ordering is coherent: the private availability cue fixes A's probe; early states precede every observation value; B alone responds; both observations follow commitments; late assignment is concealed during acquisition. The opposite-cue control therefore need not import the missing bit in the stipulated schema. Independently gated late pooling and forced-complementary acquisition answer controlled intervention questions without conditioning on naturally successful acquisition. The calibration crossover separates the stipulated button-following, component-following and complementary response maps. It does not identify literal copying instructions or an internal semantic code. Native/text differences remain differences between implemented routes, with payload, labels and processing as rivals. Actual prompt/cache leakage and usable native return remain unverified.

**Separation.** The defensible initial effect is the total integration/checking-role bundle: instructions, work, generated text and computation history all change. Current neutral instructions do not erase historical role cues. The final plan explicitly defers the stronger acquired-representation claim and preserves the unresolved cue controls. Paired role assignments, world-level clustering, held-out practice inputs present in the complete mappings, actual errors and complete episode populations are now specified. Removing cache positions does not remove a semantic category; restoration establishes reversibility in its background, not independent segment sufficiency. Own-transcript re-prefill and giving both agents an identical transcript answer different questions. Those limits appear in the actual plan and final report, so the precision narrowing does not require a new core proposal or restart the exhausted repair allowance.

The report's existing-evidence paragraph is consistent with the verified evidence table: scalar access matching is incomplete; post-cut readout recovery is not restored identity; sibling goal/rule reports do not identify action mediators; useful complete native reciprocity remains unestablished. None of these clues confirms the three new predictions.
