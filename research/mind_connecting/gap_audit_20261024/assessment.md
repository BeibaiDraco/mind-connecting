# What is actually unknown after the closer precedent audit?

October 24, 2026. This supersedes the positive novelty assessment in the earlier [goal-preservation proposal](../goal_preservation_20261024/recommendation.md). It preserves that proposal as a historical task sketch, not a validated research direction. The audit includes primary-source reading by two field authors, a project-evidence reader and an independent source/final-document reader. See [sources and search limits](sources.md), [project clues](evidence.md) and [review](final-review.md).

## The consequential correction

The broad problem—use another agent's internal information while retaining local decision control—is already studied. LACO is especially direct: separate vehicles share KV information, benefit from complementary perception and can suffer inappropriate collaborator-influenced control. It evaluates restricted-depth sharing. The functional problem is therefore not made new by two instances, training-free communication, legitimate separate tasks, or useful information. A new symbolic/compositional task can improve experimental control but does not itself establish conceptual novelty.

The other nearby literatures cover additional parts: benign latent communication can degrade behavioral safeguards; legitimate client objectives can interfere in cached state; content and discourse scope can be manipulated separately; source authority can determine permissible memory-backed actions; receiver-specific evidence selection and selective communication defenses already include utility evaluation. These are substantive overlaps, even when their implementations differ from ours. Source-specific limits are recorded in sources.md.

This does not imply that the original findings are worthless, or that every mechanism is understood. It does mean that the previous recommendation was too positive about the remaining gap.

## A real unresolved question, already recognized by the field

For a specified case of shared-state interference, **what computation causes information associated with one actor to control a decision for another actor?** Goals Leak explicitly leaves incorrect goal–client binding, inappropriate retrieval and competition between correctly represented objectives unresolved. LACO's depth comparisons do not isolate these alternatives. Factual distortion and generic impairment also remain rivals in our setting.

Consider a recipient assigned fast delivery and a donor assigned low-cost delivery. The donor knows a useful road fact. A recipient choosing the cheap route could have:

1. Attached the low-cost objective to the recipient's actor representation.
2. Retained the assignments but retrieved the donor's plan for this decision.
3. Retrieved both assignments correctly, with the donor objective dominating downstream choice.
4. Retained its own objective but acted on a distorted road fact.

The same choice can arise in all four accounts. A separate correct answer to “What is your goal?” does not identify the computation producing that choice. Likewise, deleting donor state can remove several causes together. These distinctions matter because correcting actor binding, restricting retrieval and protecting downstream control would address different failures. This is a genuine explanatory uncertainty, not a new question invented by this project and not a demonstrated mechanism.

## What the philosophical motivation adds—and does not add

“Whose past, present and future?” helps distinguish historical origin, currently accessible information, reported ownership and the commitments governing subsequent action. It does not establish that these quantities are one variable called identity. Nor does asking about them originate the distinction: the source/scope, attribution, agency and goal-isolation literatures already contain close ideas.

The project's promising empirical tension is that these readouts can dissociate. Its role is to constrain explanations, not to demonstrate that a self has merged or persisted. In particular, the Robin results make a self-exclusive interpretation premature. A future explanation may concern ordinary actor–content organization that also supports self-related judgments; that would remain meaningful if its causal structure and practical consequences were established.

## How the existing evidence narrows the question

- Matched mean access with different attribution in Qwen makes an access-strength-only account inadequate. It does not match every information dimension or exclude other state interventions.
- Selective recovery after disconnecting the same reflected state suggests that continued access matters differently for different questions. It does not show original memory restoration or prove multiple memory stores.
- Third-party Robin conflict shows that explicitly named non-self records are vulnerable too. This constrains self-specific explanations and is compatible with the manuscript's generic slot account.
- The small goal/rule mismatch and work/readout crossing suggest that when information is used matters. The initial channel diagnostic includes actual-action-driven rounds, but its goal/rule responses do not establish enacted goal takeover. Only the fixed-scene goal/stage follow-up uses entirely discarded action probes. Neither establishes useful donor knowledge or a durable change in intent.
- Source-qualified wording redistributes errors, and the self-header package reverses the predicted direction. These expose limits of attribution questions as evidence about internal ownership; they do not identify reactance.

These clues justify investigating a mechanism. They do not yet choose among the four accounts or certify a distinctive contribution. The preserved manuscript's positional/slot explanation is one concrete hypothesis, with important untested predictions; it should neither be accepted by default nor discarded as refuted by Robin.

## Consequence for the paper decision

Do not adopt the previous compositional routing task as the paper center on novelty grounds. Necessary useful donor information and separate recipient commitments remain good design requirements, but the substantive contribution must add a substantive empirical or explanatory result beyond the precedents. A strong result would distinguish competing causal accounts, correctly predict a previously untested setting, and, if claiming a remedy, preserve the particular information benefit that justified sharing. A novel failure mode could also be valuable without a remedy; another aggregate interference rate would be much weaker.

The smallest unresolved scientific decision is whether the project can specify an intervention and contrasting predictions that distinguish those accounts in a useful shared-information setting. That is a criterion for evaluating a future proposal, not an intervention already designed or validated here. No new experiment, task, metric, selector, sample size or manuscript revision is adopted. The current evidence supports a bounded attribution study and mechanistic clues; a distinctive, valuable new flagship contribution has not yet been identified. Venue prospects cannot be inferred from a philosophical framing.

## Prepared English delivery summary

I need to revise my earlier positive judgment. The missed LACO paper already tackles useful internal-state sharing with preserved local control, and other close papers cover goal interference, scope/authority and selective utility. The broad question is not untouched. The genuinely unresolved issue is which computation causes a specified cross-actor control error: incorrect binding, inappropriate retrieval, downstream competition, factual distortion or generic interference. Goals Leak itself acknowledges the first three alternatives. Our access/attribution dissociation, selective recovery, Robin counterevidence and small goal/rule mismatch constrain these explanations but do not identify one or demonstrate enacted goal takeover. The philosophical question remains useful motivation, while neither “self boundary” terminology nor a compositional task supplies novelty. The prior task should not be frozen as the new paper center. The next scientific decision is whether a discriminating causal explanation can be made testable; this audit has not yet established a distinctive top-conference-ready contribution. Existing bounded findings remain valuable.
