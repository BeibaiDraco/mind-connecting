# Closest precedents: what was read and what follows

Audit date: October 24, 2026. Primary sources support the comparisons below; no code reproduction or exhaustive priority search is claimed. Most recent sources are preprints, not independently replicated findings. Author descriptions such as identity confusion are distinguished from what their interventions identify. A missing exact task match is not evidence of novelty. Individual source notes are deliberately bounded; deciding comparisons were independently rechecked.

## Direct internal-state precedents

**LACO: Adaptive Latent Communication for Collaborative Driving** — Chen, Wu and Lee, May 21, 2026. [Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.22504v1), §§3–5 and supplement. Root and field author read methods/results; independent reviewer checked §§3–5, Tables 2–3. Separate ego-centric vehicle inference uses local observations and navigation conditioning. Full-depth KV sharing can disturb control; selected shallow sharing combines useful perception with improved closed-loop driving. This directly defeats the broad useful-sharing/local-control novelty claim. Depth changes whole representations; the experiments do not isolate goal binding, retrieval, perceptual distortion or downstream policy competition. No independent attribution outcome or crossed donor-goal/observation intervention was found. Avoid the inconsistent ORION default-depth number in Tables 2 and 3, and do not repeat a universal autonomy guarantee.

**Safety of Latent Communication** — Huzaifa et al., September 30, 2026. [Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.39788v1), §§2–6, Appendices A, B.4, C.1/C.4/C.5. Field author read deeply; root checked interface and repair. Frozen heterogeneous models share a query through trained hidden-state adapters. Benign math supervision increases harmful compliance; forced refusal prefixes support an initiation-path account. Repair jointly measures safety and utility, with heterogeneous utility changes rather than exact preservation. Selective behavioral protection with useful communication is already studied. Independent legitimate goals and source binding are not isolated. The repair-training/AdvBench evaluation split is not explicit in the checked passages; the attack-training no-overlap statement does not resolve it. Do not describe all repair evaluations as verified held-out tests.

**When Goals Leak Across Relationships** — Gupta et al., October 12, 2026. [Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17365v1), §§3–7. Root, field author and independent reviewer read deciding sections. One frozen OLMo model serves opposing clients with assignment swaps and competence controls. Goal-span probes, selected-head versus random/non-goal ablations and opposite-direction own/competitor interventions show goal-conditioned cache state affects decisions. Necessary useful cross-client information retained under intervention is not tested; recomputed-text and reused-cache conditions are not token-equivalent. Section 7 explicitly leaves binding, retrieval and objective competition unresolved, and raises useful memory with isolated goals. Both the behavioral problem and the mechanistic agenda predate our proposed reframing.

**Receiver-Conditioned Latent Communication gives 94% CacheBack** — Rossi et al. [Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.32046v1), §§2–5 and Appendix A; direct field author. Receiver queries select useful positions from an already formed sender state; distributed QA evaluates evidence use and receiver-independent controls. Receiver-specific extraction from one sender is established. These cooperative tasks do not isolate independently conflicting commitments; communicated continuous rows are not complete native KV tensors.

**When Latent Agents Lie** — Brito and Baquero. [Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.28958v1), §§3–8; direct field author. Split-evidence specialists exchange full KV plus visible commitments. Clean benefit and accepted-subset re-encoding demonstrate useful evidence dependence. Hidden attacks and adaptive defense failures coexist with utility measurements after rejection/downgrading. Authenticated manifests address post-handoff integrity, not truthfulness or internal goal binding. Re-encoding a subset is not deleting an unchanged cache component.

**When Does Latent Communication Pay?** — Cheng et al. [Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.04893v2); direct field author and earlier root audit. Deranged, zeroed and random relays distinguish example-specific information from generic channel effects. Channel removal hurting performance is insufficient evidence of useful information transfer. Donor-consistent wrong factual answers are also insufficient evidence of goal adoption.

## Scope, authority, and report versus action

**Whose Memory Is It? Scope-Aware Commit Rules for Long-Term LLM Memory (CASK)** — Gu and Li, October 6, 2026. [Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09008v1), §§3–6.6 and Appendix B; adjacent author, root and independent reviewer. Discourse scope distinguishes actual-world premises from beliefs/plans/counterfactuals, not merely the producing instance. Learned causal interventions hold payload fixed while altering scope; exported relational keys control an external memory gate. Thus content versus licensing scope is already an operational, causal distinction. Controlled-to-natural transfer is weak before adaptation; privileged provenance and learned/hybrid mechanisms must be identified. Utility is not uniformly improved, and output logits can offer a stronger high-recall frontier. This is not native two-agent goal control.

**When Memory Becomes Authority (AuthMem-Bench)** — Zhan et al., August 3, 2026. [Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.01679v1), §§2–4.4, Appendices E/F; root and adjacent author. Matched claims/tasks differ in authority-bearing source roles under an explicit benchmark policy. Memory consolidation and later native tool actions measure both unauthorized behavior and useful authorized actions. Necessary values are absent from later queries. Source text, authority metadata and conservative baselines differ in their utility/error tradeoffs. Therefore useful remembered content versus authority to act is already tested, not merely discussed. The mechanism is external memory governance; the assigned authority policy is not a universal fact about all user/assistant/tool statements.

**Computation, Not Memory** — October 19, 2026. [Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.25760v1), §§3–8, Appendices D.2/E/F; adjacent author and independent reviewer, root setup reading. File-lock tasks retain observation/action history. Reasoning generation/retention factorials and counterfactual reasoning swaps show that accurate reports or decoding need not identify action-causal information. Smaller effects of factual edits do not uniquely identify plans, since other conclusions remain. Different question/action patch sites do not prove independent representations. The appendix goal swap changes a terminal filename, not an independently acting donor's objective. This limits our goal-label interpretation without establishing the native KV mechanism.

**Where Is the “Self” of a Large Language Model?** — Li et al. [Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.16717v3), §§3.1–3.2.7 and 6.3; root and prior independent review. Native conversational roles support attribution effects and source-dependent use of an incorrect arithmetic step, with different intervention locations. The Assistant-slot bodies were not actually generated by that instance (§3.1.3); slot format/length also differ. Neither generic source/use dissociation nor causal role-index effects are untouched questions. Historical instance ownership and subjective self-awareness are not established.

**Memory, Know Your PLACE** — [Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.25179v1), §§2–3 and Tables 1–2; adjacent author. A controlled write/read factorial tests memory formation versus later influence, with irrelevant historical facts and utility probes. Its deterministic external governance receives trusted event/query semantics; the small trajectory benchmark does not identify a native agent mechanism. Write/read separation and selective retention already have direct precedent.

**Inherited Goal Drift** — Menon et al. [Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.03258v1), §§3–4.2; root. Conditioning on another agent's trajectories can disturb goal-directed behavior; correct goal descriptions need not produce appropriate action. Goal switching/reversal and instruction-hierarchy comparisons are relevant rivals to an identity interpretation. This is textual trajectory conditioning, not a native KV explanation.

## Broader constraints and reading limits

- [Binding IDs](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.17191v2), §§3–5, and [Mixing Mechanisms](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.06182v1), §§2–4: direct author checked content/binding and positional/lexical/reflexive causal comparisons. A decodable actor variable or an ownership-sensitive lesion does not uniquely identify a binding mechanism. Entity retrieval is not independent agent commitment, but generic causal binding is established.
- [Thought Communication in Multiagent Collaboration](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/b2b502c3629beadda06311386d2c6f73-Paper-Conference.pdf), NeurIPS 2025: root read abstract, §§4.2–5.2 and theorem assumptions. Shared/private factor identifiability assumes a particular latent generative structure; learned adapters and masks are evaluated on cooperative math tasks. Private factors are not demonstrated historical ownership or autonomous commitments. The proof was not independently audited.
- [LatentMem](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.03036v1): direct author read setup/methods/main experiments; role-customized latent memory improves shared-task performance, not separately valid goal isolation. [CachePrune](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.23640v1), §§1–4, concerns privacy and cache-reuse boundaries rather than goal interference.
- [Opacity Is Not Privacy](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.2186/) remains abstract/index-level only because primary full-text access failed. Its receiver-conditioned privacy/utility claim cannot be used as verified novelty closure or exclusion. Further memory-governance leads were not deeply read once direct conceptual coverage was established.
- Earlier reviewed cognitive motivation and its limits remain in [conceptual inputs](../conceptual_inputs_20261024.md). No new human neuroscience claim is made here, and no subjective mechanism is inferred from an LLM response.

## Search record and stopping reason

Search families included exact titles/citation neighbors; latent communication plus safety, goals, identity confusion, ego-centric control, binding and disentanglement; shared memory plus provenance, authority and source attribution; receiver-conditioned transfer; goal ownership/binding/competition; and self-other causal memory. Root queries included `"latent communication" "goal preservation"`, `"KV" "ego-centric" "interference" latent`, `language model "goal binding" interference`, and `"goal ownership" retrieval competition language model`. Exact-title follow-up searches yielded mirrors and commentary rather than a verified resolving follow-up. Official proceedings resolved the Thought Communication lead. Secondary hits served only as leads.

The audit stopped because multiple original sources now cover the proposed broad premise, while more search alone cannot identify which mechanism causes our observations. This is sufficient to withdraw the previous positive gap argument, not sufficient to prove there is no worthwhile unexplored question. No comprehensive first-study or top-venue judgment follows.
