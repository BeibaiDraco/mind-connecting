## Relation to the preserved manuscript

[The existing account](../../../paper/sections/account_and_discussion.tex) proposes two content cards competing at overlapping representation/position slots, with an additional default-self assignment premise. Robin conflict was explicitly predicted by this generic slot explanation. Therefore V5 limits a self-exclusive account; it does not refute the manuscript's entire mechanism proposal. Moving foreign state off the relevant positions and other causal predictions remain untested. The self-default premise has not been independently isolated.

STATIC is a particular fixed calibration-averaged rule direction, whereas the main KV study supplies structured fixed donor state through attention. Neither should be equated with all static context interventions or with a responsive second agent. Functional interface, state content, insertion location and reciprocal topology are distinct factors.

## Consequence

The strongest existing clues concern route-dependent attribution, selective recovery and generic record interference. The smaller goal/rule results motivate a causal question but cannot establish that correctly retained goals failed to control actions: separate reports do not identify the action mediator. The [previous logical check](../goal_preservation_20261024/recombination_check.json) also demonstrates that goal-dependent factual distortion can exactly mimic donor-contract choices. These constraints must survive any later interpretation, including a new experiment that does not reuse the old task.
