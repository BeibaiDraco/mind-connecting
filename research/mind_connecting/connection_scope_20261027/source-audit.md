## Corrected assessment

The previous dossier accurately recorded many individual limitations, but its headline of no promoted candidate can be mistaken for a stronger novelty rejection. These papers do not jointly prove that the proposed comparison has already been performed. Conversely, combining their nouns does not demonstrate novelty. The contribution must be an explanatory result about transporting context-dependent reference between independently acting models, preserving useful information, and identifying which interface property changes its appropriate use.

LACO is the nearest system-level precedent; binding work is the nearest mechanistic precedent. Paul supplies the conceptual link to situated action. Treat all three as foundations and demanding comparators, not automatic vetoes. An ordinary binding mechanism can be an important answer if it predicts consequential communication failures and a selective remedy. It would restrict a self-specific claim, not invalidate the research.

Bounded supplementary searches included latent communication with perspective/binding/self-other/ownership, and original binding work and follow-ups. They led to the two binding originals above. Search absence is not a novelty result. Other leads were not promoted to evidence. Prior security, introspection and uncertainty dossiers retain their original access-depth limits; this audit does not claim to reread all of them.

## Checked local evidence and implementation

- [V3 report](../../../docs/results/v3/confirmatory_v3.md) and [choice checks](../../../docs/results/story_checks.md): similar mean rule-access gains for one KV/STATIC pair accompany very different historical attribution. They do not match all accessible information. A stronger STATIC setting has nonzero donor claims.
- The same choice checks show different START/NOW recovery after removing readout access. Recipient history is retained, so this is not restoration of an earlier memory state.
- [V5 report](../../../docs/results/v5/provenance_post_collection_20261012.md): donor-only Robin conflict changes receiver-original Robin answers. The phenomenon extends beyond self questions; a balanced self-versus-other contrast is absent.
- [Existing account](../../../paper/sections/account_and_discussion.tex) already proposes generic overlapping-card retrieval plus an additional default-self premise. Its physical-position intervention remains untested. This proposal continues that lineage rather than claiming an entirely new discovery.
- [Legacy KV](../../../src/mb/runtime.py), `kv_attention`, and [native draft](../../../src/mb/v6_native.py), `_attention`: receiver queries jointly normalize local and foreign keys. Both compute `(1-beta)*own_output + beta*foreign_output`. Foreign bank identity exists in software but is not separately emitted with each head's mixed vector. Content can still encode source distinctions; mixing does not mathematically prove their loss.
- Native `_foreign` accepts at most sixteen exported ordinary generated positions, at every attention layer; `step` copies their actual cached K/V. The foreign keys retain their exported rotary encoding. Metadata owner checks protect implementation integrity, not model-level provenance comprehension.
- [V1 protocol](../../../docs/protocol/EXPERIMENT_DESIGN.md), section 6: STATIC is a calibration-averaged rule direction added to a residual stream. It is not synonymous with a fixed donor KV packet. Structured payload, receiving operation and responsiveness are different axes.

The original reports and named functions were reopened read-only. No raw result search, model execution or manuscript modification occurred.
