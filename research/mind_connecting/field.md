# Scientific distinctions that can support a new paper

The program concerns functional organization when one model's ongoing computation incorporates another's states. Theoretical motivation does not license a claim about phenomenal consciousness or the number of subjects. The key issue is whether information access, decision control and a model's representation of its own capacities are jointly or separately affected.

**Communication is an established capability, not our remaining novelty by itself.** Activation/KV communication and the distinction between message sensitivity and example-specific task value have close precedents in [the prior-use map](prior_use.md). Both a successful exchange and a donor-following error need an additional consequential endpoint. Functional placement and reciprocal contingency are independent design factors.

**Control and control attribution need separate ground truths.** Human apparent-agency work offers a comparison between actual causal control and the attributed source of an event. Here an action's dependence on interventions is observable; a model's explanation is another output. Correct goal labels cannot certify preserved action control, and a verbal explanation cannot identify the internal cause of a choice. See the existing source-backed [self-boundary review](../../docs/reviews/self_boundary_research_agenda_20261020.md). Transfer is a design analogy, not evidence of shared subjective mechanisms.

**External access and personal capacity estimates can diverge.** [Fisher, Goddu & Keil (2015)](https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/releases/xge-0000070.pdf), especially Experiment 1c and the discussion, compare search with matched explanatory content and measure later knowledge estimates. Their human results motivate separating the information received from how the system estimates its own capacity. They do not predict that an LLM must show the same effect. The actual source-to-target mapping is in [paperbank](paperbank/access_control_and_capacity.md).

**Self-prediction is already a studied, measurement-dependent capacity.** [Kadavath et al. §5.3–5.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2207.05221) test trained knowing estimates with source material and hints. [Looking Inward §3.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.13787) tests self-predictions after behavior-changing finetuning. [Generalized Correctness Models §3.1 and Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.24988) report no systematic self-prediction advantage for correctness in their settings. These results concern different training/targets and are not direct contradictions. Our candidate must distinguish prediction of an action from its correctness, and compare self-forecasts to transcript/history-based alternatives. None establishes untrained Qwen competence forecasting under our interface.

[MetaCogAgent §III-B/C](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.17292) already combines confidence, historical capability profiles and task delegation. Thus another confidence-based helper-selection system is weak novelty. The more specific candidate is prospective calibration to causally manipulated internal dependence on another currently connected instance, with retained information and hidden availability distinguished.

**Partner-specific adaptation is attractive but costly to identify.** A live stream identical to replay cannot distinguish its origin to a deterministic receiver. New private interventions create informative live/replay contrasts; fresh shadows inheriting actual histories are not globally open loop. Pair-specific adaptation also needs cross-play/partner swaps with comparable competence, content and exposure. Coordination alone does not identify this property. A reviewer identified [OverForge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39727) as an additional close lead; full assessment is pending, so this candidate carries no novelty claim.

No exact test of the proposed connection-dependent calibration combination was found in the scoped searches. This is an unresolved candidate gap, not certified priority. The main uncertainty is whether a usable connection and valid prospective measurement can be established without training. The closest introspection and delegation evidence makes that a real empirical prerequisite, not a formality.
