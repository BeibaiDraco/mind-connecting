## Initial corrections and consequences (all closed on recheck below)

1. **Specify the Figure 11 intervention.** In the integration note, add that increasing between-module weight reduces within-module weights under a fixed total. The present wording already refers to relative strengths, so this is a transfer guard rather than a withdrawal. Any candidate proposing stronger coupling with unchanged local computation must derive its own rival predictions; Figure 11 does not directly supply them.
2. **Keep transactive organization navigable.** The existing [Wegner case note](../knowledge/patterns/cases/wegner_1991.md) is not linked from the field map. Add a short link under distributed cognition or continuity after its independent pattern review resolves the assigned-structure interpretation. The coordinator reports that review supports the history-by-assignment interaction and harm to established pairs, but not a significant assignment benefit for impromptu pairs; do not translate a crossing interaction into two demonstrated simple effects. The source makes history-dependent allocation and partner replacement visible as distinct from retained facts or generic access ablation. This is a navigation repair using an already available source, not a demand for another literature sweep. I read the case note but did not independently reopen its original in this review; its stated reading depth should travel with it.
3. **Avoid inferring latent dimensionality from readout differences.** The overview's sentence that old data make a single all-or-none self boundary inadequate can be read as excluding a common underlying factor. The exposed evidence establishes different behavior across questions and interventions, but different item sensitivities or thresholds could still arise from a shared latent factor. Replace that implication with: “The results motivate measuring access, attribution and recovery separately rather than summarizing them with one binary label; they do not establish the latent dimensionality of a self boundary.” No new analysis is required for this correction.

There is no requirement to survey every consciousness theory, autobiographical-memory paradigm or social-neuroscience method before generation. A targeted next read becomes necessary only when a candidate relies on an untested inference—for example, that persona continuation work lacks the proposed separation, that partner replacement is unexplored, or that external evidence-dependence findings cannot transfer to the proposed causal question.
