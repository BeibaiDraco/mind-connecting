Failed transfer: both processes contain every document and the requester broadcasts every query. ## Consequential inference challenges even when prerequisites hold

These are not the failed transfers above.

1. **P1:** A system can retain all needed facts while relying on a remote computation to use them. Retention and distributed processing can coexist. Even selective loss with restoration identifies dependence under the tested resources, not a unique architecture or a constitutive cognitive boundary. Treat the guide's two accounts as conditional alternatives for a specified operation, not exhaustive rivals. Local retrieval scaffolding or extra compute can reproduce a cut effect unless separately controlled.
2. **P2:** A simple reactive controller can create better opportunities after perturbation and outperform replay without representing its partner. All stated prerequisites can hold. This does not defeat the question about interaction-generated situations; it defeats a stronger inference about social understanding or an irreducible other. Also, treatment changes who encounters each opportunity, so conditional accuracy differences need not identify changed decision competence. The bank already preserves these limits.
3. **P3:** An ordinary address lookup table can produce topic-specific errors and restoration after remapping while all fact and channel controls pass. This supports an operational directory but not a uniquely relational or cognitive mechanism. Conversely, preserved performance can reflect successful remapping or broadcast compensation rather than an absence of earlier organization; inspect routing before concluding direct content access. The bank's proposed measurements permit this distinction, but its “unchanged use would favor direct content access” sentence should remain a tentative interpretation.
