## 1. Can sharing a thought make its availability mutual knowledge, or does access outrun understanding of the other perspective?

**Question and importance.** After two instances gain useful access to each other's private task information, do they correctly distinguish “I know p,” “B knows p,” and “B knows that I know p”? The interesting unknown is whether internal exchange changes the organization of mutual accessibility or merely supplies each receiver with additional facts. This gives the original hope of a changed epistemic relation a functional test without asking whether either system experiences another mind.

**Comparison.** Construct a small family of coordination problems with independently controlled information delivery and receipt visibility. Arrange cases with the same first-order task facts available to A and B but different evidence about whether the other side received them or observed their receipt. Include asymmetric delivery, publicly signaled delivery, and a source-tagged textual communication comparison. A task consequence must depend on the specified level of knowledge: some decisions need only p; others require predicting B's decision given what B could know. Do not infer an infinite common-knowledge state from a two-level task. Ground truth is the experimental delivery/visibility graph, not self-report agreement.

**Measurements and rival outcomes.** Score actual coordinated decisions and separate factual, other-access and second-order access answers, with full error categories. A first-order gain accompanied by false symmetry of access would identify a concrete failure of perspective tracking. Preserved perspective distinctions despite strong private-state exchange would show that functional separation can coexist with internal accessibility. Improvement only with explicit receipts would locate an informational requirement rather than an intrinsic inability. If no actual decision distinguishes the levels, the task has not tested this question. Separate question branches do not establish the action mediator; subsequent causal manipulations would be required for that stronger claim.

**What the three sources supply.** The [original vision](../../../docs/background/Brain_Bridging_to_LLM_Selfhood_Research_Handoff_2026-09-23.md), §2.1 and Appendix A, supplies the epistemic target. [BrainNet](knowledge/field/papers/brainnet_2019.md) establishes useful source-sensitive mediated communication, but its decoded decisions/reliability analysis does not establish this higher-order distinction. [P2](knowledge/patterns/question-patterns.md) warns that successful joint behavior need not establish individual discrimination; it changes the comparison by requiring both coordination and perspective-sensitive judgments. The [data card](knowledge/dataset/card.md) supplies controlled private histories and counterfactual branches, while showing that old donor access/START/NOW questions never measured these higher-order relations.

**Closest work and unresolved boundary.** The [prior-use account](knowledge/dataset/prior-use.md) and original handoff identify self/other belief and role-index research; those are direct conceptual precedents. BrainNet already studies reliable communication, and generic theory-of-mind, communication acknowledgment and common-knowledge tasks may already answer much of this question. CAMA/ECMR address correlated support, which is related but not identical to tracking who observed whose receipt. The candidate is not “two agents know more,” generic source recognition, confidence forecasting, or duplicate-evidence correction. A worthwhile increment would require a specific relation between internal transfer and independently manipulated epistemic visibility that existing belief-tracking studies do not already establish.

**Prerequisite and first decision-changing check.** Before any model run, find the closest LLM false-belief/common-ground and latent-communication studies and inspect whether they already cross first-order access with receipt visibility. In parallel, symbolically construct the minimal worlds and prove that the required decisions differ while the relevant first-order facts stay fixed. If that separation needs covert oracle metadata or cannot survive a minimal task, stop this candidate. No present evidence supports privileged mutual access.

## 2. Does connection history create a division of cognitive work that survives changing the facts but fails when the partner's role changes?

**Question and importance.** With identical frozen weights, can paired context histories organize complementary work such that current information inventory alone is insufficient to predict pair competence? This asks what, if anything, is learned in the relationship during connection. It is distinct from defending one agent's goal against another's: both sides can share the same legitimate objective.

**Comparison.** Give pairs a controlled history in which different evidence classes or operations are predictably supplied by different sides. Compare it with independently assembled histories matched for examples, token exposure and current fact coverage. At a checkpoint, separately change the available facts, the mapping from partner/address to responsibility, or both. Restore the original mapping in another branch. New tasks should change content while preserving the learned organization. Include explicit remapping so successful adaptation can be distinguished from failure to notice replacement.

**Measurements and rival outcomes.** Record requests, actual division of work, duplicate work, omissions, retrieval coverage and final joint success. Selective damage from unexpected responsibility changes, followed by recovery when the map is restored or explained, would support history-dependent allocation. Losses only when facts disappear favor a content-availability account. Unchanged success with changed routing indicates adaptation; unchanged success with complete broadcast indicates that no responsibility structure was needed. Generic swap costs unrelated to the trained allocation favor unfamiliarity or channel-quality explanations. An ordinary directory mechanism is a valid bounded explanation, not evidence of interpersonal identity.

**What the three sources supply.** [Wegner's detailed case](knowledge/patterns/cases/wegner_1991.md) establishes an interaction of relationship history and assigned structure, including harm to established pairs; it does not establish a significant benefit for new pairs or isolate allocation. [P3](knowledge/patterns/question-patterns.md) converts that ambiguity into an explicit map/content factorial and direct routing measurement. The [field overview](knowledge/field/overview.md) connects organization to distributed cognition. The [source report](knowledge/dataset/source-report.md) shows that our instances have manipulable histories and snapshots but that current native useful exchange is not yet dependable. Frozen weights imply contextual organization, not lasting learned-weight adaptation.

**Closest work and unresolved boundary.** Transactive memory is an established scientific object, and role-customized latent memory/receiver-conditioned extraction already exist in the [computational account](../gap_audit_20261024/sources.md). ECMR already adaptively organizes multi-agent work through an external trained controller. The potentially different observation is a causally tested, history-acquired responsibility map within native interacting states, with current facts and channel quality held apart. That distinction has not been verified against partner-adaptation, team-memory and multi-agent role-reassignment research. Merely showing that changing a role label hurts is insufficient.

**Prerequisite and first decision-changing check.** Inspect closest computational partner-replacement/reassignment studies for exactly the facts-versus-allocation separation. Then check whether a short, small-model history can elicit measurable complementary routing without training or externally imposing the whole solution. If it cannot, the attractive human analogy supplies no feasible experiment here. This is a new allocation question only if those actions are measured; substituting donor-goal conflict would return to the exhausted goal-preservation argument.

## 3. Does reciprocal connection help by changing what situations the pair encounters, rather than making either instance better at interpreting a fixed situation?

**Question and importance.** For a pair with a usable channel, is the benefit of a responsive partner carried by selecting/creating informative opportunities, by better decisions within comparable opportunities, or both? The scientific payoff is a positive explanation of collective competence that need not rest on accurate individual self/other descriptions. It also gives the original responsiveness question a counterfactual meaning that a single stored donor cannot supply.

**Comparison.** Use an elementary sequential search or inspection task: each side can choose which uncertainty to inspect next, and a new private perturbation changes which observation would help the partner. From one pre-perturbation checkpoint, compare responsive exchange with a fixed pre-perturbation replay and with a nonresponsive supplier allowed the same observation/computation budget. Log which questions become answerable, not only final correctness. A replay of the successful post-perturbation sequence can separately test realized-sequence sufficiency; it cannot test whether the sequence would have been produced without responsiveness.

**Measurements and rival outcomes.** Measure total useful outcome, new relevant observations generated, coverage of task-relevant situations, and descriptive success conditional on opportunity. Responsive exchange that improves opportunity creation but not conditional decision rates is a substantive interaction-level result. Improvements within fixed opportunities suggest better evidence or interpretation. Equivalent passive supply would constrain the need for interaction in that task. More encounters without more useful success, or live interference, are informative failures only after basic task/channel competence is established. Treatment-dependent opportunity conditioning is descriptive decomposition, not by itself a causal mediation estimate.

**What the three sources supply.** [Auvray et al.](knowledge/patterns/cases/auvray_2009.md), pp. 37–39 and 42–43, provides the decisive pattern: more successful partner encounters without demonstrated encounter-normalized partner/lure discrimination. The [field note](knowledge/field/papers/perceptual_crossing_2009.md) prevents equating that human result with LLM social recognition. [P2](knowledge/patterns/question-patterns.md) supplies the post-checkpoint intervention and replay logic as an extension. The [collection account](knowledge/dataset/source-report.md) preserves one feedback-sensitive forward success, failed returns, and a second case where replay succeeds while live fails; it therefore motivates neither a blanket negative nor an already working reciprocal system.

**Closest work and unresolved boundary.** Active information gathering, interactive perception, cooperative search and multi-agent communication already study responsive sampling. BrainNet has correction loops; LACO has closed-loop collaborative driving; ECMR has adaptive deliberation. A bare live-over-replay advantage would be familiar. The contribution would have to explain a concrete organization of opportunities and show why individual-recognition or fixed-input accounts are inadequate in that same system. Targeted comparison to these literatures remains necessary; internal communication alone supplies no priority.

**Prerequisite and first decision-changing check.** Inspect whether the strongest current latent collaboration studies already measure opportunity creation separately from within-opportunity decisions under interventions. Next, establish one complete useful private-perturbation → partner response → recipient action → useful return chain on fresh material. If that fails, stop at channel feasibility; do not recast interference as collective agency. This has the largest immediate engineering uncertainty of the candidate set, without implying a lower scientific value.

## 4. After separation, is a jointly produced insight independently usable by either participant, or recoverable only by reconnecting the histories that produced it?

**Question and importance.** The original persistence question can concern the fate of a new inference rather than the persistence of a source mistake. Can the pair produce a useful relation that neither side's original facts suffice for, and after disconnection where is that relation usable on fresh cases? The unknown is whether interaction leaves locally generalizable content, transient assistance, or distributed contextual support. This is an empirical organization question, not numerical personal continuity.

**Comparison.** Supply complementary premises whose combination yields a new task-relevant relation. After independently verified successful use, separate the pair and reveal a new application. Compare retained natural histories with controls supplying the derived relation explicitly, supplying only the original premises, or restoring only the relevant interaction record. Different post-connection transformations must not be called identical local states; deterministic equality rules out a path effect once all state is truly equal. A retained answer token alone is not a generalizable insight.

**Measurements and rival outcomes.** Measure correct fresh applications, access to premises and derived relations, source reports and restoration effects separately. Local transfer after cutting suggests retained sufficiency; failure after cutting with restoration-specific rescue suggests a remaining contextual dependency, subject to interruption and retrieval controls. Correct repetition without new application is weaker retained content. Persistent source mistakes without useful inference are not an acquired joint capacity. Absence of any jointly useful inference is a prerequisite failure.

**What the three sources supply.** The original vision's reversibility/history question and [memory/continuity field account](knowledge/field/overview.md) motivate the object. [Clark–Chalmers](knowledge/patterns/cases/clark_chalmers_1998.md) and P1 ask which operations remain necessary without settling cognitive constitution. The [corrected evidence](../gap_audit_20261024/evidence.md) shows selective START/NOW recovery but makes explicit that retained text/cache remain and actual inference generalization was unmeasured. That limitation changes the endpoint to fresh use of jointly derived information.

**Closest work, overlap and unresolved boundary.** This family overlaps the earlier retained-versus-partner-dependent capacity argument and inherits its consumed refinement history wherever the deciding comparison is the same. Removing forecasts is not a reset. Tool ablation, distributed memory, collaborative inference and computation-versus-memory work are close; [Computation, Not Memory](../gap_audit_20261024/sources.md) already cautions that reported facts need not mediate actions. A distinct scientific payoff exists only if the fate of a genuinely new jointly derived relation changes the nearest comparison beyond ordinary retention or tool removal. No such distinction is certified here.

**Prerequisite and first decision-changing check.** Read the closest joint-reasoning/latent-memory transfer studies for post-separation novel application. Then specify a relation whose acquisition versus derivation versus application can be independently tested. If the question reduces to the earlier capacity ablation, return it to that existing family rather than inventing a fresh candidate identity.

## A bounded mechanistic opportunity retained without promotion

The original manuscript's additional default-self premise remains empirically separable from generic record competition. A balanced self/partner/third-party conflict design could ask whether functional self-reference has a distinctive protection or vulnerability after matching record position, truth, rehearsal, source cues and readout demand. [Robin conflict](../gap_audit_20261024/evidence.md) already defeats self exclusivity; it does not estimate a self-versus-other difference. Binding IDs, role-index work and the [introspection constraints](knowledge/introspection_sources.md) make this a demanding nearest-source comparison. Such a result could sharpen the existing paper's mechanism, but neither a self advantage nor an arbitrary self-sensitive intervention establishes a privileged self representation. This is retained as a narrower old-evidence route, not presented as the broad new direction the PI requested.
