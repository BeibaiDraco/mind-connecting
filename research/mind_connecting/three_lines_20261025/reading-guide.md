# Reading the three lines together

This guide is for the PI's reading, not a list of papers claimed fully audited. Read the original paper beside each interpretation. The [field account](knowledge/field/overview.md), [old computational source account](../gap_audit_20261024/sources.md), [new supplementary sources](knowledge/supplementary_sources.md), [introspection checks](knowledge/introspection_sources.md), and [late coordination sources](knowledge/late_coordination_sources.md) record the actual depth reached. Recent preprints are reports to assess, not independently reproduced facts.

## 1. Recover the scientific ambition

Start with the project's [original handoff](../../../docs/background/Brain_Bridging_to_LLM_Selfhood_Research_Handoff_2026-09-23.md), §§2–5 and Appendix A. It asks what changes when two already organized systems become internally coupled: access to another's information, the organization of thinking, and continuity after separation. The LLM study can investigate functional counterparts, without inheriting a premise about conscious participants.

Read [Clark and Chalmers, The Extended Mind](https://consc.net/papers/extended.html), §§2–3, for the argument that an explanation of a capacity need not stop at the organism. Ask what an experiment could establish about dependence, and what additional premise would be needed to call an external component constitutive. Do not mistake the latter philosophical conclusion for a measured outcome. [Case note](knowledge/patterns/cases/clark_chalmers_1998.md).

Then read [BrainNet](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-41895-7), methods and reliability results. It supplies an actual neural communication baseline: narrow decoded signals, joint decisions and learning about source reliability. Its meaning is greater than a metaphor, but narrower than sharing another person's thoughts wholesale. [Note](knowledge/field/papers/brainnet_2019.md).

## 2. Learn what human interaction adds beyond transmitted content

[Auvray, Lenay and Stewart, Perceptual Interactions in a Minimalist Virtual Environment](https://cepa.info/fulltexts/478.pdf), especially results §§3.1–3.3, is useful because success at the interaction level and discrimination conditional on encounters are different measurements. The first can change because interaction changes which situations occur. [Case note](knowledge/patterns/cases/auvray_2009.md).

[Wegner, Erber and Raymond, Transactive Memory in Close Relationships](https://dtg.sites.fas.harvard.edu/DANWEGNER/pub/Wegner%2CErber%2C%26Raymond1991.pdf), pp. 923–927, asks about prior organization and imposed responsibility. Established pairs were harmed by assignment; a numerical benefit for new pairs was not significant. The interaction inspires controlled history questions but does not uniquely establish an allocation mechanism. [Case note](knowledge/patterns/cases/wegner_1991.md).

[Tononi and Sporns, Measuring Information Integration](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2202-4-31), Figure 11, belongs here as a theoretical model of joining networks. Increasing external connections simultaneously reduces internal weights under a fixed-total constraint. It is not evidence that turning up an LLM bridge should follow the same curve, and its measure is not our behavioral endpoint. [Note](knowledge/field/papers/integration_2003.md).

## 3. Read the computational papers that remove easy novelty claims

| Read | The question to take into the paper | Why it changes our choices |
|---|---|---|
| [LACO](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.22504v1), §§3–5 | Can agents use internal sharing while retaining their own control task? | The broad useful-sharing/local-control premise is already studied. |
| [Safety of Latent Communication](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.39788v1), methods and repair | Can a benign-trained communication link alter safeguards? | Harm need not require a malicious sender; utility-preserving protection is an existing agenda. |
| [Goals Leak](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17365v1), §§3–7 | How does another client's goal-conditioned state affect decisions? | The interference problem and several unresolved mechanisms are already explicit. |
| [CASK](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09008v1) and [AuthMem](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.01679v1) | Does usable content have the right scope or authority? | Content, source and authorization are already separated experimentally in memory systems. |
| [Computation, Not Memory](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.25760v1), experiments and appendices | Does an accurate report identify what controlled action? | It does not by itself. Our correct goal reports require the same caution. |
| [Testing Interchangeability](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05279v1), §§3–4 | What is lost when an equally experienced partner is replaced? | Partner-specific history and notebook interventions are already tested; ordinary swap costs are insufficient novelty. |
| [Emergent Coordination](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.05174v3), methods and falsification tests | What distinguishes collective complementarity from coincident behavior? | Generic integration/differentiation is occupied; this dossier has not fully audited its estimator. |
| [CAMA](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.19701v1), methods and Appendix D | Does repeated evidence count as several independent sources? | Duplicate-support errors and remedies are already a substantive topic. |
| [Lindsey](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2025/introspection/index.html) with [Reality Check](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.26242v1), §5.3 | Does detecting an internal change identify its origin? | Anomaly detection, source discrimination and privileged self-access must be separated. |

This is a reading order, not a ranking of paper quality. The [full earlier source account](../gap_audit_20261024/sources.md) also covers CacheBack, When Latent Agents Lie, When Does Latent Communication Pay, PLACE, Inherited Goal Drift, binding work and Thought Communication. Read its limitations before using any absence claim. The new source notes add MATM, ECMR and the debate comparison. Abstract-only or inaccessible leads remain visibly unverified.

## 4. Return to our evidence with these distinctions in hand

Read the [whole-program audit](../../../docs/results/experiment_value_audit_2026-10-22.md), then the [current experimental map](knowledge/dataset/card.md) and [source report](knowledge/dataset/source-report.md). Keep three questions beside every result: what was actually changed, what was actually measured, and which alternative would produce the same observation? Separate fixed foreign-state influence, reports after disconnection, committed actions and discarded diagnostic branches. The completed data guide the next question; they do not oblige the next question to reuse the old task.
