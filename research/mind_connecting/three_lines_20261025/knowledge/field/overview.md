# What connecting systems could teach us

October 25, 2026. Field synthesis for the reopened three-line inquiry. This account expands the scientific space; it selects no preferred new question. Four new compact primary notes accompany the account. Earlier source judgments are reused at their recorded reading depth, not silently promoted to fresh verification.

## Recover the original ambition

The [original handoff](../../../../../docs/background/Brain_Bridging_to_LLM_Selfhood_Research_Handoff_2026-09-23.md), §§2–5 and Appendix A, starts from an unusually direct question: could changing the interaction between two already conscious brains change how one has access to the other, and possibly change the boundaries of thought and experience? Merely transferring a fact is an insufficient answer. Equally, the question is not exhausted by protecting an agent's assigned goal from interference.

Its philosophical importance is that the apparent boundary around a mind becomes an experimental variable rather than an unquestioned starting point. Its psychological importance is that access, authorship, control, continuity and confidence about one's capacities need not vary together. Its neuroscientific importance is that connection function, location, direction, delay and history might matter differently; counting wires or measuring synchrony does not characterize all those roles. These are reasons to investigate, not empirical conclusions that minds combine.

The LLM platform changes the epistemic target. It permits copying states, maintaining known private histories, cutting directional pathways and restoring identical starting points. Those operations can expose functional organization with unusual precision. They cannot reproduce the human premise that two conscious participants were present, or answer whether experience exists. The worthwhile intermediate aim is to discover how initially separate cognitive processes organize shared information, action and history when their internal computations become coupled.

## Six perspectives that should stay distinct

**Direct neural communication.** Real interfaces already demonstrate useful brain-mediated collaboration and differential reliance on sources; [BrainNet](papers/brainnet_2019.md) is the concrete reading entry. Its limited, decoded signal warns against treating “direct” as equivalent to unmediated thought sharing. Our extension asks when richer internal exchange changes the organization of use and attribution, beyond adding another input.

**Joint agency and social interaction.** Two processes may jointly achieve something without either accurately representing its contribution. The [perceptual-crossing study](papers/perceptual_crossing_2009.md) supplies an especially consequential demonstration of the distinction between interaction-level success and individual discrimination. The [previous human-agency account](../../../conceptual_inputs_20261024.md) adds Bolt & Loehr and Fribourg: predictability, actual control and reported shared agency can separate. Keep its correction: Bolt's computer tones were participant-contingent, although the purported human partner did not control them. None of this shows that an LLM has a human sense of agency.

**Extended and distributed cognition.** The [extended-mind argument](papers/extended_mind_1998.md) asks which resources belong in an explanation of a capacity. A complementary primary lead is Hutchins's [How a Cockpit Remembers Its Speeds](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1903_1): the analysis follows representations across pilots and instruments instead of assuming the individual is the sole cognitive unit. This pass checked its abstract and original publication record, not its full case analysis. These approaches motivate asking where knowledge or competence is maintained, and how its location changes across interaction. They do not establish that a larger functional unit is one subject.

The corrected [Wegner transactive-memory case](../patterns/cases/wegner_1991.md) adds history-dependent allocation as a distinct organizational question. Its history-by-assignment interaction and harm to established pairs should not be described as a significant assignment benefit for new pairs. The pattern author’s recorded primary-reading scope applies; this field pass did not independently re-audit that original. Partner replacement and disrupted responsibility maps may matter even when available facts are held fixed, a proposed computational extension rather than an established result here.

**Memory and continuity.** The original vision also asks what remains after connection ends. Historical provenance, later endorsement, retained information, continuation of an unfinished project and numerical personal identity are different relations. Parfit's *Personal Identity* and the handoff's Roelofs/Schechter discussion provide philosophical entry points, at the limited reading depths documented in handoff §17.2. Parfit's linked mirror failed in this refresh; no new close-reading claim is made. Conway & Pleydell-Pearce's [self-memory-system paper](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.107.2.261) is an additional lead for relating autobiographical reconstruction to goals; only bibliographic/abstract-level material was located, so it does not decide an experimental claim here. Our computational extension is concrete: after common experience, which new knowledge and action dispositions remain in each separated history, and does functional continuation track recorded origin, current state or subsequent interaction? That question needs newly collected behavior; the old removal experiment answers only a restricted report/access contrast.

**Integration with differentiation.** [Tononi & Sporns](papers/integration_2003.md) motivates separating information transmission from how the whole network is organized. The transferable scientific issue is whether useful coupling preserves complementary local computations, redistributes them, or degrades both. It supplies no ready-made LLM metric and no licensed claim about consciousness. Functional coordination, identical answers and theory-specific integration must not be conflated.

**Current computational analogues.** The [corrected precedent account](../../../gap_audit_20261024/sources.md) remains binding: LACO already combines useful internal sharing and retained ego control; Goals Leak studies legitimate goal interference and leaves several mechanisms unresolved; receiver-conditioned communication, scope/authority systems, role-sensitive attribution and report/action dissociations have direct precedents. Internal communication, selective protection, source effects or generic causal probing are therefore not untouched fields. That narrows certain claims while leaving the broader relations among organization, attribution, continuity and contingency open to specific evidence.

## Opportunities and informative alternatives

These are scientifically distinct opportunities to hand to the questioner, not a ranking or adopted protocol. Some overlap earlier candidates and must retain their review history.

| Open question | Why resolving it matters | Observation that could distinguish answers; informative negative | Present footing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Can two instances acquire useful shared knowledge while maintaining a calibrated account of who knows what? | Separates common information from common attribution; speaks to the epistemic relation between connected systems. | Cross origin, actual access and partner availability; compare useful decisions with source/access judgments. Accurate separation despite strong transfer is informative. | Old access/attribution dissociation motivates this; source/role literature is close precedent. A new contribution needs a sharper prediction than “confusion occurs.” |
| Can joint competence improve while each instance's account of its contribution becomes less accurate? | Changes the scientific object from individual mistakes to the relationship between collective achievement and individual understanding. | Independently perturb each contribution and measure joint outcome plus prospective/retrospective contribution judgments. Coordination without accurate attribution, and attribution without coordination, are distinct outcomes. | No reliable complete native reciprocal chain yet. Human joint-agency and perceptual-crossing precedents mean the generic dissociation is not novel. |
| Does interaction reorganize where a capacity resides? | Distinguishes information acquired by an instance from an ability sustained across systems. | Restore common states, cut access, substitute partners, and test transfer to fresh problems. Complete retention or entirely transient support are both meaningful. | Selective recovery supplies a narrow clue; tool-ablation and forecasting precedents constrain novelty. Earlier forecasting candidate is not reset. |
| What functional continuity survives connection and separation? | Gives the original persistence question a behavioral form without identifying persons with memories. | Track independent histories and unfinished projects through connection and separation; distinguish retained content, source accuracy and actual continuation. Fully reversible reports with lasting useful learning would differ from lasting source error without learning. | Old START/NOW asymmetry is relevant but does not demonstrate durable autobiographical restructuring. Persona/goal continuity studies need a dedicated deciding-source comparison before novelty claims. |
| When does another responsive computation matter beyond the content it sends? | Addresses the PI's original concern about interaction with another process rather than mere exposure. | Introduce a new private perturbation after shared history and compare responsive return paths with fixed/yoked inputs. If response contingencies add nothing after the specified information controls, that constrains the need for a live partner. | One forward native success and failed returns remain exposed exploratory evidence. Identical deterministic inputs must produce identical outputs. |
| Which connections support complementary computation rather than homogenization or impairment? | Connects the function/location of the bridge to organization, without assuming more coupling is better. | Hold task opportunity fixed while comparing independently characterized payload/site/timing changes; measure specialization and joint usefulness separately. Greater access with worse composition is informative. | LACO is direct precedent for depth-dependent utility/control. A distinctive explanatory contrast is required; arbitrary layer scans are not a scientific contribution. |

## How the completed project changes this map

The [corrected evidence account](../../../gap_audit_20261024/evidence.md) supports route-dependent attribution, question-dependent recovery and generic record interference. The results motivate measuring access, attribution and recovery separately rather than summarizing them with one binary label; they do not establish the latent dimensionality of a self boundary or rule out a shared underlying factor with different readout sensitivities. They also prevent a self-exclusive story: explicitly named Robin records are vulnerable, and that was predicted by the manuscript's generic-slot account. Exact prior source notes and numerical qualifications should be reused rather than restated more strongly.

The V6 results preserve a particularly useful negative: influence does not imply usable interaction. Early diagnostics included committed actions; only the later fixed-scene work/readout batch used entirely discarded probes. Correct goal names do not prove those goals control action, and partner-consistent errors do not identify goal transfer. A failed useful reciprocal chain limits what can currently be inferred, but does not close questions whose decisive observations have not yet been collected.

The field's broad lesson is methodological: treat access, contribution, attribution, control, persistence and responsiveness as potentially separable dimensions. The intellectual opportunity lies in discovering a consequential relation among them and specifying rival outcomes, not in giving the old error a more philosophical name.

## Reading route and limits

Start with the original handoff §§2–5 to recover the motivating questions, then BrainNet for the empirical human baseline, perceptual crossing for the interaction/recognition distinction, and Clark–Chalmers for the capacity-boundary argument. Read Tononi–Sporns Figure 11 with its assumptions visible. Then return to the corrected LLM source account to see which apparent opportunities already have close computational precedents. For philosophy, read the original opposing positions linked in handoff §17.2 rather than treating the bibliography as consensus.

This is bounded reconnaissance, not an exhaustive priority review. New close reading emphasizes the four linked notes. Later perceptual-crossing variants, contemporary social neuroscience, autobiographical-memory experiments, personal-identity arguments and partner-replacement/adaptation studies remain consequential follow-up leads. No proposed opportunity should claim those literatures are silent. A separate field reviewer should specifically check whether any candidate relies on a source's unexamined negative space.
