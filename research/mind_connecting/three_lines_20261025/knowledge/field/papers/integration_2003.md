# Integration theory: connection placement and strength are distinct from transmission

Tononi & Sporns (2003), *Measuring information integration*, BMC Neuroscience 4:31. [Primary article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1471-2202-4-31). Read Background, Theory/effective information, “Scaling and joining complexes,” Figure 11 and relevant discussion. This is an early idealized-network analysis, not current IIT or a human experiment.

**Problem and construction.** Efficient transmission between separate channels need not produce integrated processing. The authors define effective information using perturbations and identify complexes through a minimum-partition measure. Their model assumes stationary Gaussian processes. Figure 11 joins two optimized eight-element components through reciprocal links. It reallocates a fixed total connection strength per element: increasing between-module weights decreases within-module weights.

**Finding and scope.** The maximizing complex and integration measure vary with this reallocation; beyond the favorable range, integration falls. This does not directly predict increasing a bridge's strength while leaving local computation unchanged. The model does not show a biological merger, and its measure is not validated for transformer selfhood.

**Use here — our extension.** The transferable question is which division of computation survives a specified connection, rather than whether more edges produce more unity. Functional specialization, integration and impairment should remain separate outcomes. One could compare interfaces that preserve differentiated local computations with interfaces that homogenize or disrupt them, but only after defining an independently useful joint computation. We should not call ordinary answer agreement “integration,” estimate subject numbers from coupling, or label abrupt behavioral changes phase transitions. The existing same-answer/swapping results do not implement this theory. A negative finding that stronger access damages joint competence would be scientifically interpretable without a consciousness story.
