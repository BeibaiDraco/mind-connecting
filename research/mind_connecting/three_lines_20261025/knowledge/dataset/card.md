## Identity, evidence and units

The corpus is the project's generated V1–V6 studies, sometimes called mind-connecting or mind-bridging. These are multiple collections under different contracts, not one exchangeable table. The principal platform is two independently initialized runtime states of frozen Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507; completed V4/V5 Llama evidence is historical replication evidence, not permission to switch the next study's checkpoint.

**Documented and rechecked this session:** original protocol §§1–3; original vision §§2–5 and Appendix; the whole-program audit; corrected evidence account; original V3 B′ signal table; original V6 channel, interaction and fixed-scene reports. **Previously observed, reused here:** the October 20 bounded fixed-scene schema/coverage inspection and saved check output. **Not inspected anew:** every raw trial, current external SD availability or a full corpus-wide missingness inventory. All numerical outcomes below were exposed before this batch. They generate hypotheses and cannot independently confirm those hypotheses.

The independent unit is a generated paired episode/layout family. Sides, rounds, order rotations, conditions and discarded readout branches are repeated observations. Trial joins require the run/configuration identity plus episode, side, condition, question and rotation/round, with snapshot lineage when causal same-state comparisons are intended. Shared episode names alone do not justify pooling studies. Missing/invalid answers retain their status; no zero imputation. Natural action trajectories cease being matched worlds after different committed actions.

## What is jointly available, and what is not

Original assignments and donor counterfactuals provide factual provenance ground truth. ACC concerns answering about donor content; START concerns original assignment; NOW is a report of a current rule; WORD concerns a code-word assignment; M is the study's adjusted logit-based attribution measure. These are not interchangeable with native action, causal control, confidence or experience. CAP is a limited general-knowledge/format safeguard, not complete preservation of cognition.

Sibling question branches restore a common state and then diverge. A correct fact/rule response in one branch does not establish that the actual action branch used that information. A connected readout itself receives foreign state and can create the measured change. Link removal preserves generated text and recipient cache unless explicitly removed. Hence “after disconnection” is neither a return to baseline history nor an isolated memory test.

The native V6 window exports up to the newest 16 generated token positions across attention layers. Token K/V incorporates broader context; the window is not a purified current-fact or goal channel. Nominal gain is not matched effective influence across sites, materials or topologies. These implementation properties were documented in the existing card/reviews, not newly experimentally validated here.

The preserved [coverage script](../../../card_checks/discovery_checks_20261020.py) and [output](../../../card_checks/discovery_checks_20261020.json) establish the fixed-scene counts, key uniqueness, invariant preparation groups and absence of committed history. Their synthetic policy table also proves that accuracy can collapse distinct response policies and that donor-goal-dependent factual distortion can mimic donor-goal adoption. This logical nonidentification is not model evidence.

## Distinctions the platform could resolve

These are opportunities for new observations, without a preferred ranking or claim of novelty.

| Scientific distinction | Existing leverage | Missing collection or control |
|---|---|---|
| Shared access versus historical source assignment | Qwen access-matched route contrast and provenance controls | Several content fields and tasks with matched relevant information, explicit full-category errors and fresh confirmation; scalar access is insufficient to match all information. |
| Functional self-specificity versus generic record conflict | V5 Robin counterevidence; named/third-person controls | Balanced self, partner and third-party records with matched truth, conflict, cue and readout demand; neutral binding baselines. Existing asymmetrical Robin manipulation cannot estimate self privilege. |
| Content sharing versus jointly organized control | Fixed-memory pair swaps, live excess agreement and real-action runtime | A native task requiring complementary evidence and intervention-sensitive coordinated action; useful output beyond independently copying the donor. Same answers and reciprocal wiring are insufficient. |
| Responsive partner versus structured nonresponsive signal | ONE/TWO, cuts, exact replay and intervention forks | A validated complete feedback chain under novel private interventions, with content-matched/yoked comparisons and both successful and failed cases. Identical deterministic input cannot reveal a metaphysical difference in source. |
| Connection placement versus topology | V1 layer/support contrasts; current all-layer KV route | Independent functional characterization of input/site/timing, then finite crossed topology comparisons. No already localized “self layer”; an observation window alone does not localize function. |
| Asymmetric effects versus common functional organization | Separate A/B traces and one-way/two-way interventions | Balanced reciprocal materials and directed interventions; distinguish true asymmetry from unequal initial histories or marginal adoption. No inference of absorption/fusion follows. |
| Local endorsement versus historical continuity | START/NOW and selective post-cut recovery | Interrupted projects or commitments with enacted continuation, independent fact access and provenance, plus history/caching controls. Current NOW reports alone do not establish continuity of control. |
| Immediate influence versus durable change/path dependence | Work/readout factorial and disconnection with retained state | Matched current state/signal after different exposure histories; independent manipulation of generated text, cache and retained decisions; delayed challenges. Frozen weights permit contextual history effects, not learned-weight adaptation. |
| Actual dependence versus self-predicted ability | Snapshot/cut technology; open reports show poor anomaly reporting | Prospective condition-specific forecasts followed by paired challenges needing new partner evidence; ability, generic confidence and differential dependence separated. No such joint endpoint exists. |
| Public self-description versus action-causal organization | Correct own-goal labels, sometimes wrong rule/action; generic report/action precedent | Distinct action-causal interventions and calibrated report tasks. No consciousness, awareness-ground-truth or subject-count endpoint exists. |

The original brain-bridge idea supplies the questions about access, perspective, ownership, continuity, topology and interaction. The present platform can test functional organization among these quantities. It cannot determine whether experience tokens or phenomenal subjects become shared, vanish or combine.
