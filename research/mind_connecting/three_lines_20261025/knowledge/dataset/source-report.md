## Original purpose and what survived operationalization

The [original handoff §§2–5 and Appendix](../../../../../docs/background/Brain_Bridging_to_LLM_Selfhood_Research_Handoff_2026-09-23.md) begins with direct brain-to-brain interaction: whether higher thought, intention and attribution might become accessible in new ways; whether changes could be asymmetric, partial, reversible or history dependent; and whether connection topology and placement matter. It explicitly refuses to collapse the problem into successful fact transfer or a count of output ports. The appendix retains the PI's concern that ordinary replay may miss the epistemic interest of interaction with another experiencer.

Its LLM translation is deliberately narrower: controlled functional source/intent attribution under copied runtime states, manipulated signals and reversible connections. The [original protocol §§1–3](../../../../../docs/protocol/EXPERIMENT_DESIGN.md) distinguishes signal content from responsiveness. For a deterministic receiver, identical state and input produce identical output; responsiveness must be tested by changed counterfactual trajectories after an intervention. No special source essence is observable from an identical recording.

The shipping task gave the two instances independently known rules and secret words; a third-person Robin record supplied a nonparticipating referent. Complementary readouts targeted access, historical assignment, current stated rule, downstream choice, general capability and open/closed descriptions. The downstream BEH baseline failed and was retired. This materially narrowed what subsequent “adoption” could mean: most early results concern reports and attribution, not enacted goals.

## Which explanatory claims are and are not earned

The best-supported bounded findings concern route-dependent access/attribution differences, different recovery of historical and current reports, source-framing surprises and generic record-provenance interference. Third-person wording and rehearsal controls exclude particular simple accounts; they do not identify the remaining mechanism. V5 Robin conflict constrains self-exclusive interpretations, but the preserved manuscript's generic slot-competition account already predicted Robin interference. It does not refute that account wholesale.

The existing manuscript's slot explanation still needs causal discrimination from retrieval competition, contextual salience, output mapping and other representational accounts. A future balanced self/other effect would need more than reproducing a named-source advantage. Correct rule/fact answers in a sibling branch cannot identify what controlled the actual action. Similarly, retained influence after cutting may reside in generated text or cache; it is not by itself lasting ownership change.

The broad vision is not exhausted by these local interference mechanisms. The platform also permits new research on counterfactual partner responsiveness, directional asymmetry, complementary functional organization, history dependence and prospective recognition of dependence. Those require new measurements rather than relabeling old source reports. No priority claim or preferred shortlist is inferred from feasibility.

## Collection status and verification scope

V1–V5 include complete formal studies alongside failed gates, pilots and prospectively frozen continuations. V6 formal N=240 per checkpoint remains unstarted/on hold; small native diagnostics and engineering cases must not be counted as that collection. No experiment or new selector is authorized by this document.

Original channel-report semantics were checked directly: committed actions advanced the world and separate probes were discarded. The later fixed-scene report and earlier coverage output confirm no committed action there. B′ was checked against its original full table so the failed primary gate, secondary signal and near-zero PC family are not reduced to one verdict. Other quantities use the reviewed whole-program audit and corrected evidence trail. The full repository-wide raw coverage/missingness and every native array were not reread during this bounded author pass.
