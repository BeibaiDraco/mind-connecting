## Reading and search limits

Reuse searches previously used the project aliases, exact title phrases, latent/KV communication, source attribution, self-other/agency and causal coupling, followed by goal leakage, ego-centric control, scope, binding and receiver-dependent evidence. Exact underlying dataset reuse was not established. The current pass checked repository originals to repair collection semantics and preserve failed studies; it did not claim an exhaustive public citation/reuse graph.

Consequential access limits from the existing audit remain: *Opacity Is Not Privacy* was abstract/landing-page only; several older leads were not read in full or compared across final versions; a model-adaptation lead remained unresolved. These cannot silently become verified novelty exclusions. The earlier report's “no exact combination found” language is not priority evidence, particularly after the October 24 direct-precedent correction.

For any newly developed question, the coordinator should search conceptual equivalents of its deciding contrast, rather than only its preferred title or exact task. The dataset alone cannot certify novelty. Conversely, adjacent successful sharing, generic interference or reporting work does not by itself close questions about counterfactual responsive organization, different forms of continuity or calibrated dependence recognition. Each needs a clearly separating observation and a comparison to its closest primary source.
