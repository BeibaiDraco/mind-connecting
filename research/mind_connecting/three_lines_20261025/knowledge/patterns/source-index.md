Selection is intentionally small and heterogeneous. These papers teach how to form questions; none determines which LLM candidate is novel. Any consequential novelty judgment requires its own conceptual-equivalent precedent search. Human familiarity, subjective experience and biological integration cannot be silently transferred.

The older project note on Fisher et al. and *Looking Inward* remains a historical project-specific input; it is neither overwritten nor promoted to independently reviewed evidence in this new revision. Its forecasting transfer needs useful access contrasts and interpretable forecasts. This bank's cases provide additional routes rather than declaring that previous candidate superior.
