# Question patterns for connected cognitive systems

Revision **three-lines-patterns-v1**, October 25, 2026. Author draft; initial independent review completed and one correction applied, recheck pending. This portable bank broadens question formation beyond the earlier project-specific access/control and forecasting candidates. It supplies neither evidence of LLM subjectivity nor a novelty verdict.

Start with [question-patterns](question-patterns.md), then the three cases: [functional extension](cases/clark_chalmers_1998.md), [interaction and encounter structure](cases/auvray_2009.md), and [organized distributed memory](cases/wegner_1991.md). [Source index](source-index.md) records primary locators, access and reading depth. The project-specific mapping is outside the bank in [pattern-selection](../pattern-selection.md).

The three patterns are **single-case inspirations**, not a cross-study validated law. They cover functional organization, interaction and distributed memory; they are not a representative neuroscience review. The bank deliberately separates the worthwhile question from one possible answer. Sources are public originals or author-hosted versions; PDFs are not bundled. Any receiver of this directory can follow the stable links. No source text is reproduced verbatim.
