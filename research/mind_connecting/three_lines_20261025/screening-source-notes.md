# Deciding-source checks for the independent screening

October 25, 2026. Bounded independent screening; original papers reopened where they could erase a candidate's proposed difference. Preparation reviews are supporting evidence, not substitutes for these checks. No model run, raw reanalysis, protocol adoption or source-code edit occurred.

## Partner-history comparison

[Gao et al., Testing Interchangeability in LLM Agent Teams, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05279v1), §§3.1–3.3, §4 setup, Tables 1–4 and §4.2 discussion reopened. Identical frozen models acquire task notes and partner notes; role-matched swaps retain experience. Placebo roster changes, partner-note deletion with task notes retained, intact-team amnesia and naive replacements separate several explanations. Held-out conditions fork the same formation state. Swap penalties are larger for communication cost than task score. Process signatures and extra-message categories already measure coordination organization. Four swap pairs are the independent units. Deletion changes context length; notebook headings do not guarantee semantic separation. The task-coupling gradient also changes complexity.

**Remaining candidate difference:** independently manipulate required hand-offs while preserving component-task difficulty and test whether specifically acquired responsibility expectations explain routing errors. Native KV storage alone changes implementation. The candidate currently supplies no established reason this refinement changes a consequential scientific conclusion enough to make it the next flagship.

## Higher-order visibility comparison

[Nguyen et al., Dynamic Theory of Mind as a Temporal Memory Problem, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.14646v1), Method: Conversation Generation and Belief State Tracking, reopened. LLM dialogues contain private inner-speech annotations, scheduled belief changes and linguistic update cues. The tracker retains prior/current records and explicitly extracts second-order belief annotations to generate questions. Thus first-/second-order tracking and controlled informational asymmetry are existing objects. These methods do not by themselves validate annotations as the states causing an enacted decision.

**Remaining candidate difference:** experimentally delivered private information, independently manipulated visibility of its receipt, and decisions that actually require predicting a partner's informed action. The proposed question concerns whether increased factual access leads an agent to overestimate the partner's access, and whether an actual acknowledgement corrects the consequential error. It is not a claim that recursive belief questions are new.

[Gu and Li, CASK, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09008v1), §3 and method framing reopened. Its owner variable specifies the discourse context licensing a proposition; a belief or plan can remain useful within scope without becoming an unconditional shared-world fact. This is a direct warning against claiming content/scope separation as new. [Li et al., Where Is the Self?, v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.16717v3), methodology and scope checked: role-conditioned attribution and source-dependent reasoning are close precedents, while an Assistant slot is not proof of actual historical generation. Earlier detailed verified comparisons remain in [the source audit](../gap_audit_20261024/sources.md).

[Steele et al., author account of Belief–Reality Separation](https://papers.osteele.com/mental-spaces-belief-26/) was reopened. It reports asserted-versus-observed belief contrasts, visibility manipulations and distinct value/routing interventions. Original [arXiv HTML v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.11945v1) again returned 404. The author account makes a naive “first visibility/routing test” claim untenable; the inaccessible full method cannot certify duplication or the exact remaining gap. Obtain original PDF/code before accepting a mechanistic plan.

## Echo and generic collective-organization comparison

[Lin et al., CAMA, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.19701v1), Appendices A–D reopened. Retrieved memories can share upstream observations or propagated summaries. Query-dependent factor assignments model redundant support, with provenance as a structural prior. Expand and Trace actions respectively seek new evidence and recover dependencies. This directly occupies generic counting of correlated cross-agent memories as independent corroboration. The current early echo idea has not identified a remaining difference beyond the internal carrier.
